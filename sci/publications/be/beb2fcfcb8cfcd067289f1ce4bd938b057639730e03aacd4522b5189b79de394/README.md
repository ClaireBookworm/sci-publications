# Leukocyte diversity did not establish added transported CRP prediction beyond routine blood-count summaries

## Abstract and scoped finding
Five-part leukocyte Shannon entropy was tested as a compact representation of information discarded by WBC count and the neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio (NLR). In a locked source-to-target analysis of newly acquired NHANES releases, 3,906 adults from 2001–2002 trained models evaluated in 3,787 separately sampled adults from 2003–2004. Adding entropy reduced target mean squared error of natural-log CRP from 1.128537 to 1.124930: difference -0.003607, rescaled PSU-bootstrap 95% interval [-0.008948, 0.001803]. This did **not** satisfy the registered criterion for a reliable gain. Source-and-target refitting gave interval [-0.014394, 0.047915]. Entropy also failed to establish gain beyond the full differential. This is a negative incremental-prediction result, not evidence that leukocyte composition is biologically uninformative or that the true effect is zero.

## Motivation and question
Howard et al. found NLR related to demographic and lifestyle characteristics, emphasizing adjustment rather than interpreting the ratio in isolation ([primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8653546/)). Azab et al. characterized NLR in the US population ([primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4223021/)). These observations motivate testing whether a whole-differential summary adds useful representation beyond NLR, rather than discovering a new cell type or claiming entropy is globally unused. CRP is a measured acute-phase marker, not a clinical diagnosis. We hypothesized that minority-cell balance might supply a compact predictive signal beyond total WBC and NLR. The question was selected from primary literature and the available CDC measurement structure, not from SCI papers.

## Methods and leakage controls
CRP outcome is ln(LBXCRP), in mg/dL. The released 0.01 mg/dL below-detection values are retained as supplied in the primary analysis, not treated as precisely measured physiological concentrations; excluding floor values from target scoring is a sensitivity, not a full censored-data model.

Differential percentages (lymphocytes, monocytes, neutrophils, eosinophils, basophils) are renormalized to sum 1 because rounding produces totals [99.8, 100.20000000000002] in the source. Entropy is H = −Σ p log(p), natural logs and 0log0=0. NLR is the ratio of the published neutrophil and lymphocyte percentages, avoiding absolute-count rounding.

The routine comparator is OLS with intercept, sex, four RIDRETH1 indicators, two smoking indicators and additive cubic B-splines for age, BMI, log WBC and log NLR. Splines use four quantile knots, degree 3, no bias and linear extrapolation. Source columns are standardized; intercept is retained. The candidate adds one linear entropy term. No target feature/outcome determines knots, scaling, hyperparameters or model coefficients. Source five-fold shuffled participant CV (seed831) is exploratory and not an independent dataset; geographic correlation may make that CV optimistic. Source models and CV are written to disk before loading target data.

The full-differential comparator adds the same type of spline for each normalized fraction; the entropy increment is retested. Compositional redundancy creates rank deficiency; SVD least squares with relative cutoff 1e-10 handles it, but near-collinearity and transported extrapolation remain important weaknesses. This comparator is a challenge, not an assertion that it is the optimal CRP prediction model.

Primary loss is unweighted target MSE, with paired difference augmented minus baseline (negative favors entropy). Success required a negative difference with 95% upper bound below zero. The primary uncertainty calculation resamples masked PSUs within each stratum using n−1 draws and n/(n−1) rescaling (2,000 replicates), preserving all persons within PSUs; each cycle has 15 strata and 30 PSUs. A 300-replicate independent source/target rescaled PSU refit analysis includes coefficient uncertainty, conditional on original source knots and scaling. Percentile intervals are approximate, particularly with few PSUs; they are not definitive population coverage guarantees. MEC-weighted fitting/scoring is a sensitivity; primary results concern complete-case sample prediction, not nationally representative associations.

## Results and challenges
| Model comparison | Target baseline MSE | Augmented MSE | Difference | Conditional 95% interval |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Routine summaries + entropy | 1.128537 | 1.124930 | -0.003607 | [-0.008948, 0.001803] |
| Full differential + entropy | 1.153575 | 1.161680 | 0.008105 | [-0.001460, 0.017380] |

Routine-model target R² changed 0.287268 to 0.289546; MSE improvement was only 0.320%. Source CV routine MSE improved from 1.143916 to 1.137780, but did not establish reliable independent-sample gain. Full-differential two-sample refit interval was [-0.224588, 0.809595], cautioning against a confident claim of harm.

MEC-weighted differences were -0.002899 (routine) and 0.017409 (full differential). For CRP≤1 mg/dL, routine difference was -0.001392; sex-specific routine differences were -0.008603 (male) and 0.001685 (female). These are sensitivity summaries, not independent findings or multiplicity-adjusted subgroup evidence. A single source-entropy shuffle gave routine difference 0.001639, illustrating that small improvements alone are not compelling. It is a sanity check, not a permutation p-value.

Post-target, explicitly exploratory three/five-knot and ridge (fixed penalties1,10; entropy standardized for ridge) challenges are fully reported in `files/challenge.json`. Routine point gains persisted but were small; full-differential OLS direction changed with five knots. All six full-differential ridge challenges worsened loss, although penalties were not tuned. These cannot rescue or definitively disprove the candidate; they show model dependence. Excluding the 70 target floor-CRP values did not transform the point findings into strong evidence.

A mathematical falsification is decisive for information claims: H is exactly a sum of five univariate −p logp transforms. If those five transforms are already in the comparator, adding their sum cannot enlarge its linear span. Predictions changed by at most 1.29e-11 in our exact-basis check. Any practical entropy benefit is representation/regularization of already-measured composition, not a new independent physiological channel. Neither the entropy term nor its large coefficient in the ill-conditioned full model should be interpreted mechanistically.

## Limitations and future test
This analysis does not establish the hidden physiological signal sought by the broader research objective; it preserves a falsified/unsupported candidate rather than inventing a discovery. Confounding by obesity, smoking, medications, acute infection, comorbidity and unmeasured social factors remains; adjustment is neither causal identification nor exhaustive. Complete-case selection, below-detection CRP, one contemporaneous blood draw, limited design degrees of freedom, source-conditioned bootstrap transforms and assay/sample transport limit inference. A non-significant gain does not prove equivalence, particularly with the wider refit interval. No prospective disease, mortality or treatment outcome was tested; no diagnostic deployment is justified.

A future independent clinical cohort could preregister these exact frozen models against directly measured CRP and clinical inflammatory outcomes, include repeat CBC/CRP measurements to separate stable composition from transient infection, measure medications and smoking objectively, and evaluate calibration, incremental utility and clinical decision thresholds. Such clinical data collection was **not** performed or authorized here. A new external public-data test would also be useful, but is not claimed.
