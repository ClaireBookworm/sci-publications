# Exercise-to-rest pulse-pressure amplification adds no demonstrated cross-hospital CAD prediction beyond its component pressures

## Abstract
A literature-led public-data analysis tested log peak-to-rest pulse-pressure ratio beyond flexible additive conventional exercise measures and individual pressures. Hungarian development included293 patients;independent institutional Long Beach VA evaluation included136,106 with angiographic CAD. External Brier loss changed from0.253621 to0.253631;gain-0.000010,1000 joint source-refit/target-bootstrap95%[-0.000670,0.000536]. Source cross-validation also worsened slightly. The log ratio is an additive transformation of its components,not new measured information. No reliable incremental benefit is established;this is not evidence of equivalence or clinical utility. Historical target reuse,referral differences,missingness,and incompatible protocols limit confirmation. AI-authored,author self-checked and clean-rerun;not independent peer review.

## Question, rationale and scope
Exercise systolic pressure and heart-rate responses are commonly assessed,while diastolic measurements may be difficult during exercise. Akhras,Upward and Jackson reported a relationship between an abnormal exercise diastolic response and coronary disease in a selected angiography sample [1]. A separate primary report examined diastolic variation during bicycle exercise [2]. These motivate using both systolic and diastolic components rather than claiming that systolic pressure alone describes exercise hemodynamics. They do **not** establish this candidate's clinical validity.

We asked whether explicitly representing exercise-to-rest pulse-pressure amplification improves prediction over additive conventional measures and the component pressures. This is a representation test,not discovery of an unknown physiological mechanism. For resting systolic/diastolic pressures S0,D0 and peak pressures S1,D1,we defined P0=S0-D0,P1=S1-D1 and q=log(P1/P0). Crucially,q=log(P1)-log(P0):it is not an irreducible interaction and cannot add information in an information-theoretic sense when both components are already available. It can only change a finite model's transformation and penalization. Nor is exercise-to-rest amplification equivalent to central-to-peripheral pulse-pressure amplification,arterial stiffness or directly measured vascular resistance.

The hypothesis was positive external mean Brier-loss reduction,baseline minus augmented. Our result does not establish that hypothesis. We do not infer equivalence,absence of underlying physiological information,or absence of benefit from every possible representation.

## Methods and leakage controls
The baseline used continuous age,resting and peak systolic pressure,resting and peak diastolic pressure,resting and peak pulse pressure,cholesterol,maximum HR,resting HR and exercise ST depression. Categorical predictors were sex,chest-pain type,fasting glucose indicator,resting ECG,exercise angina,ST slope,exercise protocol and beta-blocker indicator. Including the actual pressure components **and** pulse pressures is deliberately stronger than an SBP-only comparator. We excluded fluoroscopic vessel count and thallium imaging to avoid downstream diagnostic-test information.

Raw records have76 fields with terminal dummy name;zero-based indices are explicitly in `src/analyze.py`. Sentinel-9 is missing;nonpositive cholesterol,HR and pressure values are missing. Implausible category values for sex,chest pain,glucose,ECG,angina,slope and beta blockade are marked missing. Source-only median imputation retains empty columns and adds missing indicators. Each continuous predictor receives cubic splines with four uniformly spaced source knots,without bias basis,linear extrapolation. Categorical missingness is coded-99 and one-hot encoded;unseen target categories are ignored. All design columns are source-standardized. Ridge logistic regression uses C=1,lbfgs,max_iter2000,tolerance1e-9. Augmentation adds one source-standardized q column. Imputation,knots,encoding and scaling are fit only on source-training rows. Target labels are used for scoring,never fitting,feature construction,tuning or recalibration.

Source5-fold stratified cross-validation uses shuffled folds with seed42 and training-fold preprocessing. External predictions are frozen from all eligible source rows. Primary uncertainty uses1000 paired repetitions:resample source patients with replacement,refit both complete pipelines,resample target patients with replacement and compare losses on the same resampled rows. A paired fixed-model target bootstrap is also reported. Seed491 fixes the bootstrap. Percentile95% intervals are descriptive sampling intervals,not population-wide guarantees. They do not capture site-selection,measurement,unobserved clustering or adaptive research-history uncertainty. AUC and log loss are secondary;no clinical decision threshold was selected.

## Falsification and sensitivity
All predeclared outputs,not just favorable tests,are in `outputs/results.json` and `challenge.json`. External gains were-0.000027 at C0.1,-0.000105 at C10,and-0.001673 with a linear standard-measure baseline. Complete-case restriction across continuous **and categorical** predictors retained60 patients (51 cases),gain-0.000044;the legacy output key `complete_continuous` denotes this stricter restriction. Plausible-pressure/ST restriction retained135,gain-0.000010. There were no overlapping full feature profiles to exclude. A single source-label permutation gave AUC0.440 and gain-0.000082;this is a leakage sanity check,not a calibrated null test.

Post-primary challenges were not used to select a replacement model. Removing cholesterol/protocol gave gain0.000001;adding explicit spline representations of log resting and peak pulse pressures gave gain0.000029. Restricting both source and target pulse pressures to at least20mmHg gave gain0.000039 (292 source,136 target);training/evaluating only known beta-blocker nonusers gave gain0.000003 (273 source,81 target). Such minute sign changes are consistent with a redundant transformation changing approximation or ridge geometry rather than discovering a new state variable. Target-only female results involve just5 patients and support no subgroup inference.

Manual standard-library checks independently of the model code reconstructed eligible VA records,pressure differences,log ratios,labels,mean Brier difference and bootstrap percentiles. Source and target raw common predictors and binary endpoints agreed with processed files after accounting for row order,missing sentinels and invalid slope coding. Prediction was row-permutation equivariant and functions never accept target labels. A clean rerun of a copied publication package in the same isolated environment matched predictions,bootstrap values,challenge outputs,arithmetic checks,and result JSON except runtime. These are **author self-checks,not independent peer review**.

## Limitations and future test
Referral bias,anatomical versus functional disease,64 excluded VA patients,small non-CAD target count,missing cholesterol and especially source ST slope,unavailable workload harmonization,medications and exercise measurement error constrain inference. Raw Hungarian protocol values resemble workload values (e.g.75,100,125),whereas VA uses documented protocol codes;we retained them as opaque categorical values rather than pretending they are harmonized exercise protocols. The no-protocol challenge does not resolve their underlying physiological confounding. Exercise/rest pressure timing and auscultatory definitions cannot be independently verified from this historical repository. The source and target amplification distributions differ. Missingness may reflect clinical decisions and can itself leak institution-specific pathways without label leakage. Linear extrapolation and a highly correlated,relatively large spline design may degrade external calibration. The narrow incremental interval pertains only to this fixed candidate/model family,not biological equivalence.

A future prospective observational clinical study could standardize exercise stage,posture,cuff method,rest/peak timing and medication recording,collect repeated reliable DBP readings,and compare preregistered models against conventional pressure and workload measures in an untouched hospital. Blinded adjudication could separately test anatomical CAD and functional ischemia. This describes a possible study;we did not perform it. Present evidence does not justify using this ratio for clinical decisions.
