# Campaign: connectomes, synapses and single neurons

Subcampaign of the campaign `sci:sha256:c6fef5a19cfdc10222ff79abeab6bc3903cb22958f27a5f1b79d3a0b96ff8700` (what do connectomes reveal about how nervous systems are built?).

## Subquestion and scope

Properties of individual synapses, contacts and neurons: synapse size and placement, glia at contacts, neuron morphology and cable scaling.

## Status and main result

Status: several rules found. In human H01, synapses of one connection share size (correlation 0.27 against 0.07 for neighbouring synapses of other axons), and a connection-specific size term remains; glia (oligodendrocytes, OPCs, microglia) sit at neuron somata in mouse and human; neuron cable grows more slowly with synapse number than optimal wiring predicts, and distal spine synapses are not size-compensated.

## Open questions and proposed next tests (not yet run)

Test the connection-specific size term with functional imaging of the same synapses; check glial placement in a third species.

## Publications

Grouped by what each paper reports. A failed improvement test or a model-only bound does not rule out the approach in general; each line states the paper's own scope.

### Positive findings

- [In one human cortex sample (H01), synapses of one axon segment on one neuron had more similar sizes than near synapses of other axons, for excitatory and inhibitory inputs](https://sciscape.io/p/8b80c88b43b51460d4c2e4e3c6313af8b139baa5031fc56fe6fc217e0507f9da) (`sci:sha256:8b80c88b43b51460d4c2e4e3c6313af8b139baa5031fc56fe6fc217e0507f9da`). Finding: Size correlation within a connection is 0.266 [0.258-0.273] vs 0.065 for near synapses of other axons (E to E); same for inhibitory inputs and interneuron targets; a connection-specific term remains (0.169 [0.093-0.248]); effect does not decay out to >20 um; smaller with mask voxel volume. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Huge sample and controls, but small correlations (0.16-0.27), box-diagonal size proxy and a single human sample.
- [In one mouse cerebellum EM volume, ascending axons of 455 granule cells made 1.9 times more synapses per Purkinje cell than their parallel fibres through longer contact, not a higher synapse rate](https://sciscape.io/p/810dd9944b6d50a4aaaead8c7d52c74d14c0cf833e3f1011bd4e65336bcb371c) (`sci:sha256:810dd9944b6d50a4aaaead8c7d52c74d14c0cf833e3f1011bd4e65336bcb371c`). Finding: AA connections have 1.92x more synapses (3.01 vs 1.57) due to longer contact (19.4 vs 7.2 um), not higher rate (0.80 per um contact, [0.76-0.85]); cleft area -3.9% after section-geometry control (CI -10 to +2%). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: 455 GrCs with cluster-robust intervals and geometry control, but one animal/region and pf seen over only ~55 um.
- [In one mouse and one human cortex EM volume, oligodendrocytes, OPCs and microglia sat at neuron cell bodies more often than local density predicts; astrocytes and pericytes did not](https://sciscape.io/p/62952699fcdc4798c87e3bdb90926b3ab05774759231bb7869f2940153d77540) (`sci:sha256:62952699fcdc4798c87e3bdb90926b3ab05774759231bb7869f2940153d77540`). Finding: Oligodendrocytes (2.61x), OPCs (2.25x) and microglia (2.06x) peak at 5-6 um from neuron nuclei in mouse (human oligodendrocytes 4.87x); about half of oligodendrocytes are satellites; astrocytes and pericytes show no contact peak. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): strong: Two large volumes with block bootstrap and cell-type labels, consistent across species.
- [In one mouse V1 volume, spine synapses 1.5–6 µm apart on one dendrite stretch had less similar sizes than equally near pairs on other branches; most of a 3D-distance dip came from spine geometry](https://sciscape.io/p/0b2e7db91b560398ba96f2906ebcb015d5d22dbe64bae8c8a7e93f2c69793d21) (`sci:sha256:0b2e7db91b560398ba96f2906ebcb015d5d22dbe64bae8c8a7e93f2c69793d21`). Finding: Dip in size correlation at 3-4 um (-0.0067 [-0.0082,-0.0053] MICrONS), mostly attributable to spine geometry (spine size vs distance from centre line r=0.20); positive minimum in H01. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Tiny correlations; effect mostly a geometric artefact and measures differ between datasets.
- [Synapses of one excitatory connection share a two-state size component and a graded part in two mouse cortex EM volumes; in human H01, with size-dependent noise modelled, the sharing is mostly graded](https://sciscape.io/p/60480e177996e6c33df8816106bea237940c749c4a2ff8db9d53807e4590c4fc) (`sci:sha256:60480e177996e6c33df8816106bea237940c749c4a2ff8db9d53807e4590c4fc`). Finding: Mouse: binary component replicates (states 3.5-fold apart; 60-63% of shared variance in MICrONS) with a graded part also needed; human H01: sharing mostly graded once size-dependent noise is modelled (binary share 0.45 to 0.08). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Conclusion depends on parametric model; one nuisance parameter moves binary share from 0.36 to 0.60 (mouse) and 0.45 to 0.08 (human).
- [In a 2% sample of one whole fly CNS (BANC), mitochondrial fraction increased with output synapse density across neurons of a class, but not with synapse count in 4 µm pieces of neuropil](https://sciscape.io/p/8593952a842cd59c26ffc3e432ee9c82dcc9e6aa6d59c2bbe23e315b37c71b23) (`sci:sha256:8593952a842cd59c26ffc3e432ee9c82dcc9e6aa6d59c2bbe23e315b37c71b23`). Finding: Deposited BANC mitochondria-to-neuron table is unusable (87.1% of centroids in separate segments; per-neuron column is 0 for 69%); a shell rule reassigns with 90.6% agreement. Mitochondria fill 8.1% of neurite volume (cell-type property); across neurons doubling output density goes with +9.5% (7.2-11.9) mitochondrial fraction, but not with synapse count in 4 um pieces. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Large sample and useful data-quality audit; but based on a 2% volume sample and a heuristic assignment checked on 360 cases.
- [Beyond additive axon and target terms, same-connection synapse pairs had 16–20% lower size differences for excitatory and 3–6% for inhibitory inputs in human H01 and mouse MICrONS cortex](https://sciscape.io/p/1551992d83ab325edaa2ae2623924af29ad79c7a8bd3fb19f82d24c4521324d5) (`sci:sha256:1551992d83ab325edaa2ae2623924af29ad79c7a8bd3fb19f82d24c4521324d5`). Finding: Joint ratio below 1 for excitatory synapses (human 0.832 [0.815,0.852]; mouse 0.804-0.837) and smaller for inhibitory (human 0.943; mouse inhibitory somata 0.968), indicating a connection-specific size term (about 16-20% of excess in excitatory, 3-6% inhibitory). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Huge synapse counts and two-way bootstrap CIs across both species; but segmentation merges and bounding-box size proxies bias toward 1 and each species is a single sample.
- [FlyWire v783 transmitter predictions gave synapses onto octopamine, serotonin and dopamine neurons 1.1–1.9 percentage points more of the target's own class; the increase needed target outputs nearby](https://sciscape.io/p/f9e28959c699092fa1b838a747cc4916d739887739c2dd65e0591954e41faf77) (`sci:sha256:f9e28959c699092fa1b838a747cc4916d739887739c2dd65e0591954e41faf77`). Finding: Synapses onto OA, 5-HT and DA neurons carried +1.9 (1.4-2.4), +1.1, +1.3 pp more of the target's own class (baselines 2.3-4.4%), needing target outputs nearby (about 0 if no target outputs, up to +3.7 pp when more than half outputs); explains <=0.17% of within-neuron variance. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Permutation-controlled with dose-response structure; but small effect with neuropil-level control and few literature-labelled monoaminergic neurons (60/79/390).
- [In mouse and human cortex EM volumes, each glial cell body that touched a neuron soma went with 4–10% fewer somatic synapses; glial-presynaptic detections hid this in raw H01 counts](https://sciscape.io/p/3be949fb1883f131274df0e3d3850696ad8d784e10aa217689f5f1c7e29acdda) (`sci:sha256:3be949fb1883f131274df0e3d3850696ad8d784e10aa217689f5f1c7e29acdda`). Finding: Soma faces touching glia had 0.26-0.52x expected synapses (mouse) and 0.56-0.71x (human, after removing glial-presynaptic detections); touching glia went with 4-10% fewer somatic synapses per neuron. Raw H01 counts hid the deficit because 9.6% of perisomatic detections name a glial presynaptic segment. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Within-neuron and between-neuron effects in two species with a documented data artefact; but one animal per species and association only.
- [Within 167 cortical cell types from 8 mouse and human data sets, input conductance increased with dendrite size as much as capacitance did (κ = 1.03); IT cells in one mouse M1 set did not](https://sciscape.io/p/21e0812a4c7e69500f171170f930bb84fa216337f957cc00e2b4be714a86bf17) (`sci:sha256:21e0812a4c7e69500f171170f930bb84fa216337f957cc00e2b4be714a86bf17`). Finding: Pooled kappa=1.03 (0.90-1.14; 1,461 cells, 167 types); tau did not change with size. Exception: mouse M1 IT cells (kappa 0.30; unit 0.48). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Many datasets and a measurement-error-robust ratio; but mostly Allen pipelines and slice artefacts.

### Bounds and trade-offs

- [In 1,651 complete mouse axons, local arbors used extra wire for shorter tip paths, but long-range skeletons used 31–46% more wire than nearest-attachment trees with no shorter paths](https://sciscape.io/p/2c3642370c08469c9f0c208f5f5f89a06527ea12c226b9ae5023f57a919fd4e4) (`sci:sha256:2c3642370c08469c9f0c208f5f5f89a06527ea12c226b9ae5023f57a919fd4e4`). Finding: Local arbors use 1.27x null wire for 0.96x path length (trade-off), but long-range skeletons use 31-46% more wire with no shorter paths (path ratio 1.066/1.012); whole axons 2.24x the greedy minimum wire. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Large sample with bootstrap over brains and two null trees, but Euclidean comparison ignoring tissue and tips as target proxy.

### Negative and null results

- [Arbor cable length increased as the 0.36–0.52 power of synapse number at fixed hull volume in EM neurons of fly, mouse and human, less than optimal wiring predicts for the same counts and hulls](https://sciscape.io/p/2157801884355d2800269a44fe61b457e9f0b09e786a84ea3ba35c9d2ec2f3f9) (`sci:sha256:2157801884355d2800269a44fe61b457e9f0b09e786a84ea3ba35c9d2ec2f3f9`). Finding: Cable length scales with n^0.36-0.52 at fixed hull volume, below constant-density (1) and below optimal-wiring MST prediction by 0.11-0.41; all 95% intervals exclude zero gap; also in 4,180 axonal arbors. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): strong: 6,007 neurons in four datasets with matched MST controls and cross-validation.
- [Beyond class degree, CeNGEN expression added 22–40 millinats per pair to contact prediction but −1 to 9 to synapse prediction among touching classes in two C. elegans nerve rings](https://sciscape.io/p/4694527a37755b3e38fb5edb121c58e92d590ac9aaa405a8f4b20bf2d44ce649) (`sci:sha256:4694527a37755b3e38fb5edb121c58e92d590ac9aaa405a8f4b20bf2d44ce649`). Finding: Uncontrolled expression gain for chemical synapses was 42-43 mnats/pair but fell to 1.2-1.6 mnats (CIs include zero) once contact and class degree were included; expression still added 22-40 mnats for contact prediction (permutation p=0.01) and predicted presynaptic tendency (R2 0.36, 0.21). No CAM gene was in the top 5% for contact. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Careful nested CV, bootstrap and planted-signal calibration; but only 82 classes, two animals, L4 expression, binary calls and a limited model family.
- [With synapses as targets, the cable of 266,001 neurons in two fly connectomes grew as V^0.22–0.27·N^0.48–0.49, not V^(1/3)·N^(2/3), and was about 1.1 times the minimum tree through the synapses](https://sciscape.io/p/7a6e377e9da9746c60f19a6c824ad0e3c38286fec4baeb1ae5d10de7e94db5cd) (`sci:sha256:7a6e377e9da9746c60f19a6c824ad0e3c38286fec4baeb1ae5d10de7e94db5cd`). Finding: No: free fit gave L ~ V^0.27 N^0.49 (Male CNS) and V^0.22 N^0.48 (FlyWire); with V^(1/3) fixed the synapse exponent was 0.40 (0.38-0.42) and 0.36 (0.32-0.40). Cable was about 1.1x the EMST through the neuron's own synapses, which lie along neurites (box dimension 1.0-1.2). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): strong: 266,001 neurons in two independent connectomes with consistent exponents and a mechanism (synapse sites along neurites); tests a stated theoretical law.
- [Synapse size did not offset passive dendritic attenuation in mouse and human cortex EM volumes; mouse spine synapse size fell by about 20% over the first 150 µm](https://sciscape.io/p/4c0d18c59330f78bb5a0276bc7d4e080f409d8ba5bbeae1bc7b1ceacf53a40c2) (`sci:sha256:4c0d18c59330f78bb5a0276bc7d4e080f409d8ba5bbeae1bc7b1ceacf53a40c2`). Finding: No: mouse spine synapses 140-160 um from soma were 24% (basal) and 18% (apical) smaller than at 20-40 um; compensation ratio K was -1.7 to -5.6 (basal), 0.00 (apical). Distal apical regrowth beyond 150 um is exploratory. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: 2.6M synapses with cable model, but passive model with radius uncertainty, label dependence and size proxy.
