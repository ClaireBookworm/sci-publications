# Campaign: non-invasive brain measurement, probing with ultrasound

Subcampaign of the campaign `sci:sha256:b8346ed353aa99d00b9d7b22e9e509e59ecd2745f211e0f790cccf564215b70d` (how can human brain activity be measured without surgery, better than today?).

## Subquestion and scope

Ultrasound sent through the skull and detected as sound: functional ultrasound, Doppler, localization microscopy, and skull acoustics models.

## Status and main result

Status: bounds. Most papers model skull acoustics and inverse problems in plates and spheres; several find non-identifiability or audit failures of published skull parameter sets. Functional ultrasound through the skull is limited in parietal cortex (coarser than 3T fMRI in most simulated draws), while an endovascular array near a venous sinus may resolve smaller voxels than 7T fMRI within about 1.5-2 cm (minimally invasive).

## Open questions and proposed next tests (not yet run)

Validate skull-correction rules on measured human skull data; test orbital and temporal acoustic windows with matched fMRI.

## Publications

Grouped by what each paper reports. A failed improvement test or a model-only bound does not rule out the approach in general; each line states the paper's own scope.

### Positive findings

- [A link-budget and anatomy model suggests that an ultrasound array in a brain venous sinus can resolve cortical blood-volume changes more finely than 7T fMRI within about 1.5-2 cm](https://sciscape.io/p/766182abbe7ab5000a01b56e41130215c900acb2c3ac0b7d12e4f3c1594493de) (`sci:sha256:766182abbe7ab5000a01b56e41130215c900acb2c3ac0b7d12e4f3c1594493de`). Finding: Link-budget and anatomy model finds smaller detectable voxels than 7T fMRI within about 1.5-2 cm of the sinus (10% CBV change, z=5, 60 s per condition). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: model-only, endovascular device outside strict noninvasive scope.
- [Plane strain energy tests miss negative bulk loss in an exact local isotropic model of printed skull means](https://sciscape.io/p/1478dfa5c18636d9fd127bc788e0436ea0f08ceb5e0650f6a4ffc6992ae7560e) (`sci:sha256:1478dfa5c18636d9fd127bc788e0436ea0f08ceb5e0650f6a4ffc6992ae7560e`). Finding: White 272 kHz skull means give bulk loss -210.1 MPa (energy gain) yet pass plane-strain tests; sphere outgoing power exceeds incoming by 14.94%; 2 of 10 audited parameter sets fail the 3D test. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Exact and independently checked, but largely known theory applied to a parameter audit.
- [Oblique sound modes increased target pressure under fixed power and local absorption limits in tested periodic elastic plates](https://sciscape.io/p/9552e05728cd6dd198fdfb6a409345fd3f333362b453a38837ac75423d7f0ae8) (`sci:sha256:9552e05728cd6dd198fdfb6a409345fd3f333362b453a38837ac75423d7f0ae8`). Finding: Gain at least 20% in 15 of 27 cases; median 29.6%, max 102.3%. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Deterministic.
- [Repeated echoes recover hidden sound fields beyond the channel count in fixed point models](https://sciscape.io/p/c9992db915035c656bfe70c42d23ed13e137e4804fd2aaae713a6dc68a0e2c42) (`sci:sha256:c9992db915035c656bfe70c42d23ed13e137e4804fd2aaae713a6dc68a0e2c42`). Finding: With 16 channels, 20 scatterers and 256 echo matrices at 40 dB, median direction error 0.078-0.094%, maximum 0.195%. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: noiseless-ish fixed-site simulation.

### Bounds and trade-offs

- [A passive relaxation model fits eight printed skull wave means within their reported widths](https://sciscape.io/p/9cbbe24e2f12f840dde83d6f2cf53327dc134b7ae3c85d6f027331756fa3c8da) (`sci:sha256:9cbbe24e2f12f840dde83d6f2cf53327dc134b7ae3c85d6f027331756fa3c8da`). Finding: Fits eight printed skull wave means within 0.571 of printed widths (0.332 with fixed times); other passive models fit equally yet differ by 6.3 degrees shear phase at 400 kHz over 6 mm. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: data compatibility only, shows non-identifiability.
- [Spherical Gaussian loss reverses planar weak absorption excess in the tested symmetric fluid model](https://sciscape.io/p/dab5ff619a5333e0885d766b45c87e5a2aa8d344745ddabf13296fe17ce13a26) (`sci:sha256:dab5ff619a5333e0885d766b45c87e5a2aa8d344745ddabf13296fe17ce13a26`). Finding: Planar excess (2.77, 1.37, 1.07) reverses in spheres to 0.719, 0.796, 0.906. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: theoretical, narrow.
- [Equal principal sound speeds left large pulse uncertainty in tested passive anisotropic plates](https://sciscape.io/p/686b12fff4076cb361028bb69e401269b3d8440de94dfd2408c92fc183f0728c) (`sci:sha256:686b12fff4076cb361028bb69e401269b3d8440de94dfd2408c92fc183f0728c`). Finding: No: remaining relative field error at least 66.58% in one pulse case; adding a 45 degree bulk speed within 1% still left 46.53% lower bound. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Existence bound in constructed plates.
- [Outer sound fits over an octave left different inner echoes in two passive flat plates](https://sciscape.io/p/04e412eea66bf5e112eaa623cb9d93486d2066b10d8d288b8967308fb6d1a961) (`sci:sha256:04e412eea66bf5e112eaa623cb9d93486d2066b10d8d288b8967308fb6d1a961`). Finding: No: two plates matched both within 0.489% of incident pressure over 400-800 kHz but inner echoes at 600 kHz differed by 6.973%. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Existence counterexample.
- [Normal plane-wave checks left a nine percent pressure error floor at ten degrees in six millimetre elastic slabs](https://sciscape.io/p/409bcd18c9f12d7e7cfa9c00f8cee4792d3874614a18dd821ee2d6f02cbe47a8) (`sci:sha256:409bcd18c9f12d7e7cfa9c00f8cee4792d3874614a18dd821ee2d6f02cbe47a8`). Finding: No: in eight dissipative slab models over 0.35-0.65 MHz the worst-case pressure error is >=9.19% at 10 deg and 23.17% at 20 deg for a 6 mm slab. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Explicit constructed material pairs and independent checks, but idealised slabs.
- [In CT models of 78 orbits, ultrasound through the closed eyelid and orbital roof resolves orbitofrontal blood-volume changes only in about 3-4 mm voxels under eye limits, coarser than 3T fMRI](https://sciscape.io/p/4ef8502c33cc3a3644e7c023a0bebc0d5d84e869ceb671458fc9cdb5e325e0a8) (`sci:sha256:4ef8502c33cc3a3644e7c023a0bebc0d5d84e869ceb671458fc9cdb5e325e0a8`). Finding: Only in about 3-4 mm voxels in one minute under FDA eye limits (111 M rays in 39 CTs), coarser than 3T fMRI, with usable rays crossing ~1.5 mm bone at ~40 deg. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Large CT sample with safety limits, but ray-based only; below the author prediction (1 mm).
- [Fluid phase correction gave target pressure errors in tested uniform passive elastic plates with exact normal incidence matches](https://sciscape.io/p/e7baa3dbb3061d6a25da7a137abeba5eb7cc7466b619ca4abad80a8821de6785) (`sci:sha256:e7baa3dbb3061d6a25da7a137abeba5eb7cc7466b619ca4abad80a8821de6785`). Finding: Over 13,176 plate cases, 15-degree beams kept median 99.900% of best target pressure squared, but minimum 63.36% and 777 cases below 95% (tail with small loss), though normal-incidence transmission matches to 1.78e-15. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: exact reference but synthetic and tail-driven.
- [The phase reference changed error bounds for oblique calibration in a passive skull plate library](https://sciscape.io/p/f86c96a2d9de3edf0e7d77d8d3357aeae08445b86770cf34bb79ed3a123bf903) (`sci:sha256:f86c96a2d9de3edf0e7d77d8d3357aeae08445b86770cf34bb79ed3a123bf903`). Finding: At 45 degrees the face-pressure phase reference gives a 10.59% field-error ceiling versus 0.92% for water ratio; best angles 25 vs 45 degrees; a continuous pair forces at least 5.43% for face reference. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: depends on assigned library and reviewer-added comparison.
- [Delayed linear filters recovered sound in known bone plates but retained large errors across assigned materials](https://sciscape.io/p/83606b9854be833257efabc4bfefc426a7ca9c99d885fb7af72df56be773b418) (`sci:sha256:83606b9854be833257efabc4bfefc426a7ca9c99d885fb7af72df56be773b418`). Finding: Known 6 mm plate: 1.2499% recovery error at 40 us delay (1% noise); one filter for 27 plates with 2% parameter ranges has at least 32.1344% worst RMS error (attained 32.1345%); delay cannot close the gap. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: lower bound matched by construction.
- [Oblique pressure signals reveal hidden pore sources in tested sealed porous plates that meet the Biot condition](https://sciscape.io/p/26ab92a94422a04ddd1e1ea62e2f894b05b311901d2116544dc145d662192ce0) (`sci:sha256:26ab92a94422a04ddd1e1ea62e2f894b05b311901d2116544dc145d662192ce0`). Finding: Under the Biot compatibility condition a pore source can have zero external pressure at normal incidence; nonzero lateral wave number shear coupling makes it visible. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: idealized plates, no data.
- [For a 10% blood-volume change in 1 minute, speckle and bone loss limit functional ultrasound through adult parietal bone to about 2.6-3.5 mm voxels, coarser than 3T fMRI in 59-81% of model draws](https://sciscape.io/p/4f435c31f20d51df739b9a86d5d9f558ecc6d4c5a5ea2da3ef0f666590fe3f5e) (`sci:sha256:4f435c31f20d51df739b9a86d5d9f558ecc6d4c5a5ea2da3ef0f666590fe3f5e`). Finding: Monte Carlo over 3000 parameter draws: smallest voxel median 3.5 mm parietal, 5.2 frontal, 1.7 temporal vs 2.3 mm at 3T and 1.2 mm at 7T; parietal fUS beats 3T in only 24% of draws (coarser in 59-81% depending on conditioning); microbubbles worse than Doppler at clinical dose. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate - checked against rat/human Doppler data and simulation, but outcome is dominated by assumed bone attenuation and fMRI quality.
- [Flat elastic plates gave large sound transmission errors near a turning interval in tested uniform spherical shells](https://sciscape.io/p/633723016dc8b35e8888a14194f7037416ee357dcf29d2ce2955b79d1cd7bfac) (`sci:sha256:633723016dc8b35e8888a14194f7037416ee357dcf29d2ce2955b79d1cd7bfac`). Finding: Over 42,887 cases median complex error 0.274% but 95th percentile 8.0% and up to 97% near a turning interval; wave packet error 15.4%. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak - useful reference dataset but known curvature physics.
- [A traveling wave heat rule gives temperature errors in tested passive elastic skull plates](https://sciscape.io/p/c8affe61270b955bfb3f1cc5a4edaf630502df893b0b1da06e6cb2fc921b3c94) (`sci:sha256:c8affe61270b955bfb3f1cc5a4edaf630502df893b0b1da06e6cb2fc921b3c94`). Finding: On 279 plates peak 10-s temperature error median 4.15%, max 16.0% (35% at lower frequency, 33.8% at normal incidence). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak - modest corrections to one published approximation.
- [At fixed spatial wave number, some passive bone plate models had zeros that prevent a stable causal exact inverse](https://sciscape.io/p/2f6771c939e61299f5b03b378a09fb2b55fac3b4447941bdcc38f2ef863d0c56) (`sci:sha256:2f6771c939e61299f5b03b378a09fb2b55fac3b4447941bdcc38f2ef863d0c56`). Finding: No at fixed wave number: a 6 mm plate has a transmission zero with growth rate 218,544/s at 232.5 kHz, so an exact causal inverse grows in 4.58 us. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate - verified in multiple solvers and FE, narrow geometry.
- [Matched sound transmission and outer reflection left different inner reflection in tested passive plates at one frequency](https://sciscape.io/p/bb25605331ebd0fb486c98add1ef2ceb41a6bcb477ec8be2696515fe839bba9c) (`sci:sha256:bb25605331ebd0fb486c98add1ef2ceb41a6bcb477ec8be2696515fe839bba9c`). Finding: No: at 836 kHz matched plates had inner reflected power 0.4245 vs 0.2123; equal-reflection models err up to 12.0%. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak - exact counterexample but known theory.
- [Receiver distance preserves information in ideal evanescent sound modes with only plate thermal noise](https://sciscape.io/p/28e27d58de9f5f418d10eba4cd6dfcbe6f63ee4948deb59f30799a5a86d7e225) (`sci:sha256:28e27d58de9f5f418d10eba4cd6dfcbe6f63ee4948deb59f30799a5a86d7e225`). Finding: No: signal and plate noise decay equally; with 1 uPa/rtHz detector noise a gap raises information 10.5% at 0.2 MHz and 24.7% at 0.5 MHz, but nearest is best at 1 mPa/rtHz. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak - exact but idealized.
- [External sound reflection sets passive information limits at one frequency for reciprocal matched ports with equal temperature and readout noise](https://sciscape.io/p/5dbb7468aeaae62d1287253f42dcb3d50e10e033e1a19b810d3c4edece850182) (`sci:sha256:5dbb7468aeaae62d1287253f42dcb3d50e10e033e1a19b810d3c4edece850182`). Finding: Ideal match increases information 1.15-1.92x; return rank 3-13 reaches 90%. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak - exact but idealized.

### Negative and null results

- [The critical angle did not set a five percent error limit for fluid models of tested passive bone plates](https://sciscape.io/p/d63c94e37c0ed8e1c6c2015b584ee68168971e49c4e126c2a762d9391f8b9069) (`sci:sha256:d63c94e37c0ed8e1c6c2015b584ee68168971e49c4e126c2a762d9391f8b9069`). Finding: In 675 plate-frequency cases error exceeded 10% below half the critical angle in 472; median first 5% crossing at 0.266 of that angle; narrow beams often much better. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Large sweep with independent FE check, but flat-plate effect largely anticipated.
- [Two passive fluid models gave different normal transmission through flat layers with the same bulk dispersion root](https://sciscape.io/p/4c3a7a64c219ca3fbdad7b749cd765872208301b4ed2c5d4101134901571fe56) (`sci:sha256:4c3a7a64c219ca3fbdad7b749cd765872208301b4ed2c5d4101134901571fe56`). Finding: No: median transmission difference ~2.5%, max 46.32% across 243 cases. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Existence demonstration.
- [One shared waveform cannot fit the tested skull pulse recordings within ten percent](https://sciscape.io/p/18553c0cf90df452c841d0daaa51842d669f7453b7d090a73a864e136151adaf) (`sci:sha256:18553c0cf90df452c841d0daaa51842d669f7453b7d090a73a864e136151adaf`). Finding: No: lower bound 20.07-32.48%; fitted 29.68-48.16% error across 16 recordings. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Provable lower bound on real recordings.
- [Some archived phase data have large errors near phase wraps in flat fluid skull models at 500 kilohertz](https://sciscape.io/p/9dbb90a565c4c3ce5a126308b97eeb60156bde2a8500f120436eea6e0c35421d) (`sci:sha256:9dbb90a565c4c3ce5a126308b97eeb60156bde2a8500f120436eea6e0c35421d`). Finding: Amplitude-shape errors are 0.23-0.81% but complex-field errors are 36-52%, clustered near the phase wrap (0.93 deg to 105 deg in the last bin), consistent with an interpolation/export artifact. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Concrete audit of public data with an identified artifact, but partial execution of the planned nine-benchmark audit.
- [Quadratic phase fits left larger slope residuals in six skull sound maps than in their water controls at 270 kilohertz](https://sciscape.io/p/2aa20bba3493a95e1ea8c9d248f8ad44de2525b9dba63fe466867d67e3985b90) (`sci:sha256:2aa20bba3493a95e1ea8c9d248f8ad44de2525b9dba63fe466867d67e3985b90`). Finding: Quadratic fit left phase-slope residuals of 1.41-5.39% of water wave number in six skull maps vs 0.41-1.43% in water controls at 270 kHz. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Small dataset with confounds noted by the reviewer.
- [A reused skull mean parameter set passes a plane strain loss check but adds energy in a solid sphere](https://sciscape.io/p/7d15692e90245b6eb14236167b0456eb55a1a8fecd9736dab14f6332a026ef71) (`sci:sha256:7d15692e90245b6eb14236167b0456eb55a1a8fecd9736dab14f6332a026ef71`). Finding: With rho 1900 kg/m3 the bulk loss is -0.231 GPa; plane strain loss is nonnegative but a radial wave in a 4 mm solid sphere gives output/input power 1.15705 (FE controls agree to 0.00187 pp). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: exact and checked, but narrow and a constructed case.
- [A source scan ratio raised pulse errors in selected water scans from a human skull dataset after calibration at 55 mm](https://sciscape.io/p/755436e6c8e6df88fc06176c3a66536abe1bb874b714687d1e0c5614c71d7d5d) (`sci:sha256:755436e6c8e6df88fc06176c3a66536abe1bb874b714687d1e0c5614c71d7d5d`). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: exploratory audit of one rule, direction changed mid-study.
- [A fitted gain and lateral shift leave large errors in six measured skull sound fields at 270 kilohertz](https://sciscape.io/p/de737c9804da455084629058ab59f5c380ff76541542becf14843261a3d66f0c) (`sci:sha256:de737c9804da455084629058ab59f5c380ff76541542becf14843261a3d66f0c`). Finding: At 270 kHz complex RMS errors were 51.6-78.8% (amplitude-only 32.3-44.3%; with axial shift 50.2-70.6%) in six paired scans from three ex-vivo skulls; in-sample fits. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: real measured data but only three skulls.
- [Nine terms after phase removal could not correct eight lossless fluid skull grid models within ten percent at 500 kilohertz](https://sciscape.io/p/a5aa0026bafc961cb00bdcc18e52f227d33e7ae475039f1734279defa083ef59) (`sci:sha256:a5aa0026bafc961cb00bdcc18e52f227d33e7ae475039f1734279defa083ef59`). ### Methods, tools and resources

- [Calibration uncertainty changed the best tested sound measurement angles in uniform passive elastic plates](https://sciscape.io/p/76bd0b06b716bd6d8d0099b0463556363945abc43390e3931fd070b2d171ff7c) (`sci:sha256:76bd0b06b716bd6d8d0099b0463556363945abc43390e3931fd070b2d171ff7c`). Finding: Angles 0/30/40 deg give 0.672% worst shear uncertainty versus 1.737% for best two-angle set; adding an amplitude slope changes the best set to 0/15/20 (4.03%). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: synthetic design audit.
- [A symmetric spatial loss law preserves reciprocity and passivity in periodic fluids for fixed exponents between one and two](https://sciscape.io/p/9a4442aee53789c21f66185c897ca1b5951bc101380a2ed22d95a6e004a22a17) (`sci:sha256:9a4442aee53789c21f66185c897ca1b5951bc101380a2ed22d95a6e004a22a17`). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Exact proof plus numerics, but small increment on known fractional-loss literature.
- [Two spatial moment terms reduced maximum periodic damping error for Gaussian test fields in one and three dimensions](https://sciscape.io/p/d4a28a0ce25fd911206482e397d866a6f19a6612c320e5a3a693e7d67531cbde) (`sci:sha256:d4a28a0ce25fd911206482e397d866a6f19a6612c320e5a3a693e7d67531cbde`). - [Thirty-two curved steps kept sound transfer error below one percent on the primary grid of uniform passive spherical shell modes with at least one percent transmitted power](https://sciscape.io/p/d0c80108ee17ce643d2911c055ccdd9c307a9283b1e34c50a6552ef175169c3a) (`sci:sha256:d0c80108ee17ce643d2911c055ccdd9c307a9283b1e34c50a6552ef175169c3a`).
