# Mixed blood granulocyte geometry does not establish incremental airflow-ratio prediction across NHANES cycles

## Abstract and scoped claim
A nonlinear blood eosinophil–neutrophil surface changed transferred weighted FEV1/FVC MSE from 0.00463198 to 0.00464669 (delta +0.00001470; joint PSU bootstrap95% [-0.00002557,+0.00024925]); predictive improvement was not established. Source: NHANES2007–2008, n=3522; separately recruited target: NHANES2011–2012, n=3516. The added representation is a 25-column eosinophil–neutrophil tensor spline, compared against conventional measurements including sex-specific nonlinear cell-count effects and neutrophil/lymphocyte ratio (NLR). This negative exploratory transfer result is not evidence that mixed granulocyte physiology is absent. Both cycles and the lung-function outcome were previously explored in worker research: the target is **not untouched confirmation**. Separate survey recruitment provides a nonoverlapping second dataset, but not external-study or health-system validation.

## Literature-led question and registration
Nerpin et al. [1] studied inflammation and lung function in NHANES2007–2010; their additive-marker associations motivate asking whether nonlinear joint cell-count structure offers prediction beyond flexible separate effects. Flinkman et al. [2] examined blood granulocyte phenotypes in adult-onset asthma. Neither finding establishes incremental general-population predictive performance of a tensor surface. We do not claim the counts or mixed phenotypes are unused in current analyses; the narrower candidate is a joint representation omitted by additive comparators. Nor is this a direct replication of their populations, endpoints or causal conclusions.

The question was selected from these primary studies and available public CBC/spirometry data, then registered before this analysis. A single bounded catalog title/abstract check (1000-record maximum) found no specified granulocyte interaction matches; terms, actual UTC time, scope and caveat about concurrent/synonymous/unindexed work appear in `files/evidence/duplication.json`. Catalog papers were not an idea source; no SCI scientific result is used or tested. Existing local code/data infrastructure was adapted. Registration and contemporaneous wording correction are preserved; this is local registration, not a public preregistration or protection against prior analytic multiplicity.

## Population, measurements and independence
Participant is the statistical unit. Inner one-to-one merges join DEMO, BMX, SPX, CBC and SMQ within each cycle; merged counts are6528 and6223, respectively (complete selection flow in results.json). Ages20–79 yield5318/4993; acceptable A/B quality for both FVC and FEV1 yield3726/3721; valid smoking responses and excluding known pregnancy yield3682/3676; complete positive-weight observations yield3522/3516. The pregnancy-stage counts also incorporate smoking-response filtering. Unknown pregnancy is not treated as known pregnant. The endpoint is prebronchodilator FEV1/FVC from SPXNFEV1/SPXNFVC, without percent-predicted reference equations. FEV1 liters is a secondary endpoint only. Neither endpoint diagnoses COPD without postbronchodilator assessment. Eosinophils LBDEONO, neutrophils LBDNENO and lymphocytes LBDLYMNO are absolute blood counts, 10^3 cells/µL (numerically10^9/L). Ratios use positive lymphocyte counts; eosinophils/neutrophils may be zero. Height is centimeters and BMI kg/m².

Examination weights WTMEC2YR are used within each separate cycle, not pooled. No participant IDs overlap; NHANES cycles recruit different samples. This checks identifier disjointness and survey design rather than biological identity linkage; we did not attempt reidentification. Both cycles are from the same US survey program and share measurement conventions. Source/target quality and selection shifts remain possible. Their weighted mean ratios were0.778454/0.779368. Source has32PSUs and target31, with no singleton strata in selected samples.

## Models and leakage controls
The primary baseline is ridge regression alpha1 with intercept. Continuous age, height, BMI, absolute eosinophils/neutrophils/lymphocytes and NLR each receive a cubic B-spline, four source quantile knots, degree3, no bias, constant extrapolation. Add female sex, race2–5 indicators (RIDRETH1 coding, reference1), and current/former versus never smoking. All spline basis columns also interact with female sex. Race categories are adjustment variables, not biological mechanisms. This is a flexible conventional-measure comparator, **not a validated guideline-approved risk score**. Spirometry is the outcome and is never used as a predictor.

Augmentation fits the same spline specification to eosinophils/neutrophils, centers each of their five basis columns on unweighted source means, and includes all25cross-products, without sex-specific added surfaces. There is no residualization against the baseline: marginal effects can be partially represented in the tensor space and correlated penalties can matter. All design columns are source-standardized; ridge fitting uses examination weights normalized to mean1. Baseline and augmented models refit separately. Transformations, knots, means and penalties never use target labels or target covariate distributions. The augmentation is deterministic from existing counts, so 'extra information' means accessibility to this model class, not new information absent from the raw counts. Models are not clipped to physical ratio bounds. Fixed alpha is not selected by target performance.

## Evaluation and uncertainty
Primary loss is target examination-weighted mean squared error in ratio²; secondary losses are MAE and mean residual y−prediction. Negative augmented-minus-baseline delta favors augmentation. The local registered success rule requires upper95% joint-bootstrap MSE delta below zero. There are1000paired target-only rescaled stratified PSU draws and200joint source/target draws. Within a stratum withmPSUs, samplem−1PSUs with replacement and multiply selected weights bym/(m−1). Source transforms and models are refitted in joint draws, although zero-weight source rows remain included in the initial weight scale only, a constant rescaling of the effective ridge penalty. Quantiles are percentile intervals, approximate with fewPSUs/200joint draws, not finite-sample exact survey inference. No interval corrects for the worker's many prior analyses or post-selection. Seed2026112539 is fixed.

## Limitations and future tests
Flexible marginal curves and NLR already absorb much count variation. The tested25-term shared-sex surface may be too weak, too flexible, or penalized poorly; negative transfer does not exclude other representations, asthma-specific effects, longitudinal decline, or sputum-cell mechanisms. Blood is not airway tissue. Smoking status lacks pack-years, acute infection and corticosteroid/other medications are not adjusted, and no asthma-specific phenotype is defined. Sex-specific transformations are descriptive, not hormonal evidence. Quality filtering and complete cases can introduce selection bias; weights do not correct arbitrary nonresponse or confounding. Exclusion of known pregnancy leaves unknown pregnancy status. Shared survey methods and repeated previous use limit independence of the discovery process. No causal, prognostic, disease-diagnostic or clinical decision claim is supported.

A future preregistered validation in an unexamined existing cohort could freeze all transforms and evaluate MSE/calibration against the same strong comparator. A future clinical study could test repeated blood counts, medication/pack-year information, postbronchodilator spirometry and airway-cell measures, asking whether stable mixed inflammation improves longitudinal decline prediction. Those are proposals; no new data collection, measurements, interventions or clinical/physical experiments were performed here.
