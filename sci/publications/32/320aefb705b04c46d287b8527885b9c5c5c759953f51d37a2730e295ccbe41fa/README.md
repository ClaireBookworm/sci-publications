# A magnitude time-arrow feature fails frozen cross-study stair-direction transfer

## Abstract and central claim
Does temporal direction in acceleration contain body-state information omitted by conventional amplitude and cadence summaries? We tested a signed cubic-increment statistic of acceleration magnitude for stair descent versus ascent. In 2,935 windows from 32 WISDM subjects, grouped out-of-fold AUC changed from 0.73928 to 0.75228. Source-fitted models were frozen before reading the independent UCI HAR target: in 2,950 windows from 30 subjects, AUC changed from **0.83125 to 0.80427**, gain **−0.02698** (conditional paired subject-bootstrap 95% interval **−0.05258 to −0.00446**). The two-sample source-coefficient-refit interval was broader, **−0.07756 to 0.01986**. Thus the predeclared improvement criterion fails. This is evidence against this fixed pipeline's transport benefit, not proof of equivalence, universal harm, or absence of physiological temporal information.

## Motivation and scope
Stair direction is an observed locomotor body state, not a diagnosis. Biomechanical work distinguishes ascent/descent impulse and timing, while standard accelerometer summaries discard some temporal order. We do not assume that larger impacts imply greater total joint load, and do not infer a mechanism from phone signals. The study tests an omitted temporal statistic beyond a specified conventional feature family; it does not establish that all current analyses ignore temporal information, nor claim algorithmic novelty. Deep sequence models, axis-specific features, and the full UCI 561-feature set are not benchmarked. The contribution is a reproducible failed-transfer test across independent studies rather than a proposed clinical biomarker.

Literature/data selection preceded the documented SCI lexical duplication check. Its single check returned 15 lexical matches, chiefly unrelated “staircase” research; no WISDM/UCI HAR study was identified in those records. That cannot exclude unindexed or concurrent work. ## Registered prediction and methods
The prior plan is `files/question-registration.json`; amendments are explicit. Descent=1, ascent=0. No disease outcomes or new measurements. A useful pattern required positive held-out-subject and frozen-target AUC gain with the target paired 95% interval excluding zero. This was not met.

WISDM records are parsed with newline/semicolon delimiters, trailing commas stripped. One malformed partial record is dropped; 1,098,208 complete records remain. Contiguous sequences break at subject/activity changes, nonpositive time differences or gaps >0.2s; zero timestamps are excluded. Stair segments are linearly interpolated at 20Hz and chunked into nonoverlapping 51-sample windows (2.55s nominal acquisition duration; sample support 0–2.50s). No window crosses a detected boundary. Acceleration is converted from m/s² to g using 9.80665. UCI 128-sample total-acceleration windows are interpolated to 51 points on the same 0–2.50s grid, already in g. Its native filtering and overlapping segmentation cannot be undone. The primary interpolation does not add an anti-alias filter; a post-inspection sensitivity applies an 8Hz fourth-order Butterworth filter before target resampling. This preprocessing mismatch is a real limitation, not a biological explanation.

For magnitude m=norm(acceleration), the 13 conventional features are mean, SD, 10th/50th/90th percentiles, skewness, excess kurtosis, RMS successive difference, largest-power Fourier-bin frequency, spectral centroid, spectral entropy, and fractional power in 0.5–3 and 3–8Hz bands. Fourier powers use demeaned magnitudes with zero DC. The added statistic is A=mean(diff(m)^3)/(mean(diff(m)^2)^(3/2)+1e-12). A changes sign with time reversal and is orientation-rotation invariant. Its spline can use both signed and unsigned aspects; it is not a pure test of the signed linear coefficient. Baseline features are time-reversal invariant; numerical tests verify these algebraic properties.

Both models use source-fitted standardization, cubic additive splines (4 uniform knots, degree3, no bias), a second standardization and L2 logistic regression, C=0.1, tolerance1e-8, maximum2000 iterations. Hyperparameters are fixed, not selected on target outcomes. Each subject gets equal total fitting/scoring weight and each direction half of that person's weight. Sample weights are scaled to sum to source window count for fitting. Five-fold GroupKFold confines all a person's source windows to one fold. Final source-only models are serialized and hashed **before the target loader is called**; the hash is recorded in results. Discovery CV informed interpretation, not hyperparameter selection. After the first target read, additional challenges are labeled exploratory; no second untouched confirmation is claimed.

Primary AUC is pooled with subject/direction-balanced weights, not the mean of individual AUCs. Brier and threshold0.5 balanced accuracy are secondary. Fixed-seed (73419) paired bootstrap resamples target subjects 1,000 times, keeping their windows together and recalculating weights. A 200-replicate two-sample bootstrap also resamples source subjects and refits weighted coefficients/scalers; spline support retains observed source covariate support, so this is not complete uncertainty over newly drawn signal distributions. No window-level independence, p-value fishing, target recalibration or participant-label random split is used. These are conditional convenience-sample intervals, not national-population survey estimates.

## Limitations and future discriminating test
A source-side gain does not establish a portable body-state marker. Phone mounting, sensor bandwidth, irregular sampling, truncation, gait speed, staircase geometry and differing task execution are confounded with study. Sex, clinical status and subject demographics are not uniformly available; no covariate-adjusted mechanism can be inferred. The restricted magnitude baseline is meaningful for orientation robustness but not state-of-the-art HAR. Bootstrap intervals over30/32 convenience volunteers are unstable for wider populations; source coefficient uncertainty includes zero. No equivalence margin or noninferiority test was planned. Activity labels do not establish muscle physiology, metabolic cost, disease, fall risk or clinical utility. No new data were collected here.

A future study could preregister cross-device/cross-location validation with simultaneous pocket/waist recordings and independently measured stair direction, speed and force-plate timing, testing whether a signed temporal statistic retains incremental value under harmonized bandwidth. Older adults or mobility-impaired participants would require their own prospective clinical validation. Those studies are proposals only, not experiments performed or evidence supplied by this analysis.
