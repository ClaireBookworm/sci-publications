# Phosphate–albumin coupling gives no clear incremental digit-symbol prediction across separately sampled NHANES cycles

## Abstract
A fixed serum phosphate-by-albumin interaction was tested beyond flexible individual laboratory measures, conventional mineral summaries and demographic covariates. Models were fitted in1173 NHANES2011–2012 adults aged60+ and tested without refitting in1384 independently sampled2013–2014 adults. Weighted DSST MSE increased from163.0692 to163.2028 points²; baseline-minus-augmented error was−0.1336 (conditional target-PSU95% interval−0.2986 to+0.0286; joint coefficient-refit interval−7.9199 to+8.4141). Sensitivity directions varied with regularization and weighting; source cross-validation did not favor augmentation. This does not demonstrate incremental predictive value, equivalence, causality or clinical utility. It does not rule out other representations or meaningful gains under better-identified models.

## Literature-led question
[Slinin et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5749239/) studied phosphate and cognition in older men and found an executive-function association, not consistent longitudinal/global cognitive associations. [Schram et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17979900/) reported calcium-related cognitive findings in older cohorts. In dialysis, [Shoji et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11547714/) evaluated phosphate, calcium–phosphate product and nutritional adjustment, including effect modification by nutritional indices. These are observational, population-dependent findings, not evidence that a new predictor will work.

Our inference from this literature was that the joint phosphate/albumin state might carry nutritional/mineral context not captured by additive individual terms. We therefore tested one explicit centered interaction for incremental DSST prediction. We do not claim phosphate, albumin, nutritional effect modification or calcium–phosphate product is novel. This is a narrow out-of-sample representation test, not a comprehensive search of all current analyses. Any gain would be a better use of already recorded variables, not additional acquired information. A ratio and conventional mineral summaries were already included in the comparator to discourage a weak-baseline result. No accepted blood-only clinical DSST prediction standard is asserted.

The dated question, comparator, decision rule and resource budget were registered before the main analysis (files/question-registration.json; local shared-service registry/mineral-cognition.json). The single post-selection SCI duplication check returned36 titles/abstracts; none tested this exact feature/comparator/outcome study. Related studies concerned different features or outcomes. The query, actual timestamp and response are in files/evidence/catalog-check.json and catalog-attempt-1.txt. This limited check cannot exclude unpublished or unindexed overlap. No SCI publication generated the question or was used/tested as scientific evidence; none is cited for participation.

[CFQ documentation](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/2013/DataFiles/CFQ_H.htm) defines CFDDS, the digit-symbol substitution score, a cognitive-performance measure rather than a disease diagnosis. Raw inputs are not packaged; the retrieval script verifies their hashes. [CDC terms](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/policy/data-user-agreement.html) permit public-use statistical analysis without special approval and prohibit identification/restricted linkage. We complied.

Of9756 source records,1791 were age60+ and1173 met complete-case, positive phosphate/albumin/weight and valid education-code criteria. Of10175 target records,1841 were60+ and1384 met the same criteria. Variables required for complete cases are listed exactly in code/analyze.py. These are separately recruited cross-sectional cycle datasets, not splits of one cohort. There is zero SEQN overlap. Public IDs do not allow ruling out unrecognized reenrollment; we performed no identity linkage. Both datasets share a survey programme, protocol and population frame: this is cross-cycle transfer, not validation in another country, clinical programme or disease cohort. These cycles and DSST outcomes were previously inspected in this workspace for other hypotheses and are widely studied. The present target test is question-specifically frozen, not globally untouched external confirmation. No favorable result was selected for publication.

## Frozen models and uncertainty
Baseline continuous inputs: age, family-income/poverty ratio, BMI, albumin, creatinine, glucose, bicarbonate, phosphate, calcium, albumin-corrected calcium Ca+0.8×(4−albumin), calcium×phosphate and phosphate/albumin. The corrected calcium formula is a conventional proxy, not measured ionized calcium or a universally accurate correction. Each continuous input uses a cubic spline with four uniformly spaced source-range knots, no spline bias and linear extrapolation. Sex, race/ethnicity and education use indicator columns. Corrected calcium and product/ratio comparators are deliberately redundant biological summaries, not separate measurements.

The augmented model adds z(phosphate)×z(albumin), standardized from the source only. Design columns are source-standardized. Ridge alpha1 has an unpenalized intercept. Source MEC weights are divided by their mean; target errors use target MEC weights normalized similarly. All knots, encodings, scaling and model coefficients are source-fit. No target outcome or covariate is used for fitting or hyperparameter choice. Target risk is weighted concurrent score prediction, not future decline forecasting. Source5fold shuffled CV refits every preprocessing step within folds.

The prespecified primary statistic is weighted MSE(baseline)−MSE(augmented); positive means better. Evidence requires a target95% interval wholly above zero. We resample the observed masked PSUs with replacement within each public stratum (2000 draws), holding both models fixed. A second300-draw source-and-target PSU bootstrap refits coefficients with source representation fixed; it includes some training uncertainty but not knot/scaling re-estimation. Source has31 PSU/stratum cells across14 strata (some strata have3 PSUs); target has30 across15 strata. No singleton strata remain. These simple public-design percentile intervals are approximate, not official replicate-weight or full-population guarantees. Small PSU counts, nonresponse, measurement error and pipeline/choice uncertainty are not fully represented. Seed71847 is fixed.

## Results
Source/target complete-case n were1173/1384. Weighted confirmation RMSE changed12.76985→12.77508 DSST points, and MSE163.06918→163.20278 points². Primary gain was−0.13360 points² (−0.082% of baseline MSE). Conditional target interval [−0.29863,+0.02856] crosses zero; coefficient-refit interval [−7.91988,+8.41406] is much wider and crosses zero. The latter prevents a claim that clinically meaningful alternative-model benefit has been excluded. Weighted MAE likewise slightly increased10.09782→10.09843 points. Mean of five source-fold gains was−0.41135 points² (an unweighted mean of fold-specific weighted errors, not a pooled national estimate).

## Interpretation and limitations
The registered interaction did not establish improved confirmation-cycle prediction beyond this flexible baseline. This does not refute phosphate or albumin physiology, prove zero association/equivalence, identify a causal mechanism or support a treatment/supplement. The wide training-refit interval and sign changes under weighting/regularization restrict inference to the specified pipeline and data.

Age, sex, race/ethnicity, education, income, BMI and renal/metabolic proxies reduce some confounding, but blood draw timing, medication, kidney disease, inflammation, nutrition, sensory/motor ability and unmeasured social factors remain. No causal adjustment sufficiency is claimed. DSST combines several cognitive and motor/sensory processes; disease incidence and cognitive decline were not outcomes. Restricted complete cases, survey-frame similarity, prior outcome exposure, highly redundant predictors, top-coded age and lack of a truly external clinical cohort limit generalization. Source bootstrap fixes preprocessing. Target use for sensitivity descriptions cannot be recycled as new confirmation. This work supplies one informative failed representation test, not a discovered clinical biomarker.
