# Joint AST–ALT information did not establish incremental cognitive-score prediction across NHANES cycles

## Abstract
Among 1170 NHANES2011–12 training and 1391 NHANES2013–14 test participants aged60+, a log-AST×log-ALT interaction changed MEC-weighted DSST mean squared error from 164.527790 to 164.495284. Baseline-minus-augmented MSE was 0.032506 (target-PSU bootstrap95%CI -0.094995 to 0.166605); incremental benefit was not established. RMSE changed from 12.826839 to 12.825571 score points. This is failure to establish an increment for one specified representation, not evidence of no enzyme–brain association or equivalence. No clinical recommendation follows.

## Question and relation to literature
Liver enzymes and their AST/ALT (De Ritis) ratio already have cognitive associations [1,2]. The 2024 study pooled these same NHANES cycles to study nonlinear enzyme associations. We do not discover the ratio, infer a new mechanism, or claim all current analyses ignore joint enzymes. Instead, can the interaction of enzyme levels recover predictive information beyond flexible separate enzymes **and the established ratio**, in a separate survey-cycle dataset? This tests a possible gap between published marginal associations and incremental transportable prediction. Its negative outcome is preserved rather than relabeled a biomarker discovery. No SCI publication is used or tested; none is cited.

Question selection used primary literature and data availability, not the catalog. Registration preceded this question's model fitting (files/question-registration.json). One documented question-specific SCI duplication check, including three access retries, returned403; duplication remains unknown, not excluded (files/documentation/catalog-check.json). The prior completed payload/request/receipt/evidence were archived before active files were replaced.

## Population, data and access
CDC unrestricted public-use NHANES2011–12(G) is discovery/training and2013–14(H) is cross-cycle testing. Demographics(DEMO), examination BMI(BMX), standard biochemistry(BIOPRO) and cognitive testing(CFQ) were merged one-to-one by SEQN. The statistical unit is a participant, not a visit or laboratory replicate. These are different cross-sectional samples, not random partitions of one dataset. Included identifiers are unique within and disjoint between cycles. Public files cannot establish whether a person ever re-enrolled; no identity reconstruction was attempted. Common sampling frame and assay protocols limit external independence. This is not a validation in another country, clinic, or contemporary cohort.

These public DSST outcomes and cycles have appeared in earlier, different-question workspace analyses and in the motivating paper. Thus they are not a globally pristine holdout. The current question/term/baseline were locked before this analysis, with no target-selected tuning; source CV is exploratory discovery, H is a fixed cross-cycle test. An entirely untouched independent cohort remains necessary for strong confirmatory claims.

Eligibility: age>=60, observed DSST(CFDDS), all model covariates, positive AST/ALT and MEC weights, and education codes1–5. Missing values were excluded without imputation. Age-eligible source 1791 and target 1841 decreased to 1170 and 1391 complete cases; source/target male counts were 581/670. Full sample files had 9756/10175 participants. Complete-case selection and inability to complete cognitive tests can bias associations. Age is public-release top-coded. DSST is a continuous performance measure, not diagnosed dementia, prospective decline, or global cognition.

AST(LBXSASSI) and ALT(LBXSATSI) are U/L; albumin(LBXSAL) is g/dL, creatinine(LBXSCR) mg/dL, BMI kg/m². Sources and SHA256 input hashes are in files/input-manifest.json. Reused cache inputs were rehashed; original access dates where unavailable are unknown. Public files need no special approval, but their agreement restricts use to statistics and prohibits identification [5]. We comply; raw participant files are not redistributed here. Source retrieval is separate from offline computation.

## Locked models and uncertainty
Baseline weighted ridge regression includes age, income-to-poverty ratio, BMI, albumin, creatinine, natural log AST, natural log ALT, and raw AST/ALT, each expanded by cubic B-splines with4 uniformly spaced source-fitted knots, no spline bias and linear extrapolation. Sex, race/ethnicity and education are one-hot categorical. These are conventional risk/performance correlates and established enzyme measures, not a validated clinical cognition score; we do not claim comparison to every clinical model. The proposed extra column is the product of source-standardized log AST and log ALT. All expanded columns are source-standardized before ridge; primary penalty alpha=1, intercept unpenalized. The baseline already controls nonlinear enzyme marginals and their ratio; ratios/interactions are deterministic re-expressions, not new measurements. Collinearity means interaction coefficients cannot be given a mechanistic interpretation.

Training and testing weights are each normalized to mean1 within cycle; model estimation and primary error use WTMEC2YR. Normalization does not alter weighted mean loss. No target outcomes, moments or knots enter training. The source fivefold CV refits transformations within folds (source_cv in results.json); it does not tune alpha or select the interaction. Predictions are not clipped to the DSST range. The primary estimand is baseline-minus-augmented test MSE; positive favors augmentation. Success was registered as a95% interval entirely above0, not merely a positive point estimate. This criterion was not met.

Uncertainty uses2000 paired resamplings of public PSUs within public strata, holding source model fixed; there are 30 target PSUs. This is an approximate survey-design bootstrap, not a fully calibrated official NHANES variance estimator. A300-replicate sensitivity resamples both source(31 PSUs) and target and refits regression coefficients, holding the original source feature transforms fixed: interval [-0.725344, 0.303923]. It omits uncertainty in knots/scaling and should not be called a full-pipeline refit interval. Fixed seed71831. Public survey stratification labels, rather than participant rows, define the bootstrap sampling groups. Complete-case weights are not adjusted for nonresponse, and estimates should not be treated as nationally representative population effects. CIs quantify model/test uncertainty under this resampling, not unmeasured confounding.

## Limits and future test
No predictive superiority, causal liver–brain pathway, disease screening utility or clinical biomarker is established. Cross-sectional enzyme variation can reflect nutrition, muscle, medications, alcohol, liver injury, frailty, socioeconomic conditions and reverse causation. Age, sex, race/ethnicity, education, income, BMI, albumin and creatinine adjustment does not eliminate these confounders; medications, alcohol, neurological disease and assay drift were not comprehensively controlled. Small samples, selection, top coding, splines and regularization can obscure real signals. The negative result applies only to this joint term, baseline, outcome, age group and cross-cycle comparison. It does not refute earlier enzyme associations or exclude alternative representations. This work does not demonstrate information outperforming standard measures; it records an explicit failed candidate under the research objective.

A future independent prospective cohort could preregister this exact feature construction, collect repeated liver and muscle biomarkers plus medication/alcohol/nutritional measures, and test whether the frozen predictor improves repeated DSST or incident cognitive decline beyond a clinically validated baseline. That would distinguish stable physiology from transient enzyme shifts and test calibration and meaningful utility across sites. No new measurements or clinical experiments were performed here.
