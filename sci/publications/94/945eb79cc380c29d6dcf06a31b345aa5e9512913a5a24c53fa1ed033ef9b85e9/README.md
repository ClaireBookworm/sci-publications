# GGT–ALP coupling adds no reliable cross-cycle prediction of the prebronchodilator FEV1/FVC ratio

## Abstract
A registered product of source-standardized log serum GGT and log alkaline phosphatase was added to flexible conventional enzyme, demographic and smoking predictors of prebronchodilator FEV1/FVC. Training used NHANES2007–08 adults20–79 (n=3510); frozen transfer used the separately recruited NHANES2009–10 sample (n=3910). Target weighted MSE worsened from43.251250 to43.252298 percentage-points squared. Joint source–target survey-PSU bootstrap95% interval for MSE gain was[−0.044692,0.031355]. Source cross-validation and regularization checks were also negative. This specific feature provides no demonstrated incremental prediction. Validation is between cycles of one survey, not independent institutions, and does not establish a causal liver–lung mechanism or clinical utility.

## Scientific question and scope
Serum GGT is associated with pulmonary function in primary population research [1], and prospective UK Biobank work already examines conventional liver-function markers and COPD [2]. Joint GGT–ALP interpretation is familiar in assessing ALP elevations, although a primary clinical analysis reports limited utility of GGT for that task [3]. We ask a narrower computational question: does **one nonadditive joint enzyme feature** improve out-of-cohort airflow prediction after accounting for flexible marginal enzyme values and conventional covariates? Concordant high enzymes could reflect a different systemic state than isolated ALP elevation. This motivates, but does not demonstrate, a mechanism. The feature contains no new measurement; its only potential value is predictive representation beyond the specified baseline. We do not claim joint enzyme interpretation is unused generally.

## Registered prediction and methods
Outcome is `100*SPXNFEV1/SPXNFVC`, percentage points [5,6], **not** postbronchodilator COPD, future decline, or a diagnosis. No spirometric input enters the predictors. Conventional baseline: additive cubic splines (three evenly spaced knots; linear extrapolation; no spline bias column) of age, BMI, height, logGGT, logALP, logALT, logAST, albumin, bilirubin, creatinine and log(GGT/ALP), plus one-hot sex, harmonized race/ethnicity (RIDRETH1) and never/former/current smoking. Smoking derives from SMQ020/040; special/missing responses are not treated as smokers or nonsmokers. Logs are natural; serum enzymes are U/L. Bilirubin, creatinine and albumin are not log-transformed. The log-ratio spline gives the baseline an additional joint-enzyme summary, a conservative test of extra representation. These conventional measurements are not a validated clinical prediction score.

Augmentation adds just `(logGGT−source_mean)/source_SD * (logALP−source_mean)/source_SD`. Source means/SD are unweighted in the main fit. Spline and categorical transforms are fitted exclusively in source; each design is then source MEC-weight standardized. Weighted ridge regression uses fixed alpha10 with unpenalized intercept. Weights are normalized to mean1. All transformations, coefficients and hyperparameters are frozen before target scoring. Target-only diagnostics do not select a new model. Source five-fold GroupKFold holds out entire stratum–PSU groups, refits preprocessing within folds, and is exploratory, not independent confirmation. Alpha1/100 are sensitivity tests, not selection.

Primary metric is target survey-weighted MSE; gain is baseline minus augmented MSE, so positive favors the feature. Registered success required positive gain with joint source–target bootstrap95% interval entirely above zero;1% relative MSE reduction was a descriptive utility benchmark, not a clinical threshold. MAE is secondary. Survey uncertainty uses rescaled bootstrap: within each retained stratum with m PSUs, draw m−1 PSUs with replacement and scale selected multiplicities by m/(m−1). There are2000 target-only paired replicates and300 independently resampled source–target full pipeline refits, seed181222. Source preprocessing is re-estimated in each refit; original MEC weights and resampling multiplicities enter fits. The retained domains have no singleton strata. These approximate percentile intervals have finite bootstrap Monte Carlo error and do not adjust for all selection or broader program-wide multiple testing.

## Limits, interpretation and prospective test
This is a negative result for one representation and baseline. It does not refute liver–lung biology, all enzyme interactions, or nonlinear models generally. Conventional risk factors and enzymes are correlated; regularization and spline basis influence representation. Complete-case inclusion, spirometry exclusions, limited age range, assay/protocol shifts and residual smoking intensity, alcohol, medication, liver/bone disease and environmental confounding constrain generalization. No alcohol or pack-year adjustment was available in this declared input set. Survey weights do not remove those issues. Cross-sectional laboratory prediction of concurrent airflow is not causal inference, diagnosis, or treatment selection. Broader program exposure and repeated biomarker questions prevent strong pristine-confirmation or novelty claims. No reliable previously unused health information was discovered here.

A future independently recruited cohort could preregister and freeze this exact pipeline, obtain conventional enzyme and standardized pre/postbronchodilator spirometry measurements, document alcohol, pack-years, medication and liver/bone conditions, and test paired prediction errors with adequate precision. Longitudinal measurements could distinguish stable systemic physiology from transient enzyme variation and assess subsequent airflow decline. A clinical or experimental study would require separate authorization and design; none was conducted in this public-data computation.
