# Swing-fraction serial memory does not improve cross-dataset Parkinson probability prediction in a small-source transfer test

## Abstract
Swing and stance subphase dynamics may contain information absent from stride variability. We tested one short-lag swing-fraction memory scalar against demographic, stride and swing summary measures, using31 gaitndd participants for training and153 gaitpdb participants for frozen testing. Adding lag-one swing-fraction correlation changed external Brier from 0.324931 to 0.327361 (delta +0.002431; joint source/target bootstrap95% interval [-0.026992,+0.025016]); the prespecified incremental-benefit criterion was not met. AUC increased from 0.623879 to 0.673741, but probability accuracy worsened and both models underperformed a source-prevalence constant. This exploratory negative result is conditional on the estimator, case mix and small training sample; it neither establishes equivalence nor excludes physiological information in subphase dynamics.

## Question and rationale
Can local serial organization of the fraction of a stride spent swinging add Parkinson/control information beyond conventional gait summaries? [Plotnik, Giladi and Hausdorff2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17503027/) motivates examining subcycle timing beyond speed and variability. [Hausdorff2009](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2719464/) distinguishes gait dynamics from marginal variability. The [gaitndd documentation](https://physionet.org/content/gaitndd/1.0.0/) explicitly asks whether subphase dynamics differ from stride dynamics. Thus serial gait analysis and subphase timing are not new scientific ideas. Our contribution is a narrow, reproducible incremental-prediction test, not a claim that all current analyses ignore swing timing. The scalar is absent from our chosen baseline, not unused throughout the literature. It is also not the published bilateral phase coordination index (PCI); the source duration tables do not directly provide the corresponding alternating heel-strike phase sequence.

Selection followed primary literature and public documentation, before a single bounded duplication check. `files/evidence/registration.json` froze question, baseline, criterion and checks before main results. The catalog query on2026-11-25T07:22:42.566259+00:00 returned1000 records, with0 matches to['swing fraction', 'duty factor', 'swing-time fraction', 'duty memory']; endpoint and scope are in duplication.json. Bounded search cannot rule out older or concurrent unpublished duplication. We did not choose this question from catalog papers. The [previous gait-volatility paper](https://sciscape.io/p/6b22294aeb50f499b74fba1a0728d876e650c2f0d87c673cb2197676e8e4ee39) is cited only because its extraction, source/target cache, model-fitting and isolated reproduction framework were actually adapted; neither its feature nor its outcomes are inputs here. This is one further exploratory hypothesis in a broad research program without program-wide multiplicity adjustment.

## Measurement and prespecified models
Use20–110s, excluding startup and approximately harmonizing durations. Source duration tables supply stride (columns2/3) and swing (4/5), one-indexed. Source percentage columns corroborate interpretation. Target100Hz force totals (columns18/19) define foot contact above20N; retain contact onset only with preceding swing>=0.08s and following contact>=0.10s. A cycle goes from an accepted onset to the next accepted same-foot onset; swing is the next onset minus the current accepted contact's offset. Select by cycle ending time20–110s. Failed contacts may create apparently valid doubled intervals; threshold sensitivities only partly address this risk. Source and target event processing differ intrinsically.

Round stride x and swing s durations separately to0.01s in both datasets, then f=s/x. Validity requires0.5<=x<=2.5s and0.1<f<0.7; retain only adjacent valid rows without bridging excluded intervals. At least40 adjacent pairs per foot are required. For each foot, M=Pearson correlation(f_i,f_(i+1)) over valid adjacent pairs; average the two feet. Zero-variance correlation is defined as0. No absolute value or lag selection is performed. This statistic measures local persistence, not long-range fractality or neural mechanism. Because f shares a stride denominator and event timing errors, apparent memory need not be independent physiology. It is invariant to time reversal; positive rescaling preserves correlation only with the validity mask fixed.

Baseline predictors are age, female indicator, mean stride, log strideCV, stride lag1 correlation, mean swing fraction, log swingfractionCV, and relative interfoot stride-mean asymmetry. CV uses sample SD. Average footwise summaries except asymmetry=absolute foot mean difference divided by their average. Augmented model adds M. Independently standardize each predictor set using source data, fit L2 logistic regression C=1, max_iter3000,tol1e-9; freeze it for target prediction. No imputation or target calibration. The source sample31 is small for8/9 predictors, making overfitting a major limitation. Speed is excluded from primary to preserve sample availability, then explicitly adjusted in sensitivity. Conventional stride/swingduration variability controls are also challenged below. These are relevant summaries, not an exhaustive clinical classifier or PCI comparison.

Primary endpoint: target Brier score, augmented minus baseline. Success requires the upper95% bound of a joint class-stratified participant bootstrap below0. We also report AUC and log loss, without changing the primary endpoint based on results. Seed2026112511;2000 target-only draws,500 joint draws independently resampling source participants within class, refitting both pipelines, and resampling target within class. Percentile intervals condition on class counts, not population prevalence, site sampling, pipeline uncertainty, or research-family selection. SourceLOO refits preprocessing and regression but is not another dataset.

### Exact opposing-label duplicate sensitivity
The prespecified153-record target result above includes two copies of the same raw signal under opposing labels, violating independent-record assumptions for that pair. We preserve it rather than silently revise the registration. After the author audit, we excluded **both** records, without adjudicating either label, leaving151 target records (83 Parkinson,68 controls). The frozen source model and feature definition are unchanged. Deduplicated Brier was0.325203 versus0.327821, delta+0.002619; joint500-resample95% interval=[-0.030602,+0.028170], seed2026112512. AUC changed0.623494 to0.673281. This post-audit correction likewise fails to establish incremental Brier benefit. Its interval is preferable for independent-record interpretation, though unresolved person-level overlap remains. Original official retrieval URLs, byte counts, identical hashes, predictions and resampling arrays are preserved. The duplicate signal is a data-quality observation about these specific public files, not a generalized accusation about the database or its contributors.

## Limitations and future test
This negative, estimator-specific result means incremental probability benefit was not demonstrated. Small training n, rich predictors, weak transport calibration and broad uncertainty prevent an equivalence claim. Distinct measurement pipelines, uneven age/sex/speed distributions, medication, footwear, turning and protocol differences can confound transport. Site subgroup analyses do not fix them. No causal inference, broad disease screening, diagnostic thresholds, population risk estimates, or certified participant nonoverlap follows. Program-wide exploratory reuse reduces strength versus untouched validation.

A future preregistered clinical validation could freeze extraction/model before accessing a new nonoverlapping cohort, use synchronized foot-event annotations to quantify denominator/sensor artifacts, compare PCI and optimized clinical baselines, stratify medication and speed, and assess calibration and prospectively defined functional outcomes. Such recruitment/measurements were not performed here and are outside this existing-data project.
