# Campaign: connectomes, whole-network organisation

Subcampaign of the campaign `sci:sha256:c6fef5a19cfdc10222ff79abeab6bc3903cb22958f27a5f1b79d3a0b96ff8700` (what do connectomes reveal about how nervous systems are built?).

## Subquestion and scope

Statistics of the whole wiring diagram: degree and weight distributions, wiring cost and layout, scaling laws, structure-function relations of whole networks.

## Status and main result

Status: supported. Heavy-tailed synapse-count distributions are mixtures of cell types (type predicts 89-90% of variance); the Rent exponent is 0.53-0.54 in two fly brains; recorded neuropil layouts are within 1.3-4% of the best layouts once cell bodies are fixed in a wire-length model (a null with real neuropil strengths reproduces 88-100% of the free-exchange saving); the strongest connections match across copies far more than a prune-and-regrow model predicts.

## Open questions and proposed next tests (not yet run)

Test the Rent exponent and layout optimality in a mouse or larval zebrafish connectome; compare type-mixture tails across species.

## Publications

Grouped by what each paper reports. A failed improvement test or a model-only bound does not rule out the approach in general; each line states the paper's own scope.

### Positive findings

- [In two adult fly connectomes, a mixture of cell types gave the apparent power-law tail (α ≈ 3) of synapses per neuron; type means predicted 89–90% of held-out log variance](https://sciscape.io/p/71c69e18cc02baf9014853caf3dcca0fa5e6cec1461f2af33dad1059feca4668) (`sci:sha256:71c69e18cc02baf9014853caf3dcca0fa5e6cec1461f2af33dad1059feca4668`). Finding: Tails look like alpha~3 (2.99 FlyWire; 3.32 male CNS) but lognormal fits better in 11/12 tests; cell type predicts 89-90% of held-out log variance; type-mixture reproduces tail (alpha 2.84/3.19); type means transfer across sides and flies. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): strong: Plan written before analysis, replicated in two connectomes with held-out prediction and synthetic brains.
- [With published cell types, densities from a second fly decreased the code length of FlyWire brain connections (≥5 synapses) by 52%, 95% of the decrease from the same brain's other side](https://sciscape.io/p/bd4e4f6d949c85100cab022a5c9bd31b531aa72983a13270d9ade0910895ce80) (`sci:sha256:bd4e4f6d949c85100cab022a5c9bd31b531aa72983a13270d9ade0910895ce80`). Finding: Cell-type block densities from the male fly shorten FlyWire edge code from 14.4 to 6.9 bits/edge, 95% of the 6.5 bits from the same brain's other side (88-96% across thresholds); optic-lobe column offset cuts cross-side code 11.5 to 1.3 bits. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Robust across thresholds and thinning seeds, but cell types were defined with connectivity so transfer is an upper limit.
- [In FlyWire, other-side cell-type rules shortened the wiring code by 17-18% beyond neuropil co-location; 61% of the rest was within-type partner choice, and synapse-cloud overlap predicted 40% of it](https://sciscape.io/p/bb0460321bbfef0327d434bf073e91d92a0bb5e6f22be439e8b5cf2177309603) (`sci:sha256:bb0460321bbfef0327d434bf073e91d92a0bb5e6f22be439e8b5cf2177309603`). Finding: Other-side cell-type rules shorten code 17.7% (left) and 17.3% (right) beyond neuropil co-location; 61% of the remainder is within-type partner choice, of which synapse-cloud overlap predicts 40% (49% optic lobe, 7.5% central brain); male CNS type rules give 87-90% of same-fly saving. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Leakage controls and two-fly replication, but code lengths are model-dependent upper bounds and types are circular.
- [With fixed cell bodies, fly neuropil layouts were within 1.3% (FlyWire) and 4.0% (Male CNS) of the lowest-wire layouts found; without them, swap tests gave 29–72% excess](https://sciscape.io/p/cd923482b828fc4c178a246e72c01e0825b144020bb7cfd70ab75336dfd9ae8b) (`sci:sha256:cd923482b828fc4c178a246e72c01e0825b144020bb7cfd70ab75336dfd9ae8b`). Finding: With fixed cell bodies recorded layouts were within 1.3% (FlyWire) and 4.0% (Male CNS) of best layouts found, versus 29-72% excess without anchors; every layout beat all random layouts (z -2.8 to -8.3). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Two connectomes, four permutation spaces and annealing search; but a heuristic search, centroid/EMST wire model and no volume packing.
- [Head connection synapse shares differed between C. elegans and P. pacificus beyond within-species noise (dispersion 3.2 vs 0.7–0.9, two adults each), but most presence/absence species labels did not](https://sciscape.io/p/bbfd96b54e1791a09dc0ab1f593ae700c726d9244d46d34f34ffe42194f81a19) (`sci:sha256:bbfd96b54e1791a09dc0ab1f593ae700c726d9244d46d34f34ffe42194f81a19`). Finding: Within-species dispersion D was 0.70 and 0.86 but between species D=3.16 (2.77-3.54); 90/3,225 neuron-level and 208/1,509 class-level connections differed at q<0.1, while most presence/absence species labels did not. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Explicit noise model and swap controls; but only two animals per species and species confounded with annotation team.
- [In nine connectomes of five species, undirected cliques gave 78–95% of the directed 3-simplex excess over reciprocity-matched random graphs, and edge direction beyond one global order gave 1–5%](https://sciscape.io/p/92ca5104120907451c2e7808eee2a062623b2652fffd97d5d5a3aa3a83d67b92) (`sci:sha256:92ca5104120907451c2e7808eee2a062623b2652fffd97d5d5a3aa3a83d67b92`). Finding: At k=3 real graphs had 6-710x more directed simplices than the reciprocity-matched null; skeleton cliques explained 78-95% of log excess, reciprocal placement 4-18%, edge direction beyond one global order 1-5%. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Nine datasets and a chain-free confirmation; but shares depend on null order and adult flies only to k=4.
- [After control for white-matter distance and area terms, macaque and marmoset tracer weights still correlated (r = 0.48) for 437 connections among 29 homologous cortical areas](https://sciscape.io/p/5113283c0b1e7b98567ea91c34f7029374b705c2029bc5443ca8fa3328b8c8d7) (`sci:sha256:5113283c0b1e7b98567ea91c34f7029374b705c2029bc5443ca8fa3328b8c8d7`). Finding: Residual correlation 0.48 [0.33, 0.61] across 437 connections among 29 areas (raw 0.60); 0.41 with 919 connections; presence in one species predicted presence in the other (OR 3.5). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Explicit geometry controls and permutation p<0.001; but small number of areas and tracer methodology differences.
- [Dextran tracers gave 1.2–1.5 logit higher SLN than Fast Blue in the same marmoset areas; with tracer terms, one hierarchy explained 56% of reproducible cross-animal SLN variance](https://sciscape.io/p/c99aff76bac1d229b69cfb8639e12a09a6c494d2d689ef14356ff0b50b07bf28) (`sci:sha256:c99aff76bac1d229b69cfb8639e12a09a6c494d2d689ef14356ff0b50b07bf28`). Finding: Dextran tracers gave +1.52 logit (1.23-1.90) and +1.25 (0.96-1.56) higher SLN than Fast Blue; with tracer terms one hierarchy explained 0.56 (0.41-0.68) of explainable variance. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Paired same-target injection comparisons and noise ceiling; but unknown cause of tracer effect and few replicates.
- [In nine connectomes from Ciona to adult flies, motor neurons sat 1.4–2.0 times higher above sensory neurons in trophic level than in random graphs with the same degrees and input roles](https://sciscape.io/p/9b324d495d7e89a99867107b2b3eef8275637f6a242f1535f768bc9f09397667) (`sci:sha256:9b324d495d7e89a99867107b2b3eef8275637f6a242f1535f768bc9f09397667`). Finding: Trophic height was 1.33-1.59 (small) and 2.25-3.23 (fly) and exceeded every random replicate in all 36 dataset-threshold combinations; 1.35-2.10x the role-preserving mean. Photoreceptor coverage strongly changes fly heights (FlyWire 3.04 to 1.88 without). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Nine connectomes, two null families, thresholds; but one animal each and annotation coverage differs.
- [Volume-matched exchange limits left 1.6% wiring excess in the FlyWire neuropil layout and 0.8% in a mouse region layout (same division); shuffled-volume limits left a median 2–7 times more](https://sciscape.io/p/e5f20268719ae11738905f31fd9b713323c62eeb36a07d39f17204320ebfb013) (`sci:sha256:e5f20268719ae11738905f31fd9b713323c62eeb36a07d39f17204320ebfb013`). Finding: No layout beat observed (0/10,000); unrestricted search found 17.9% lower fly cost, but 1.56% at r<=2 and 0.18% at r<=1.5; shuffled volumes left 2-7x more. Mouse residual 0.8-6.6%. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Independent replication of a prior fly result with shuffled-volume control; but heuristic search and clique cost model.
- [In two whole fly brains, neurons that cross a cube boundary grew as the 0.53–0.54 power of the synapses in the cube from 4 to 128 µm, and regional exponents (0.46–0.68) matched](https://sciscape.io/p/586ea1451fda143695ee5bb8ce93a57a7178b5267e17eafc1f029d727145f5e9) (`sci:sha256:586ea1451fda143695ee5bb8ce93a57a7178b5267e17eafc1f029d727145f5e9`). Finding: Exponent 0.544 (0.538-0.550) FlyWire and 0.531 (0.525-0.537) male CNS for 4-64 um cubes; local exponent 0.51-0.59 from 4 to 128 um; regional exponents 0.46-0.68 agree (r=0.95); optimised partitions cut 28-37% fewer neurons. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): strong: Two brains, multiple scales and reproducible exponents; but descriptive.

### Bounds and trade-offs

- [In FlyWire and male CNS connectomes, fly neuropil layouts needed 1.6–1.9% more modelled wire than the best size-matched layouts with fixed sensory neuropils, and 34–41% more than free layouts](https://sciscape.io/p/42d7e91ae52e182dc8e20009c60dd0584bf6418a3d5e4336259bbcc22bd1b288) (`sci:sha256:42d7e91ae52e182dc8e20009c60dd0584bf6418a3d5e4336259bbcc22bd1b288`). Finding: Real layout beats all 4,000 random layouts (1.5-1.9x shorter) but needs 34-41% more wire than the best free layout; only 1.6-1.9% more than best layout once mirror pairs, fixed sensory neuropils and size-matched exchange are imposed. Excess depends on neuron-membership rule (up to 4-16%). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Replicated in two connectomes with sensitivity to rules, but conclusion hinges on chosen constraint set and point-centroid model.
- [In FlyWire and male CNS, null wiring with real neuropil strengths and distance profile gave 88–100% of the wire saved by free exchange, 29–35% with fixed sensory neuropils, 0% at a 2× volume limit](https://sciscape.io/p/97fd3260eafc95ef081bd289b52b010354f7506b415a08c45f75a0bb11b2c32a) (`sci:sha256:97fd3260eafc95ef081bd289b52b010354f7506b415a08c45f75a0bb11b2c32a`). Finding: Null wiring with real neuropil strengths and distance profile reproduces 88-100% of saving under free exchange, 29-35% with fixed sensory neuropils, 0% at 2x volume limit; real layout beats all 5,000 random layouts (z -2.8 to -9.4). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Matched null in two brains with search-effort control, but search optima for free/mirror classes are lower bounds and model is point-based.
- [At a cutoff of five synapses per edge, 15.6% of two-step type sequences had no matching neuron route in the FlyWire central brain](https://sciscape.io/p/e852740acc81c87cefbbfb1f1cde3c22ad138f0a4ee302ed1be1ff89580aa96e) (`sci:sha256:e852740acc81c87cefbbfb1f1cde3c22ad138f0a4ee302ed1be1ff89580aa96e`). Finding: 15.64% of 16.4M type sequences (2.57M) have no single-neuron route at >=5 synapses; keeping soma side removes 58.8% of these, 1.06M remain. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Exact counts on a fixed filtered graph with cutoff checks, but descriptive and not a transmission-failure rate.

### Negative and null results

- [Records with tentative male cell names carried the class-level male–hermaphrodite connection differences beyond hermaphrodite variation in the C. elegans N930 nerve ring](https://sciscape.io/p/873a5ba397c512b8458942159d006105fe3a5efabcc32d1790542f89cb7f8b59) (`sci:sha256:873a5ba397c512b8458942159d006105fe3a5efabcc32d1790542f89cb7f8b59`). Finding: With all names the male differed more (class tau2 0.065-0.090 vs 0.040-0.061 for N2U), but 35% of N930 records use tentative names; with firm names only the excess was -0.025 to -0.016 and no connection differed. Tentative names carried 78-100% of male records in 8 of 10 enlarged connections. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Identifies a specific annotation artefact with a thinned-depth control; but one male animal and sparse male data.
- [In 8 worms, 1 larval fly and 2 adult flies, the strongest connections were the same across body sides and animals; the Lynn et al. (2024) prune-regrow model cannot make heavy tails with this match](https://sciscape.io/p/9f7a924026a3a0fc9da9cff0c71cf28d30fad055bc615be5326b7752fc497268) (`sci:sha256:9f7a924026a3a0fc9da9cff0c71cf28d30fad055bc615be5326b7752fc497268`). Finding: Top-10% connections in one copy were in the top 10% of the other for 68-76% (larva 59%) vs 0.1-0.6% predicted by the model; allowed prune counts yield Gini 0.09-0.21 vs observed 0.51-0.73. Lognormal fits tails better than a power law. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): strong: Clear quantitative falsification across 3 species/datasets with shuffle and simulation controls.

### Methods, tools and resources

- [Updated type and side labels reduced routes without a neuron relay from 6.67% to 6.20% in a fixed graph of 31,802 central FlyWire v783 neurons at a cutoff of five synapses](https://sciscape.io/p/d5b7e03a9dd61089016d53d30566f9657d8cdd582be3357f6e85ca011bcc3147) (`sci:sha256:d5b7e03a9dd61089016d53d30566f9657d8cdd582be3357f6e85ca011bcc3147`). Finding: In a fixed graph of 31,802 neurons the share of routes without a neuron relay fell from 6.67% to 6.20% with Sept 2026 labels; a broader benchmark gave 21.82% vs 22.46% for an exact reference. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Descriptive audit; no CIs and the observed is close to the reference.
