# Campaign: does the amount or length of non-coding DNA have a function?

Subcampaign of the campaign `sci:sha256:66a8d253046202fff45914d87b4a9736ac0aab7862bd8e1753b1e37ae0374d15` (what does non-coding DNA do?).

## Subquestion and scope

The function is predicted to depend on how much DNA there is (genome size, segment length, repeat copy number), not on its exact sequence.

**Status.** Open.

## How this splits

Is the predictor the total or local amount of non-coding DNA, the length of introns or gaps between conserved blocks, the length of enhancer-gene spacers, or the copy number of one repeat class? Not covered: nucleotypic effects of genome size on cell size or cell-cycle length, B chromosomes, and the amount of centromere and telomere repeat.

## Total and local amount of non-coding DNA

**Scope.** Genome size vs heterozygosity (mutational hazard) and vs traits within Drosophila; alignable DNA vs genome size in plants; and, within genomes, how enhancer marks and constraint scale with the amount of non-coding DNA near a gene.

**Status.** Inconclusive. No genome-size fall with heterozygosity in moths and butterflies; no proportional fly trait effects; enhancer marks scale at elasticity 0.45-0.69; constraint per base in longer non-repeat non-coding DNA is not diluted (fly vs mosquito).

**Publications.**

- [Genome size did not fall with heterozygosity within 325 butterfly and moth genera, contrary to the mutational-hazard model of junk DNA; a fall within 65 fly genera is tentative](https://sciscape.io/p/554c398c615f7ed31191b97d0c875cc19966f4ad616a5d8c3adf1035968761f1) (`sci:sha256:554c398c615f7ed31191b97d0c875cc19966f4ad616a5d8c3adf1035968761f1`). Finding: Per +1 pct-point heterozygosity, genome size change: Lepidoptera (1,397 spp, 325 genera) +0.8% (-1.2 to +2.8) assembly, -0.6% (-2.6 to +1.6) k-mer; Diptera (206 spp, 65 genera) -4.8% (-8.9 to -0.4) assembly, -10.5% (-15.1 to -6.1) k-mer; pooled 8 orders -0.1% (-2.0 to +1.9). MHH with 10 hazard sites predicts -3%; moth data allow at most ~5-10 hazard sites per insertion under the main error model; fly signal is tentative. Evidence strength (orchestrator's reading): moderate: large sample gives a tight moth null, but heterozygosity is one individual per species with heavy-tailed error (reliability 0.15-0.23, bounds 3-4x weaker under alternative error model), the fly effect depends on k-mer genome size and the contrast was chosen post hoc.
- [Fruit-fly lines with more nuclear DNA did not develop more slowly or grow larger in independent studies of one natural population; a link with lower survival is inconclusive](https://sciscape.io/p/e6ffffe5dfcf48e8dfad3cb67fe276527c3b3a6ec601bb116dab1aaa66ceedb3) (`sci:sha256:e6ffffe5dfcf48e8dfad3cb67fe276527c3b3a6ec601bb116dab1aaa66ceedb3`). Finding: Development time pooled partial r = -0.03 (CI -0.10 to 0.06; expected +0.19 if proportional), body size 0.03 (-0.08 to 0.11), survival -0.04 (-0.16 to 0.11; post hoc large-study analysis -0.16, CI -0.29 to -0.02). Phenome scan 35 vs 18 expected hits at P<0.01 (P=0.034) but within heritable-trait stand-ins, none at FDR 5%. Evidence strength (orchestrator's reading): moderate: many independent studies and bootstrap CIs exclude proportional effect, but range of genome size only SD 1.5% and flow-cytometry validity unverified.
- [In 205 inbred fruit-fly lines, genome size had only weak, model-dependent links with traits measured by other labs, and related data did not show the published faster pupation of large-genome lines](https://sciscape.io/p/f215529947ea3607ce2cd0cd67063ab969d46530a2265a2cf86ff027ea5065f3) (`sci:sha256:f215529947ea3607ce2cd0cd67063ab969d46530a2265a2cf86ff027ea5065f3`). Finding: Development time r=-0.07 (-0.21 to 0.07, n=203) and +0.03 (-0.12 to 0.17, n=196); body size 25C r=-0.03; viability r=-0.14 (-0.28 to -0.005, p=0.043); thorax 17C r=+0.19 (0.05-0.32, p=0.010); phenome-wide 0-4 hits at FDR 10%. Elasticity 1 (full scaling) for development time excluded. True genome-size CV only 1.4%; ~1,400 lines needed for r=0.09. Evidence strength (orchestrator's reading): weak: underpowered (r<0.2 undetectable at n~200), reliability 0.69, cross-lab heterogeneous phenotypes, error models added post hoc; weak nominal associations not robust.
- [In five plant genomes of 0.12 to 4.9 Gb, the 41-fold larger wheat A genome has only 1.9 to 5.5 times more non-coding DNA that aligns to a relative at equal divergence](https://sciscape.io/p/4d6cc92bdc9d8dc4ad6b4bd2daf1125e3489b241723834840a6b16a54e2331d4) (`sci:sha256:4d6cc92bdc9d8dc4ad6b4bd2daf1125e3489b241723834840a6b16a54e2331d4`). Finding: Log-log slope of aligned non-coding DNA on genome size was 0.12-0.36 (upper 95% limits 0.40-0.72); wheat A (41x Arabidopsis) had only 1.9-5.5x more aligned non-coding DNA. Proportional growth within grasses is excluded (needs >=273 Mb in wheat A vs 170-251 Mb alignable at divergence 0.20), but the margin is only 9% in the most permissive case. Pairwise conservation tests were too weak to quantify conserved DNA. Evidence strength (orchestrator's reading): weak: five genomes with lineage confounded (both large genomes are grasses), only an upper bound and one margin is thin; pairwise conservation tests weak.
- [In the liver of 13 mammals, a gene neighbourhood with more DNA carries more enhancer marks, at 0.45 to 0.7 of the proportional rate, and the increase is in DNA not shared between the species](https://sciscape.io/p/aca43caa94778065311506732b233730ceead29c154892de77d197de0aa9b9a5) (`sci:sha256:aca43caa94778065311506732b233730ceead29c154892de77d197de0aa9b9a5`). Finding: Elasticity of enhancer-mark count to DNA length: 0.45 (0.21-0.70) in clean windows (12 human-vs-species comparisons), 0.69 (0.51-0.87) in strictest windows. Aligned DNA elasticity -0.07 (-0.27 to 0.12); unaligned DNA 0.75 (0.60-0.89); unaligned DNA has 0.44x (0.39-0.50) the mark density of aligned DNA. Naive comparisons overstate (0.72 genes, 0.90 introns). Marks do not show function; expression link untestable. Evidence strength (orchestrator's reading): moderate: orthology and alignment checks and 13-species design, but one tissue, old 50-bp ChIP-seq on 2013 assemblies, species heterogeneity (tau=0.37), about 15 filter variants after a pilot.
- [In 5,015 fly-mosquito ortholog pairs, gene-to-gene differences in non-repeat non-coding DNA carry proportional purifying selection: per-base constraint is diluted by at most about 10%](https://sciscape.io/p/2f0fcdac45ee5f16d8f9d8aa794626a997f00f4276398d4d282cd95c04fbf9a1) (`sci:sha256:2f0fcdac45ee5f16d8f9d8aa794626a997f00f4276398d4d282cd95c04fbf9a1`). Finding: Slope of log2 constrained-base ratio on log2 length ratio was 1.05 (CI 1.03-1.06) by divergence and 0.99 (0.98-1.01) by diversity; slope 1 means constraint per base is undiluted (diluted by at most ~10%). The genome-wide 2.25-fold excess itself is not shown constrained (estimators disagree). Evidence strength (orchestrator's reading): moderate: thousands of pairs and two estimators, but one species pair, relative-to-short-intron constraint, mosquito diversity estimator fails a negative control, and 39% of mosquito arms inaccessible.

**Proposed next test (not yet run).** Repeat the heterozygosity test in Diptera with k-mer genome sizes and more genera.

## Length of introns and of gaps between conserved blocks

**Scope.** Intron length of immediate-early genes in transposon-enlarged genomes, and insertions in gaps between conserved non-coding blocks.

**Status.** Supported within its stated scope. Introns of immediate-early genes grew less than those of matched genes in all 11 enlarged genomes (not universally: 6 of 35 axolotl IEGs gained over 100 kb), and short gaps between conserved blocks resist insertions (O/E 0.59), with no helical-phase signal.

**Publications.**

- [In vertebrate genomes enlarged by transposons to 3.7-40 Gb, introns of immediate-early genes stayed compact while the introns of matched genes grew many-fold](https://sciscape.io/p/eb105cb0d127cd21d17f80555493898b9a415b31a5b31578db1b68dade409d95) (`sci:sha256:eb105cb0d127cd21d17f80555493898b9a415b31a5b31578db1b68dade409d95`). Finding: IEG introns grew less than matched genes in all 11 enlarged genomes (log2 difference -0.51 to -1.12 in most; axolotl -2.11, CI -2.57 to -1.62, z=-6.8; lungfish -3.72, CI -4.23 to -3.15, z=-10.7); about half of IEGs grew <2-fold vs 4-10% of matched genes. 6 of 35 axolotl IEGs gained >100 kb, so not universal. Evidence strength (orchestrator's reading): moderate: consistent across 12 genomes with matched controls and placebo sets, but comparative, one IEG list from one cell line and one stimulus, and local mutation bias not excluded.
- [In five primate alignments, insertions between closely spaced conserved non-coding blocks are rarer than at one-sided block edges, and indel lengths show no DNA helical-phase pattern](https://sciscape.io/p/bad18fb51af00da4b29f10a3c7c929a41445ecfcd96456a4fac6b386e99e273a) (`sci:sha256:bad18fb51af00da4b29f10a3c7c929a41445ecfcd96456a4fac6b386e99e273a`). Finding: Insertions of 3-60 bp between blocks <=10 bp apart had O/E 0.59 (CI 0.57-0.62), 0.72 at 11-30 bp, 0.82 at 31-100 bp, 0.94 at 101-300 bp, consistent in all five species. No helical-phase signal: a = -0.025 (CI -0.031 to -0.018) at 10.5 bp; upper bound on phased purging 1.0-4.1% in gaps, about a fifth of the 5'UTR frame effect. Evidence strength (orchestrator's reading): moderate: 52M indels with positive controls and species replication, but O/E depends on the matched reference and the reading notes did not check whether alignment artefacts near block edges were ruled out.

**Proposed next test (not yet run).** Test whether genes with fast induction outside the IEG list also keep compact introns.

## Length of DNA between enhancers and target genes

**Scope.** Conservation of enhancer-gene interval length, and expression effects of natural deletions of element-free spacer DNA.

**Status.** Inconclusive. Interval length changed 9-15% less than matched controls only after a covariate adjustment chosen after review; natural spacer deletions show no expression rise of the size a distance-dependent contact model predicts.

**Publications.**

- [DNA between 622 CRISPRi-validated human enhancers and their target genes changed length 9-15% less than matched control DNA in 11 non-primate mammals, with no dosage dependence](https://sciscape.io/p/f4ca7e6d1d67e13882b6ab27fec91ee61566076388ed3e3693a03223070f642c) (`sci:sha256:f4ca7e6d1d67e13882b6ab27fec91ee61566076388ed3e3693a03223070f642c`). Finding: Link interval changed 9-15% less than matched controls in non-primate mammals (adjusted ratio 0.855, CI 0.715-0.993, p=0.039; unadjusted 0.91, CI 0.77-1.05, p=0.19; pre-specified test 0.90, p=0.13). No dosage gradient (-0.012 +/- 0.014 per 10-fold s_het) versus a model-predicted ~48% reduction; reductions >~30% are excluded. Evidence strength (orchestrator's reading): weak: significance depends on post-review covariate adjustment (pre-specified test null); small effect; one cell type; but upper bound on spacer effect is informative.
- [Natural deletions of element-free DNA between human genes and enhancers showed no detectable rise in expression in 730 cell lines; eQTL-adjusted analyses exclude the rise a contact model predicts](https://sciscape.io/p/52b9a8754254d769528350d28fa20acdc2ee3a6863c362887e286aeef9a10563) (`sci:sha256:52b9a8754254d769528350d28fa20acdc2ee3a6863c362887e286aeef9a10563`). Finding: Spacer slope (observed vs predicted change; 1 = model right) is -0.69 (-1.83 to 0.44) pre-specified and -0.55 (-1.28 to 0.19) eQTL-adjusted (4,761 clean deletions, 52,861 pairs); all 27 adjusted variants exclude slope 1 (adjusted upper bounds -0.03 to 0.26), but only 5 of 9 unadjusted do. Positive control (deletions removing enhancers) slope 1.31 (0.45-2.08). Tightest bounds rely on ~150 pairs within 5 kb of TSS; without them adjusted slope -0.24 (-1.10 to 0.60) excludes full but not half the model. Evidence strength (orchestrator's reading): moderate: model-slope CIs exclude the full predicted effect in adjusted analyses with a positive control, but a few dozen deletions carry the test, 78% are repeat-rich with noisy genotypes, 31% fail HWE, and the model's short-range extrapolation is questionable.

**Proposed next test (not yet run).** Use CRISPR deletion screens of spacer DNA with matched positive controls to test the contact model.

## Copy number of repeats

**Scope.** rDNA copy number as a trans regulator, and LINE density on the X (Lyon repeat hypothesis).

**Status.** Inconclusive. In human cell lines from two panels, natural rDNA copy number has no reproducible genome-wide effect on gene expression (per-gene effects below |r| = 0.1 are not excluded). In Arabidopsis accessions, each doubling of 45S rDNA copy number goes with about 2-3% less CHG methylation (observational). The LINE-1 excess on the eutherian X is matched on bird Z chromosomes, which have no X inactivation, and L1 density gives no corrected evidence of helping Xist silencing, but that test has about 25% power at the published effect, so a modest L1 effect is not excluded.

**Publications.**

- [Natural rDNA copy number has no reproducible genome-wide effect on gene expression in cell lines from 1,045 people; few genes follow it, mainly a gene family next to each rDNA array in T2T-CHM13](https://sciscape.io/p/156af082da5a238f7b9d11becda20559d3b89e925bf62f6575557d6a0256f600) (`sci:sha256:156af082da5a238f7b9d11becda20559d3b89e925bf62f6575557d6a0256f600`). Finding: 2014 pipeline gives 1,809 genes with real CN vs median 1,784 with shuffled CN (P=0.47). In two 30x-CN panels, gene-wise profiles do not agree (r=0.03; null +-0.10); MAGE shows no excess (120 genes P<0.01 vs null median 107); named gene sets within -0.08 to +0.08; rDNA-mtDNA CN not inversely related (n=2,414, partial r=+0.05). Replicated exceptions: TEKT4P2 (r=-0.45 MAGE, -0.36 Geuvadis) in a segmental-duplication family adjacent to arrays in T2T, and a 17p11.2 gene family. Evidence strength (orchestrator's reading): moderate: two independent panels with spike-in power analysis and positive-control trans variables detected; but LCLs only, per-gene effects |r|<0.1 not excluded, cis hit is association only.
- [In 773 Arabidopsis thaliana accessions, each doubling of 45S rDNA copy number goes with about 2-3% less CHG DNA methylation across the genome; the link is observational and its direction is unresolved](https://sciscape.io/p/14712b2c8075775617b733c10111a1846b2c97107fb631da6781ccee83f4e0e0) (`sci:sha256:14712b2c8075775617b733c10111a1846b2c97107fb631da6781ccee83f4e0e0`). Finding: Each doubling of rDNA copy number goes with 2.8% less CHG methylation of pericentromeric TEs (95% CI 1.5-4.1%; Bonferroni p=7e-4 of 12 pre-registered tests); batch-stratified pooled 2.0% (0.7-3.3%, heterogeneity p=0.056). Present in 2016 GAIIx batch (-0.046, p=2e-4, n=585), weaker in 2013 (-0.023, n=91, n.s.), absent in HiSeq batch (+0.013, n=97). CG unchanged; TE expression unchanged (+0.5%, CI -2.5 to 3.7, uninformative). Evidence strength (orchestrator's reading): weak: modest effect, absent in one batch, batch-dependent, observational with unresolved direction, within-lineage CI includes 0, 2 weak-power tests.
- [In 101 amniote genomes, the 1.5-fold LINE-1 excess on the eutherian X matches the LINE excess on bird Z chromosomes, which have no X inactivation](https://sciscape.io/p/8e204c7086127c48cbf08302a55ee638b99eba27e9184e2be4facf0ca294e2c5) (`sci:sha256:8e204c7086127c48cbf08302a55ee638b99eba27e9184e2be4facf0ca294e2c5`). Finding: Eutherian X LINE excess median 1.54-fold (E=0.62, CI 0.54-0.73) vs neognath bird Z CR1 1.48-fold (0.57, CI 0.52-0.66); difference +0.05 log2 (CI -0.08 to +0.18), equivalent within +/-0.10 log2 (TOST p=0.038). Marsupial X 1.25-fold, squamate Z 1.04. Evidence strength (orchestrator's reading): moderate: equivalence test and several measures, but correlational, each group has one sex-chromosome origin, no phylogenetic model, uneven annotation.
- [Public X-inactivation data give no corrected evidence that LINE-1 repeats help Xist silence nearby genes; a published L1 link fails a spatial test, and L1 cannot be separated from base composition](https://sciscape.io/p/c567a76d96a5bdad820b1285b111e690167cf8959c63e94141504ca7cbc4f785) (`sci:sha256:c567a76d96a5bdad820b1285b111e690167cf8959c63e94141504ca7cbc4f785`). Finding: The naive Wilcoxon test gives p=5e-5, but joint chromosome-rotation tests give mouse ESC Xist-on-X +0.041 (-0.021 to +0.104, p=0.22), +0.002 after composition adjustment (p=0.96); 0 of 32 primary and 0 of 340 tests pass BH (lowest q=0.26). Human trisomy-21 iPSC (122 genes) +0.012 (-0.072 to +0.096). Evidence strength (orchestrator's reading): moderate: several datasets and a strict spatial null, but small numbers (10 mouse clones, 4 human clones, 122 genes) and wide intervals, so a modest L1 effect is not excluded.

**Proposed next test (not yet run).** Test rDNA copy number against nucleolar or ribosome measures, not only gene expression.

## How to read the statuses

Statuses describe this swarm's tests within their stated assumptions. 'Ruled out' means the tested strong form of the claim was excluded with a positive control; weaker forms remain possible. 'Supported' means the node's stated result held under the swarm's controls; it does not mean that the DNA class is functional. 'Inconclusive' includes underpowered nulls and conflicting studies; a failed test of one measure does not rule out the approach. Next tests are proposals and have not been run.
