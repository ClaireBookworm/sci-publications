# Leukocyte composition entropy does not establish incremental elevated-CRP prediction across two NHANES samples

## Abstract and scoped claim
In 4,458 adults from the separately sampled NHANES 2001–2002 release, adding five-part leukocyte Shannon entropy to a 1999–2000-fitted WBC/NLR and demographic model changed weighted Brier error from 0.18325286 to 0.18326671. The gain was −0.00001385 (source-and-target PSU-bootstrap 95% interval −0.00126464 to 0.00070276); incremental predictive benefit was not established.

Development PSU-grouped cross-validation suggested a small gain, but it did not transfer to the separately sampled confirmation release. This is a negative incremental-prediction result, not proof of equivalence, an entropy biomarker validation, or evidence that CBCs cannot reflect inflammation.

## Question and scientific motivation
The primary NHANES study [Seasonal Variations of Complete Blood Count and Inflammatory Biomarkers](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4636256/) examined seasonal CBC/CRP patterns. [Howard et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annepidem.2019.07.015) documented demographic and lifestyle correlates of NLR. These motivate checking whether a whole-composition summary captures information not used by a WBC/NLR model, while controlling major demographic/body-size correlates. We hypothesized a positive out-of-sample Brier gain from five-part leukocyte entropy. The tested direction of predictive gain was fixed, not an entropy coefficient sign.

We do not assert that entropy is absent from all current analyses or that its use is universally novel. Entropy is a deterministic transformation of differential counts; it cannot add information conditional on their exact joint distribution. Any improvement here would be a representation/regularization benefit relative to specified comparators, not a new measurement. A stronger all-five-count comparator challenges this distinction.

## Features, outcome and baseline
Outcome is contemporaneous CRP≥0.3mg/dL =3mg/L. It is a nonspecific inflammation proxy, not persistent inflammation, a disease diagnosis or future risk. CBC and CRP are different assays, with no CRP-derived predictors. No new samples or experiments were collected.

Normalize the five percentages (neutrophils, lymphocytes, monocytes, eosinophils, basophils) to sum1, then calculate H=−Σp log(p), using natural logarithms and0log0=0. The recorded percentage sum must be within0.5 of100. Standardize H on the source and append one linear term. See [1999 CBC documentation](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/1999/DataFiles/LAB25.htm) and [2001 CBC documentation](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/2001/DataFiles/L25_B.htm).

Baseline: ridge logistic regression, C=1, with cubic spline bases for age, BMI, log(1+WBC), log(neutrophil%/lymphocyte%), four unweighted source-quantile knots, degree3, no bias spline, standardized bases; one-hot sex, race/ethnicity, and smoking (never/current/former/missing). Source WTMEC2YR weights are normalized to mean1. The baseline includes standard inflammatory measures WBC/NLR and common correlates, not a validated clinical risk score. Augmented model differs only by standardized H. Knots, scaling, category dictionaries and coefficients are fitted on source only. Target is never used for fitting or model selection. Reported `interaction_coef` is a legacy code field name for the entropy coefficient, not an interaction; it equals0.223989 per source SD conditional on the baseline.

Five-fold GroupKFold uses source stratum–PSU groups, with preprocessing refitted inside every fold. It does not hold out whole strata. Confirmation freezes the full-source model. Both models use max_iter2000,tol1e-8 and all fits assert convergence. No pretraining or hyperparameter search occurs. Primary metric: WTMEC2YR-weighted paired Brier baseline-minus-augmented error (positive better). AUROC and log loss are secondary.

Uncertainty uses1,000 target-only stratified rescaled PSU bootstrap draws, conditional on the fitted source model;300 source-and-target draws additionally refit preprocessing and both models. Within each stratum containing m PSUs, sample m−1 PSUs with replacement and multiply weights by m/(m−1). Percentile95% intervals are descriptive finite-bootstrap intervals, not equivalence bounds. Public masked clusters approximate the survey design; bootstrap estimates do not account for all possible cross-cycle geographic dependence. RNG seed72812. No outcome-based tuning follows confirmation.

## Limitations and future test
The modest development gain did not transfer; small benefit or harm remains compatible with uncertainty. These data cannot establish mechanism, clinical benefit, causality or long-term prognosis. Residual confounding includes medications, acute infection, season and underlying disease. Rounded differential percentages and one CBC/CRP per person limit reliability. Nonlinear interactions or other entropy encodings were not exhaustively tested. Separate survey cycles are weaker evidence of transport than another country or health system. Generalization beyond complete-case noninstitutionalized US adults or these assays is unsupported.

A future independent prospective clinical cohort could measure repeated CBC/CRP under standardized timing, record medications and acute illness, freeze these models in advance, and test repeated inflammation and clinical outcomes against flexible count-based comparators with decision-curve and calibration assessments. This proposal has not been performed. There is no basis here to replace CRP testing or change care.
