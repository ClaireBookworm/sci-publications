# Swing-fraction serial memory does not establish incremental Parkinson prediction across two public gait collections

## Abstract and scoped claim
In gaitpdb-to-gaitndd transfer (162 source IDs;31 target participants), adding lag-one swing-fraction memory increased logloss by 0.02814; joint bootstrap95% interval [-0.01081,0.10085]. Incremental benefit not established. External logloss was 0.36159 without and 0.38973 with memory; AUC was 0.92083 for both. The source-only cross-validation improvement did not transfer in the observed direction. The intervals do not establish harm, equivalence, or absence of information. This is a computational negative result about one fixed feature, model, and transfer, not a clinical diagnostic validation.

The question was chosen from primary literature and dataset documentation, not from the SCI catalog. Frenkel-Toledo and colleagues studied distinct stride/swing variability responses to speed [1]. We ask whether the *ordering* of swing fraction, not just its mean and variability, adds portable information about PD. This is not a claim that serial gait analysis or swing fraction is new, nor that all current analyses ignore ordering. An exhaustive novelty search was not performed. The contribution is a deliberately small, reproducible incremental external audit.

## Population, outcome and preprocessing
Statistical unit: one public participant ID. Outcome: PD versus healthy-control label, not severity, future falls, treatment response or a physiological mechanism. Of165 source files,162 passed quality rules (92PD,70controls); JuCo02,JuCo03,JuPt05 had fewer than30 accepted strides on at least one foot. All31 targets passed. Counts differ from the166 people in the source landing-page summary because only165 `_01` files were available. The source counts by constituent study are saved, not inferred from summary claims.

For source foot force, contact is total force>20N. Consecutive contact onsets define a stride; exactly one intervening contact offset is required. Swing runs from that offset to the next onset. No smoothing, target-trained thresholding, interpolation or closing across rejected cycles is used. Source Gender1 is interpreted as male,2 as female, consistent with the HTML metadata. Ragged trailing metadata tabs are ignored after the first14 documented fields. Target stride and swing durations are read from columns2/3 and4/5, with elapsed time from column1; percentage fields are not used.

We restrict each foot to elapsed times10–110s, stride0.5–2s, swing/stride0.15–0.60, finite measurements, and at least30 accepted strides. Time is the supplied elapsed time, not re-zeroed to the first derived row: some target files begin after10s and thus contribute shorter windows. Source timing is an onset timestamp whereas the exact target event convention is not independently verified; boundary alignment is a domain-shift limitation. Threshold10N/30N changes only source extraction, because raw target signals were intentionally not acquired.

For each foot compute mean stride, sample-SD/mean stride CV, mean swing fraction and its CV. Average each across feet. Static asymmetry is absolute difference of foot mean fractions divided by their average. Candidate memory is Pearson correlation between successive fractions, computed only when both original adjacent strides pass filters, then averaged across feet. Pairs are centered separately; insufficient/constant-pair series return0. No gap is converted into a false adjacency. This is a short-lag descriptive statistic, not a fractal exponent or dynamical mechanism.

## Prediction and uncertainty
Baseline: age,male,mean stride,stride CV,mean swing fraction,fraction CV,static asymmetry. These timing/variability controls reflect conventional summaries [1]; the comparison is not against all possible clinical assessments. Augmented model adds just memory. Both use source-fitted StandardScaler and L2 logistic regression,C=1,max_iter2000,otherwise scikit-learn defaults; no tuning, feature selection, class balancing or target recalibration. No target measurements or outcomes fit the model or preprocessing. One source-only stratified fivefold split (seed20261125) provides descriptive internal performance; it is not a second dataset.

Primary endpoint is target mean binary logloss difference (augmented−baseline); negative is better. Secondary AUC is descriptive. Both are participant-weighted. The primary target conditional interval uses2000 class-stratified participant bootstrap draws with fixed fitted models: [-0.00462,0.07017]. The joint interval refits both models on500 class-stratified source-ID resamples and evaluates independently class-stratified target resamples: [-0.01081,0.10085]. Percentile intervals condition on the observed case/control mix; they do not include collection-level or hidden-person-cluster uncertainty. All random draws use a fixed NumPy generator seed20261125. No multiplicity-adjusted significance or population clinical probability is asserted.

## Confounding, limitations and proposed next test
Target controls are substantially younger (mean39.31years) and mostly coded female (male fraction0.125), unlike target PD (mean66.80,male fraction0.667). Source controls/PD have mean ages63.54/66.22. Demographics alone attain target AUC0.9042: high full-model AUC is not evidence of disease-specific physiology. Age/sex adjustment cannot eliminate residual confounding or selection; target age>=45 sensitivity retains only20people and remains negative. Gait speed adjustment and gait-only comparison are imperfect checks, not causal identification. Medication, walking course, device, timing algorithms, disease severity and recruitment differ or are insufficiently harmonized. Source-force extraction has not been manually adjudicated against gold-standard events; synthetic tests cannot establish biological measurement validity. Rounding and source/target sensor resolution may alter short-lag correlation. No outcome precision exists at the stride level: strides are never independent test subjects.

This single prespecified candidate and fixed model did not establish unused, transferable PD information. We do not infer that ordering contains no information, or that a larger study would fail. The external assessment was untouched by *this project's* fitting/design before the initial run; it is not a prospective clinical study or globally sealed dataset. All sensitivities after that opening are exploratory and no additional untouched target remains.

A future independently recruited, age/sex/speed-balanced observational validation could predefine harmonized bilateral event detection, medication state and window duration; establish event-level reliability against annotated ground truth; and lock these two models before examining diagnosis or longitudinal falls. It could test whether memory predicts outcomes beyond routine summaries. We did not recruit, measure, intervene or perform that proposed study.
