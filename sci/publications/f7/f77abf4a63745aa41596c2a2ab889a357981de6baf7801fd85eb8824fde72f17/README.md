# Campaign: non-invasive brain measurement, coupled methods: one energy modulates another

Subcampaign of the campaign `sci:sha256:b8346ed353aa99d00b9d7b22e9e509e59ecd2745f211e0f790cccf564215b70d` (how can human brain activity be measured without surgery, better than today?).

## Subquestion and scope

Methods in which one form of energy marks or modulates another before detection: photoacoustics (light in, sound out), ultrasound-tagged light, acoustoelectric imaging, ultrasound-tagged electrical recording, and MR or ultrasound elastography.

## Status and main result

Status: confounds dominate, with one narrow gain. In simulated transcranial photoacoustics the signal is dominated by scalp (median scalp/brain ratio 56 in one calibrated model; 85-96% of apparent-brain voxel signal in another; cortical changes diluted to a few percent). With continuous ultrasound tagging 61-99% of tagged light comes from scalp, but gated pulsed tagging takes 33-72% from cortex under the beam. In a four-shell sphere model, scalp acoustoelectric imaging needs about 40 minutes to 9 hours of activity per 1.6 mm voxel (about tenfold uncertainty). Reported functional MR elastography effects need 13-35% intrinsic stiffening against an estimated 3.7-13%, and same-run voxel selection inflates means 1.6-5x.

## Open questions and proposed next tests (not yet run)

Measure scalp and brain photoacoustic contributions separately in a head with controlled scalp perfusion; test gated ultrasound-tagged light on a layered phantom with a cortical target.

Finding: Filters for 1.714 mm patterns have worst RMS error 0.061-0.123 across eight models; at 3 mm gap lower bounds 0.43-0.62; unchanged filters on other maps fail (44-185 units). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate - bounds rigorous but known-transfer condition severe.
- [Distinct time changes in four uncorrelated sources permit sound readout in three fixed elastic skull models with white noise](https://sciscape.io/p/840eaea64a6833846960c6282b430fe63b037d5b7b462e2c3050d43067408927) (`sci:sha256:840eaea64a6833846960c6282b430fe63b037d5b7b462e2c3050d43067408927`). Finding: 8.12% median worst-source waveform error, original method 27.9% failing prior 10% prediction. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak - simulated.

### Negative and null results

- [Scalp acoustoelectric imaging needs about 40 minutes to 9 hours of neural activity per 1.6 mm focus to detect typical human cortical currents at FDA intensity, within about tenfold](https://sciscape.io/p/99dcea1ef37e3041fe5e4574aed946dd33227c1d2e0f70b71b08e42805c3157f) (`sci:sha256:99dcea1ef37e3041fe5e4574aed946dd33227c1d2e0f70b71b08e42805c3157f`). Finding: Reciprocity/Johnson-noise derivation: SNR scales with time-averaged acoustic intensity; scalp electrodes reach only 4e-7 to 1e-5 of the electrode-free limit. Detection needs ~40 min (sulcal) to ~9 h (gyral) per 1.6 mm voxel at 0.72 W/cm2, K=0.1%/MPa, ~10-fold uncertainty; epidural 3-30 s; reported mouse sidebands exceed bulk AE by 2.6-360x. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): strong: first-principles limit, explicit uncertainty, tested against published claims.
- [Different passive materials give equal solid motion in uniform models with incompressible parts of equal density and unknown boundary loads and fluid flux](https://sciscape.io/p/4606bce395ab68827b96910fce713132b2bf1a2058c51612b8e30056d27fc932) (`sci:sha256:4606bce395ab68827b96910fce713132b2bf1a2058c51612b8e30056d27fc932`). Finding: Exact family of passive materials gives identical full solid-motion fields; solver agrees to 4e-10; a pressure-free two-field inverse recovers porosity (error 5e-10) when shear and compression are both present. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: rigorous but idealized, numerical-only check.
- [In a 2D head model, skull-reflected scalp signal outweighs cortex 20–55-fold in deep voxels of transcranial photoacoustic images, so small scalp blood changes could mimic reported brain responses](https://sciscape.io/p/a8f60969f343a2854b039777a70494454a166a8d05dd4536640b1cb03c8cca26) (`sci:sha256:a8f60969f343a2854b039777a70494454a166a8d05dd4536640b1cb03c8cca26`). Finding: Skull-reflected scalp signal outweighs cortex 20-55x in deep voxels; +0.83% voxel signal per +1% scalp blood rise at 1064 nm, so scalp changes could mimic brain responses (amplitude ratios uncertain x2-5). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: concrete mechanism and numbers, but 2D and no data.
- [In simulated adult heads with the published processing, scalp signal is much larger than brain signal in the cortical voxels of 1 MHz photoacoustic images through the skull](https://sciscape.io/p/d4a689f07f9925a3a51790208faf095fc889961517203ffe2683d2e32dc58bad) (`sci:sha256:d4a689f07f9925a3a51790208faf095fc889961517203ffe2683d2e32dc58bad`). Finding: Scalp/brain signal ratio 56 (median, 95% CI 41-66; 3.9-64 across voxel rules; 3D head 43); skull reduces cortical response 81-110x, so a 2.2% published response becomes ~0.02-0.03%; bone acoustics (echoes, loss, scattering) cause it. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): strong: Multiple controls (no-skull, acoustically transparent skull, 3D, independent wave-solver check, calibrated to measured skulls), though skull model is simplified.
- [Published functional MR elastography does not isolate a neural stiffness signal: the fast mouse study uses circular statistics, and human visual-cortex stiffening is within vascular and CSF mechanics](https://sciscape.io/p/b3a2c2b28f912821f90039ece2b9db34f31f0f0c07029f37428f4b0a7893f560) (`sci:sha256:b3a2c2b28f912821f90039ece2b9db34f31f0f0c07029f37428f4b0a7893f560`). Finding: No: the mouse ROI selection and test, replicated by Monte Carlo, reproduce reported means from noise alone (false positive 94-99% with equal per-animal noise, 36-78% with unequal); human visual-cortex stiffening is within vascular (2.6-7.2%) and CSF (median 1.3%) mechanics. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Strong circularity argument and consistent simulations, but without raw data and with partly confirmed predictions.
- [A shared gain left large errors between recorded short and long pulse sound fields through four human skulls](https://sciscape.io/p/88b8715e40fc3dd13bd5a47790e7bf2c59708eb1d4cd44ab745c81699566aff9) (`sci:sha256:88b8715e40fc3dd13bd5a47790e7bf2c59708eb1d4cd44ab745c81699566aff9`). Finding: No: with one fitted size and phase, RMS map error was 8.25-99.90% of the long-pulse map across 16 scan pairs; only one pair under 10%; water controls also had errors. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Uses real measured data but confounded by sensor and time differences.
- [Gain and delay leave large errors in five filtered adult skull phantom record pairs and in noise controls](https://sciscape.io/p/b9eb7a48e457454d53ad8555d799dd48bb36e43089aee9896ab420f5bc67784e) (`sci:sha256:b9eb7a48e457454d53ad8555d799dd48bb36e43089aee9896ab420f5bc67784e`). Finding: No: median lower bound on relative error 60.82-62.64% at 0.5 MHz across five configurations (32,000 bounds all above 24.47%); noise controls also large. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Uses measured data with noise controls but confounded.
- [Published functional MR elastography effect sizes lie inside the range that voxel selection alone produces, and if they are real they need a 10 to 32 percent change in cortex](https://sciscape.io/p/5188033146a8f1666b58823d0f3b33cf8974d7696673738e045a0a0b8bda504f) (`sci:sha256:5188033146a8f1666b58823d0f3b33cf8974d7696673738e045a0a0b8bda504f`). Finding: Published fMRE effect sizes lie inside the range produced by voxel selection alone; if real they need a 10 to 32 percent change in cortex. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Two reviewers; consistent with b3a2c2b2.
- [In a layered head model with sound measured behind four human skulls, the halo cuts the 1 MHz contrast advantage over 0.5 MHz to parity in two skulls and breaks 1 MHz in the third](https://sciscape.io/p/c569019fe6e7277a738f2abbfcaabac81a88afb77348bac355dd78032bc8ed92) (`sci:sha256:c569019fe6e7277a738f2abbfcaabac81a88afb77348bac355dd78032bc8ed92`). Finding: Parity in two skulls and 1 MHz fails in the third. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Public skull data.
- [In a 2D physics model of photoacoustic brain imaging through 5 mm of scalp and 7 mm of skull, scalp signal made up 85-96% of the brain-voxel signal and diluted cortical responses 26- to 240-fold](https://sciscape.io/p/8e08a98e8578aabf9e226272cef856d6031c992a2e9d249f4c4fac32c399e53f) (`sci:sha256:8e08a98e8578aabf9e226272cef856d6031c992a2e9d249f4c4fac32c399e53f`). Finding: With 5 mm scalp and 7 mm skull, scalp signal is 85-96% of the apparent-brain voxel signal (6-24x cortical signal), diluting cortical absorption 26-240x with negative weight in 32-65% of voxels; a 2% scalp change gives brain t of ~5-17. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): strong: Replicates the preprint pipeline end to end with several anatomy draws and checks; 2D limits remain.
- [At a 0.5 megahertz cutoff, reducing scan angles reversed skull and water prediction error order in one phantom pair](https://sciscape.io/p/00289115a18ce916908e6ea9ce4a1f05e65153d9d2d3857d5741eb9345aa9879) (`sci:sha256:00289115a18ce916908e6ea9ce4a1f05e65153d9d2d3857d5741eb9345aa9879`). Finding: At 200 of 400 angles errors were 17.84% with skull vs 19.58% without (skull lower in all five pairs); at 100 positions 21.68% vs 20.32%; at 40, 43.79% vs 35.56%, so error order reverses. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: single skull, prediction failed and result reversal.

### Methods, tools and resources

- [Separate wave records give exact shear stiffness confidence sets in incompressible models with known Gaussian noise](https://sciscape.io/p/5d685a9db0486dc9c0a3cd5507b15544142e7caefbcfb0c9de86b5542ad48544) (`sci:sha256:5d685a9db0486dc9c0a3cd5507b15544142e7caefbcfb0c9de86b5542ad48544`). Finding: Coverage 94.475-95.625% over 60,000 tests; local ellipsoid gave 68.75% in one condition. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Coverage tested.
- [Low added frequencies reduced slow shear stress uncertainty in three relaxation models at fixed modulus error](https://sciscape.io/p/4178c675cc7ce505fcf9c931e535afae5cd004e50c023335eb3d9a9181277a47) (`sci:sha256:4178c675cc7ce505fcf9c931e535afae5cd004e50c023335eb3d9a9181277a47`). Finding: 1.322 Hz reduced widths 4.89-6.27x. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Simulation only.
- [Storage and loss identify axes in uniform models with two fiber families and different response ratios](https://sciscape.io/p/6c9a7f56d3056cda9af656b8b8a861ecd4885ded112ace11e9f3f0ce790745a9) (`sci:sha256:6c9a7f56d3056cda9af656b8b8a861ecd4885ded112ace11e9f3f0ce790745a9`). Finding: Yes: contraction verified to 3.18e-15 over 196 cases; in a 55-degree crossing example 95th-percentile axis error 2.061 deg under assigned noise. - [Paired sound fields calibrate closed sound models up to one phase in assigned resistive layers](https://sciscape.io/p/2e3d8b9d3fa88b2b8cbc5225b04c2a079d0dd0af69ac7bbe44842bbdbe4b2f80) (`sci:sha256:2e3d8b9d3fa88b2b8cbc5225b04c2a079d0dd0af69ac7bbe44842bbdbe4b2f80`). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: noise costs very high, conditional.
