# BUN–filtration coupling does not establish incremental hemoglobin prediction in temporal NHANES validation

## Abstract and scoped claim
A log blood-urea-nitrogen (BUN) × estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) interaction did **not establish incremental contemporaneous hemoglobin prediction** beyond flexible individual kidney markers, their usual ratio, iron, albumin and demographic covariates. Source NHANES2011–2012 n=4,481; frozen target2013–2014 n=4,881. Weighted target MSE fell from0.986280 to0.986029 (g/dL)², a gain of0.000251 (95% joint source-refit/target survey-PSU bootstrap interval −0.002281 to0.003052). RMSE changed from0.993116 to0.992990g/dL; MAE slightly worsened from0.764459 to0.764544g/dL. The point gain is0.0255% of baseline error. This is an inconclusive incremental-prediction result, not equivalence, anemia diagnosis, prospective prediction, or a causal test.

## Motivation and prior specification
The [KNOW-CKD primary study](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0257305) examined BUN and anemia accounting for eGFR. It motivates asking whether the hemoglobin information in urea depends on filtration context beyond additive standard measures. We do not claim that a BUN–anemia association is novel or that absent clinical use of this exact product has been established. A [primary BUN/creatinine study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27658757/) motivates including that familiar ratio as a comparator. We did not access KNOW-CKD individual data or claim replication of its longitudinal CKD outcome; the present question is a narrower general-population representation test.

## Models and uncertainty
The baseline is ridge regression alpha1 with unpenalized intercept. Additive cubic B-splines,3 uniformly spaced knots, linear extrapolation, cover age, BMI, logBUN, logcreatinine, eGFR, log(BUN/creatinine), serum iron and albumin. Sex, race/ethnicity and smoking categories are one-hot encoded. Standard individual renal/anemia-related measures, not an established clinical prediction calculator, define this comparator.

Race-free eGFR follows the [2021 CKD-EPI equation](https://www.kidney.org/professionals/ckd-epi-creatinine-equation-2021):142×min(Scr/k,1)^a×max(Scr/k,1)^−1.2×0.9938^age×1.012(if female); k=.7/.9 and a=−.241/−.302 for female/male; Scr in mg/dL. Units mL/min/1.73m². This is an estimated renal measure, not measured clearance or a clinical CKD diagnosis.

Augmentation adds one product z_source(logBUN)×z_source(eGFR). All input-design columns are source-weight standardized after spline/encoding construction. Source weighted means/scales, knots and category encoders are learned only on each training fold/resample and frozen for target prediction. No target centering, outcome-informed transformation, threshold search or recalibration occurs. Primary success required baseline-minus-augmented target MSE gain positive with95% interval excluding0.

Seed181835;300 joint bootstrap iterations independently resample m−1PSUs with replacement within each stratum containing mPSUs, with multiplicity×m/(m−1). Both source models and transforms are refitted; target observations receive resampled PSU multipliers. The percentile interval includes source estimation and target survey variability, not model-choice, missingness or method-change uncertainty. Few strata and finite resamples make tail precision limited. Fivefold source CV groups entire PSUs; it is a source diagnostic, not the independent validation.

## Interpretation and limitations
We found no compelling incremental signal for this particular product over a strong additive baseline. We did not demonstrate absence of information in kidney physiology, equality of models or failure of the KNOW-CKD findings. The data are cross-sectional; hydration, protein intake, inflammation, menstrual loss, occult bleeding, iron stores, erythropoietin treatment, medication use, muscle mass and comorbid disease are incompletely controlled. Serum iron alone is not full iron status. Restricting eGFR and extreme kidney values challenges but does not eliminate confounding. Flexible eGFR and creatinine covariates are correlated; ridge and knot choices affect the representation. A single interaction does not exhaust nonlinear structure. No causality, mechanistic specificity or clinical utility follows from the association/prediction exercise.

A future preregistered clinical observational study could collect repeated BUN, measured renal function, hydration/protein-intake indicators and hemoglobin, with iron-status and therapy documentation, to test whether within-person urea–filtration coupling precedes hemoglobin change beyond standard measures. It should use an independently recruited cohort and frozen model and assess clinical decision benefit. That study was not performed; no new measurements, recruitment or physical experiments occurred here.
