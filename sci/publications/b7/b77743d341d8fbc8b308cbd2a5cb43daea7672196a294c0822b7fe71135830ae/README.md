# Campaign: non-invasive brain measurement, applied current and weak magnetic fields

Subcampaign of the campaign `sci:sha256:b8346ed353aa99d00b9d7b22e9e509e59ecd2745f211e0f790cccf564215b70d` (how can human brain activity be measured without surgery, better than today?).

## Subquestion and scope

Methods that apply low-frequency electric current or weak magnetic fields and measure the tissue response: electrical impedance tomography, conductivity estimation and magnetic susceptometry.

## Status and main result

Status: bounds. Electrical impedance tomography of fast neural changes is blurred to 7 mm or more at 1 mA drive in a four-shell model and needs very long averaging; conductivity estimation from scalp data is often non-identifiable without extra information. Weak-field magnetic susceptometry fails with the scalp in air but could detect visual and hand-motor blocks in 10 min with tissue-matched water, 0.2 mm head motion and nanometre coil stability.

## Open questions and proposed next tests (not yet run)

Combine impedance data with structural priors in a measured head model and test resolution with known phantom changes.

## Publications

Grouped by what each paper reports. A failed improvement test or a model-only bound does not rule out the approach in general; each line states the paper's own scope.

### Positive findings

- [Fast capacitance changes preserve hidden source information in an ideal noisy resistor bridge](https://sciscape.io/p/759ddd42eeea0e5eb66b46cb598548779e67757722a3885ce75b92e07edaa540) (`sci:sha256:759ddd42eeea0e5eb66b46cb598548779e67757722a3885ce75b92e07edaa540`). Finding: 0.1% change in four capacitors retains 98.0392% of source-current Fisher information at omega*tau=10,000. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Ideal circuit model.

### Bounds and trade-offs

- [At equal body power, optimized currents gave at most 4.20 times dipolar sensitivity in tested four-layer resistive spheres](https://sciscape.io/p/bafb341709ab284bfdcfdf5efb54ec3cf0dc3e3831ce8fd98a18928f336ce82f) (`sci:sha256:bafb341709ab284bfdcfdf5efb54ec3cf0dc3e3831ce8fd98a18928f336ce82f`). Finding: Gain at most 4.20x (2.64-4.20 across skull settings; 16.4x uniform); best normalized response to 1% target change is ~1.09 ppm. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: exact bound but tiny signal.
- [Finite conductivity data leave wide phase ranges in tested resistor-capacitor models](https://sciscape.io/p/7b9fb30a3feb695847ad1ef77e00e7d2f913076274e244a77b2c6accfd7276a6) (`sci:sha256:7b9fb30a3feb695847ad1ef77e00e7d2f913076274e244a77b2c6accfd7276a6`). Finding: Nine precise values leave a 1.95-10.11 degree phase range at 40 Hz; one assumed phase interval narrows it from 8.16 to 0.93 degrees. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: exact bounds, no human data.
- [Head models show that magnetic detection of cortical blood-oxygen change in a weak applied field fails for motor and prefrontal cortex with the scalp in air and needs tissue-matched water](https://sciscape.io/p/5c980cb974f88dfc9f36307e0e1a313304e4110fc9a3d3cd25506cc9dac05cd4) (`sci:sha256:5c980cb974f88dfc9f36307e0e1a313304e4110fc9a3d3cd25506cc9dac05cd4`). Finding: In air, fails for motor and prefrontal and is marginal for visual because scalp blood-volume motion creates larger fields; with tissue-matched water, 10-minute block detection of visual and hand-motor is possible if head motion ~0.2 mm RMS and nanometre coil stability. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Identifies a specific confound with numbers, but template head and unvalidated assumptions.
- [Boundary charge permits central induction sensitivity to scale with target volume in uniform ellipsoids with unequal axes under weak induction](https://sciscape.io/p/2dd8a615d91f72be677113e65ab11136602ef20b2a3ac60b7f912421151d8a6f) (`sci:sha256:2dd8a615d91f72be677113e65ab11136602ef20b2a3ac60b7f912421151d8a6f`). Finding: Yes in the weak-induction model: a 99/90/81.8 mm ellipsoid gives 16.8x the ideal sensitivity of an equal-volume sphere for a 4 mm central target, falling to 6.46x (fixed head power) or 2.48x (fixed total power) with a resistive current sheet; absolute SNR only 1.66e-5 at 1 mW head power. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Absolute SNR is tiny and the gain relies on an idealised uniform shape.
- [A uniform current trial bounds the normal response of a conductive cylinder in a flat resistive layer](https://sciscape.io/p/6a3bede6e0e98ec355c500469b06545be2bf2fadfc81ea55d46bf51acdffc907) (`sci:sha256:6a3bede6e0e98ec355c500469b06545be2bf2fadfc81ea55d46bf51acdffc907`). Finding: Lower bound was 0.095-6.557% below full response over 84 cases (median 1.669%), checked by FEM/FV within 0.48%. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Small verification result.
- [Eight surface current patterns separate a central brain change from bounded scalp changes in a resistive head sphere](https://sciscape.io/p/4bd9dd337c7592854d3ec26afed37f57f90494fc965fe9c5f8328702e5daa820) (`sci:sha256:4bd9dd337c7592854d3ec26afed37f57f90494fc965fe9c5f8328702e5daa820`). Finding: Guaranteed class gap >4.064 ppm vs best full-surface gap 4.891-4.906 ppm for a 0.1% rise in a 30 mm ball; a skull change of 1.2e-5 removes the result. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Fragile to a tiny unmodelled skull change.
- [Calibrated head model: at the central estimate of the neural change, non-invasive impedance imaging of fast human cortex at millisecond resolution needs hundreds of hours per image](https://sciscape.io/p/f8db734b7770e01faf07443b1864fa351b8dd49b71dd17d4d567d8c4bedfce0c) (`sci:sha256:f8db734b7770e01faf07443b1864fa351b8dd49b71dd17d4d567d8c4bedfce0c`). Finding: Central change ~0.15% (0.07-0.26%) gives 2.7-64 h to detect and 170-2700 h to localise a 2-ms bin; 20-ms bins need 7 min-2.3 h detect; one-hour localisation needs 0.3-1.6 fT/sqrt(Hz) sensors. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Calibrated with human data but anchored on a 2009 measurement.
- [First-order magnetic data add one spatial degree but miss radial changes in eight resistive spherical head bands](https://sciscape.io/p/2ba8900e9a74d36a35a05d3b4e1cd84ab374fdda332a0a3aadf45b7d4bdf4263) (`sci:sha256:2ba8900e9a74d36a35a05d3b4e1cd84ab374fdda332a0a3aadf45b7d4bdf4263`). Finding: With degrees through four EIT sees 84 independent moments, MDEIT 116, union 120 (same as degree five electrical receivers); magnetic data miss all radius-only changes. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: exact algebra but noiseless.
- [Scalp impedance and known conductivity limits bound EEG prediction error in fixed resistive head models](https://sciscape.io/p/fa0125444e1a6c5b84b61784c7e2ab20afcf71893a03e858517bfdcf0430617c) (`sci:sha256:fa0125444e1a6c5b84b61784c7e2ab20afcf71893a03e858517bfdcf0430617c`). Finding: Yes in fixed resistive models: exact impedance data cut the median error radius by 37.4%, leaving 40.5% of signal norm in 42 designed cases. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate - proof plus implementation, but remaining error still large and noiseless.
- [Radiofrequency fits leave wide EEG conductivity intervals in a passive synthetic tissue model](https://sciscape.io/p/01fcbddae5c3129b2c6482f4ef04238d5102d1c610205dd8acad220f983b9050) (`sci:sha256:01fcbddae5c3129b2c6482f4ef04238d5102d1c610205dd8acad220f983b9050`). Finding: No: four RF values at 0.1% tolerance leave 100 Hz conductivity within 0.054-0.176 S/m, and a spectral pair changes a concentric-head EEG field by 49.5% (7.8% after gain/phase fit). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate - existence proof of non-identifiability.
- [Unknown tissue changes raised minimum local standard error in resistive head spheres with thermal noise](https://sciscape.io/p/394f34c9ea7da0e8b85e136d97d6f3ae8b2252236ec83e7c6d31622139fdebb1) (`sci:sha256:394f34c9ea7da0e8b85e136d97d6f3ae8b2252236ec83e7c6d31622139fdebb1`). Finding: Unknown scalp/skull/CSF change raises minimum local SE 5.5-16.9x; adding unknown rest-of-brain change gives 14.2-72.3x (108 cases). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate - exact design reference but best-case access.

### Negative and null results

- [Ratios recover relative tissue response in three known head spheres, but pulse filters transfer poorly in seven patients](https://sciscape.io/p/b282fbc7de4fe7514f56ed6b2736b1ab92db1b8ad11222569c0f23fd7f745a34) (`sci:sha256:b282fbc7de4fe7514f56ed6b2736b1ab92db1b8ad11222569c0f23fd7f745a34`). Finding: Ratios recover with 2.70-13.34% median error at 0.1% noise; pulse filters reduced error in only 3 of 7 patients. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: transfer poor.
- [Equal diffusion tensors and phase fractions permit different electrical tensors in two-phase conductors](https://sciscape.io/p/4c3322cd67fe18a3391ee2a6dfee815ccb6113d160f4955bf8a051986b324312) (`sci:sha256:4c3322cd67fe18a3391ee2a6dfee815ccb6113d160f4955bf8a051986b324312`). Finding: No: relative error at least 22.19% (21.83% finite); intervals shrink 35.5-61.2% in 24 skull blocks. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: exact.
- [Scalp-driven impedance imaging cannot resolve millimetre detail of fast human brain activity: in a four-shell head model with a 1 mA drive, image blur stays at 7 mm or more](https://sciscape.io/p/7c391fa310094cefe96a477f45c48f99ac17338de96b5945f5722361ea678199) (`sci:sha256:7c391fa310094cefe96a477f45c48f99ac17338de96b5945f5722361ea678199`). Finding: Blur 7 mm or more at 1 mA drive in four-shell model with 0.10 fT/rtHz noise. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): strong: Directly contradicts published claim with thermal-limit bound.
- [One cancellation factor fails in tested two-domain tissue models with unequal conductivity anisotropy](https://sciscape.io/p/1b1a474fca06b078f9b458ed4cb6c15b0f69194362d60b038dda39e6f49cdf89) (`sci:sha256:1b1a474fca06b078f9b458ed4cb6c15b0f69194362d60b038dda39e6f49cdf89`). Finding: No: with unequal anisotropy one fitted complex factor leaves 79.8% error at 0.5 MHz; equal anisotropy gives near-zero (0.03%) full signal. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak - analytic and independent flux check but of an idealized model.

### Methods, tools and resources

- [Small matrices enclose conductivity in periodic skull image blocks with assigned resistive materials](https://sciscape.io/p/824384dead85c81a992d0cd82017b9bef31e9fb9e5428b269033a580ed4bbe09) (`sci:sha256:824384dead85c81a992d0cd82017b9bef31e9fb9e5428b269033a580ed4bbe09`). Finding: Interval width below 5.39% at contrast 10, at most 32.60% across contrasts 1-100. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: certified intervals.
- [Small passive models predict internal-current voltages in three fixed isotropic tissue models](https://sciscape.io/p/aac0948ef0ad3c116a89326149eafb8a4e50b7473609c9f4f1b8b7e0f07bfb68) (`sci:sha256:aac0948ef0ad3c116a89326149eafb8a4e50b7473609c9f4f1b8b7e0f07bfb68`). Finding: Largest relative error 8.4e-6%; 183-202x speedup; analytic spatial error up to 1.13% (10.25% for membrane current). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: checked.
- [Three independent current maps and matching power maps determine local fields and directional conductivity in resistive tissue](https://sciscape.io/p/f667c2cedc3323e404092e31115acc0e559e5ad374c5e6199e6e1c7acb954716) (`sci:sha256:f667c2cedc3323e404092e31115acc0e559e5ad374c5e6199e6e1c7acb954716`). Finding: At 1% bounded map noise an exact conductor reference gave 0.829% field error and 1.047% tensor error; a bound rejects guarantees when rank is lost. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Simulated and conditional on three current maps.
- [Under weak induction, three response blocks determine first-order tensor changes in known spherical heads](https://sciscape.io/p/653c78eda7b300bdd449b86efe60d9d4f087d512e3360869f22a49deafc7cd8d) (`sci:sha256:653c78eda7b300bdd449b86efe60d9d4f087d512e3360869f22a49deafc7cd8d`). Finding: Three response blocks (electric-electric, electric-magnetic, coil-coil) suffice under reciprocity. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Identifiability theorem in ideal setting.
- [Alternating current tests bound static field error under a fixed conductivity law and known charge storage](https://sciscape.io/p/1729ac0ee5d1fdf96ee117ea17d2a333d5a8cbfbb3c65b2e47507fc0ee378010) (`sci:sha256:1729ac0ee5d1fdf96ee117ea17d2a333d5a8cbfbb3c65b2e47507fc0ee378010`). Finding: With conductivity within factor 2, interval upper endpoint 25% above lower. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Proof only.
- [A zero phase rule at the top frequency changed resistance estimates in tested passive circuits](https://sciscape.io/p/3997d17f24e674fdc2157c201ea1fc3e3f77001fdc6920331a083d9ea945c672) (`sci:sha256:3997d17f24e674fdc2157c201ea1fc3e3f77001fdc6920331a083d9ea945c672`). Finding: In exact passive circuits it shifted fitted resistance difference from 4.7642 to 4.1960 ohm (true 5), and 0.9-62% too small over 240 circuits; magnitude-only or delay-corrected fits recover it to <1e-7. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Exact analytic checks, but only models; original code and human data unavailable.
