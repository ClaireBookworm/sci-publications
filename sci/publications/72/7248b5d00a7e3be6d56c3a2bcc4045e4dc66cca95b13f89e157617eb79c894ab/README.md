# Campaign: connectomes, cell types, circuits and repeated copies

Subcampaign of the campaign `sci:sha256:c6fef5a19cfdc10222ff79abeab6bc3903cb22958f27a5f1b79d3a0b96ff8700` (what do connectomes reveal about how nervous systems are built?).

## Subquestion and scope

Groups of neurons: cell-type wiring, motifs, and how similar repeated copies are (left-right, serial homologs, columns, individuals).

## Status and main result

Status: supported for repeated copies, mixed elsewhere. Serial homologs in the fly nerve cord and in Platynereis are much less similar than left-right copies, mostly through partner types present in one segment only; mushroom-body glomerulus-pair structure repeats in all 21 hemisphere pairs; inhibitory outputs go mostly to same-level targets. Lineage and birth time add little after neuropil overlap: a raw 19.6-fold same-clone excess vanishes with control and reverses at 2 um. Fly wide-field visual neurons of one type share their field and partners (76 of 100 groups) and feed back to their own input types; function was not tested.

## Open questions and proposed next tests (not yet run)

Test serial-homolog differences in a second nerve-cord connectome with matched proofreading; record wide-field neuron dendritic compartments to test whether shared-field cells compute separately.

## Publications

Grouped by what each paper reports. A failed improvement test or a model-only bound does not rule out the approach in general; each line states the paper's own scope.

### Positive findings

- [In 4,300 FlyWire left-right neuron type pairs, a count model with an absent-side term found all-or-none absences 12 times more often where local arbors differed over 10-fold; balanced arbors held 45%](https://sciscape.io/p/7610446b065494a807ae6917207e25309f5d4640d569f7c4adfcf6cff41dd085) (`sci:sha256:7610446b065494a807ae6917207e25309f5d4640d569f7c4adfcf6cff41dd085`). Finding: One-sided pairs rise from 3.7% to 45% as local arbor ratio goes from <1.5 to >10; ~2,456 all-or-none absences (CI 2,162-2,780), 12x higher rate in most unbalanced stratum, but 45% of absences still occur in balanced arbors. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Clear dose-response across strata, but absence term is model-dependent and biology vs reconstruction is inseparable.
- [In the FlyWire brain, 4% of GABA and 9% of glutamate output synapses went forward in a hierarchy fitted on held-out cholinergic wiring, against 33% for acetylcholine (pairs with 5 or more synapses)](https://sciscape.io/p/b5bd2f8330678be25dbdd3f94cee424a03b62a518b3f5bef7866ed8e4f2543c4) (`sci:sha256:b5bd2f8330678be25dbdd3f94cee424a03b62a518b3f5bef7866ed8e4f2543c4`). Finding: Only 4.1% (GABA) and 8.5% (Glu) of held-out output synapses go forward vs 33.1% for ACh; most inhibitory output goes to same-level targets; holds in 8 variants, both hemispheres, 10 neuropil groups (GABA); positive control on visual projection/centrifugal neurons passes. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Consistent across variants with held-out design and positive control, but second hierarchy measure fails the control and transmitter labels are predictions.
- [In male and female fly nerve cord connectomes, serial T1–T3 copies of neurons had 1.5–2 times the input difference of left–right copies, and these segment differences repeated between the two flies](https://sciscape.io/p/4be25677999ecfabb5dc5e36b5c4ff2732c8c2638702d1a688d22d34c2f7daae) (`sci:sha256:4be25677999ecfabb5dc5e36b5c4ff2732c8c2638702d1a688d22d34c2f7daae`). Finding: Serial copies are less similar: input TV 0.60 vs 0.31 (MANC; 452/453 sets) and 0.65 vs 0.45 (BANC); mostly partner types present in one segment only; segment differences repeat across sides (r 0.63-0.66) and between flies (r 0.56-0.64, 0.84-0.92 corrected). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): strong: Replicated across sides and two animals with null and label controls, effect in 99% of sets.
- [In four Drosophila medullas from two connectomes, most column-to-column variance in single-connection synapse counts was specific to one eye; a map shared by both flies explained a median 4–8%](https://sciscape.io/p/71fcc009c08684bb979476833e7531291e22ea07a7407d9dfc33aad65b394b62) (`sci:sha256:71fcc009c08684bb979476833e7531291e22ea07a7407d9dfc33aad65b394b62`). Finding: A shared map explains median 4-8% across flies (14.5-20.5% same eye); column-level deviations do not repeat between eyes (r 0.01-0.05) though they agree between synapse tables (r 0.88); shared field is lower at eye edge. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Two connectomes and two synapse tables, but shared images/proofreading mean agreement is not proof of biology, and alignment approximate.
- [In 3,352 medulla columns of two fly connectomes, a column gain that fell at the eye edge and cell factors gave 55–73% of within-column synapse count variance; inputs to a cell did not trade off](https://sciscape.io/p/c486036e825cbbd0066a2df6ad8038bd9edb34ff1154650fa224c2ea85d4349a) (`sci:sha256:c486036e825cbbd0066a2df6ad8038bd9edb34ff1154650fa224c2ea85d4349a`). Finding: Column gain gives 33-42% and cell factors a further 15-35%, leaving connection-specific 10-17% beyond Poisson (upper limits 12-20%); gain falls at eye edge and maps agree between eyes (r 0.55-0.86); inputs to a cell do not trade off. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Covariance decomposition checked by simulation with block bootstrap, in four eyes, but only 12 types/30 connections.
- [Glomerulus pairs that shared Kenyon cells more or less often than degree-matched random wiring within KC subtypes repeated in all 21 pairs of 7 fly mushroom-body hemispheres from 4 connectomes](https://sciscape.io/p/9f3da37036e784e224c84f81dd8ef4542a7c18a292626925d2367f88071c2f7f) (`sci:sha256:9f3da37036e784e224c84f81dd8ef4542a7c18a292626925d2367f88071c2f7f`). Finding: Glomerulus-pair co-innervation z-scores agree in all 21 hemisphere pairs (r 0.29-0.43, null <=0.06), about 75% of non-random variance shared; bouton-field overlap predicts z (r 0.64) but agreement persists after removing it (r 0.13-0.30); food-odour glomerulus core. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): strong: Replicated in 21 pairs across 4 connectomes with degree-preserving null and held-out top-pair test.
- [In one Platynereis larva connectome, serial copies of neuron types had partner cosine 0.18–0.20 against 0.46–0.48 for left–right copies; without partner position the gap fell to 0.08–0.14](https://sciscape.io/p/b23bee36d8f94330879bbb74a0e5b1cb3ad2c0b433decb738ad9552ebe8baac7) (`sci:sha256:b23bee36d8f94330879bbb74a0e5b1cb3ad2c0b433decb738ad9552ebe8baac7`). Finding: Serial copy partner cosine 0.18-0.20 vs 0.46-0.48 for left-right copies (16/16 input types, 21/24 output types); gap shrinks to 0.08-0.14 without partner position; counting-noise ceiling 0.67-0.80. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Consistent across types with noise ceiling, but 32 types, few synapses per neuron (median 12-14) and one larva.
- [In 2,229 mouse projection neurons from two atlases, neuron fraction and axon per reaching neuron each set about half of target-to-target axon variance; only neuron fraction decreased with distance](https://sciscape.io/p/64d3d705ed307a54573dc51b0af4740ea7fe257c94cabb68f28b48e245bc2ef7) (`sci:sha256:64d3d705ed307a54573dc51b0af4740ea7fe257c94cabb68f28b48e245bc2ef7`). Finding: Each factor sets about half of the variance (beta_p 0.54 [0.50-0.58] and 0.63 [0.57-0.68]); only neuron fraction decreases with distance (-0.133 per mm). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Two atlases agree, but reconstructed neurons are selected samples and groups differ.
- [In three fly connectomes, an extra neuron of a cell type added about 80-90% of a typical neuron's synapses where label swaps were unlikely; pooled estimates near 100% were inflated](https://sciscape.io/p/846126f487c5fe9c83e63983544ac697d61be1a247997458710261ef3fd3fbaf) (`sci:sha256:846126f487c5fe9c83e63983544ac697d61be1a247997458710261ef3fd3fbaf`). Finding: Elasticity b of 0.91-1.01 overall, but label swaps also give b=1; where swaps were unlikely an extra neuron added ~80-90% of a typical neuron's synapses. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Three connectomes plus synthetic label-swap control, but control cannot model partial or merged neurons.
- [Partner profiles supported most tentative cell names in the C. elegans male N930 nerve ring, but not the two neurites named RIB that carry all 32 nerve-ring RIA→RIB records](https://sciscape.io/p/c5aa579d941e046089712817f85bf404167f60a74160888fd6ef6f90c1ffca13) (`sci:sha256:c5aa579d941e046089712817f85bf404167f60a74160888fd6ef6f90c1ffca13`). Finding: 25.1% of N930 postsynaptic name slots are tentative vs 0-0.47% in hermaphrodites; partner profiles support most tentative names but not the two neurites named RIB carrying all 32 RIA to RIB nerve-ring records. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Independent evidence (partners) and hermaphrodite rank baseline, but sex/series/annotation confounded.
- [With predicted transmitters, neurons of one fly cell type had a more constant inhibitory input share than matched random input sets in two connectomes, but left and right type totals did not](https://sciscape.io/p/a64cf0f5be0228dbfe4889c6c4b380bd2e1bff874db1ce907a6fae44af2cdec7) (`sci:sha256:a64cf0f5be0228dbfe4889c6c4b380bd2e1bff874db1ce907a6fae44af2cdec7`). Finding: Yes within one type on one side: mean percentile 0.375 (0.363-0.388; 1,939 groups, 52,027 neurons) in FlyWire and 0.411 (0.390-0.430) in hemibrain, robust to 15 variants; about 22% lower variance than null. Left-right type totals did not show it (0.494, 0.488-0.501). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Two connectomes, many robustness checks and null calibration; but predicted transmitters and connectivity-defined types could still inflate it.
- [In three fly nerve cord connectomes, leg-segment serial homologs had input wiring cosine 0.49–0.54 against 0.74–0.88 for left–right pairs; the segment differences repeated between animals](https://sciscape.io/p/290bdbfbd7d5831e9cb925389d80fb1cf7fbb5ca6536575e37dfc89cbb2c2175) (`sci:sha256:290bdbfbd7d5831e9cb925389d80fb1cf7fbb5ca6536575e37dfc89cbb2c2175`). Finding: Input cosine for serial homologs was 0.49-0.54 vs 0.74-0.88 for left-right pairs (serial dissimilarity 3.9x LR in males, 2.5x between-animal); segment effect shared across animals explained 66% of within-set variance, T1 most different. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): strong: Three connectomes, complete serial sets and a factorial split showing repeatability; though cell-type matching may overstate similarity.
- [In two male fly nerve cords, partner neuron numbers gave 1–6% of the leg-neuromere input contrast of serial cell types; absent partners and repeatable per-neuron synapse shares gave the rest](https://sciscape.io/p/f06da18b851a04bebd68f29f6495ad650261721fe5d2a01d74816c87e844e4e7) (`sci:sha256:f06da18b851a04bebd68f29f6495ad650261721fe5d2a01d74816c87e844e4e7`). Finding: Partner neuron number explained only 0.8-1.9% of the squared log2 contrast (count model gave 1-6% of contrast), absent partners 41-42% of TVD, and per-neuron synapse shares the remaining 58-59%, repeating between animals (r 0.83-0.88 vs 0.11 left-right). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Two animals and several label schemes, but both males and annotation partly connectivity-derived.
- [In one Platynereis larva connectome, serially repeated trunk cell types had less similar wiring than left–right pairs, and their segment differences were the same on both body sides](https://sciscape.io/p/50c9bf651e4cc08b5482b4cc5cf4941f7ff5a4f0dba3755ae8848d8b0ef3ae7b) (`sci:sha256:50c9bf651e4cc08b5482b4cc5cf4941f7ff5a4f0dba3755ae8848d8b0ef3ae7b`). Finding: Noise-corrected input cosine was 0.76 (0.71-0.82) for left-right vs 0.45 (0.32-0.57) for serial; segment effects shared by both sides explained 58-66% of variance (permutation p=0.0002). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Noise-corrected and several robustness variants; but one larva and low synapse counts.
- [Fixing each neuron's number of targets removed 26–35% of Fisher-test co-projection motifs and added 53 exclusive pairs in 30,433 MAPseq and 1,444 traced mouse neurons](https://sciscape.io/p/ed869ce2c81308828ac0a03131d13d3a9f6bfad06c34dee143c4166213801a15) (`sci:sha256:ed869ce2c81308828ac0a03131d13d3a9f6bfad06c34dee143c4166213801a15`). Finding: Fixed-breadth null removed 26-35% of Fisher over-representation calls (kept 180/276 and 29/39), made no new over calls and added 41 and 12 exclusive pairs; breadth multiplied expected co-projection by 0.89-1.58. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Proper constrained null with null-matrix validation; but conservative and tested on sparse traced samples.
- [Exact bouton degrees with kept dendrite lengths gave 75–79% of the excess granule cell input sharing in one mouse cerebellum connectome; the residual was largest in cell pairs under 10 µm apart](https://sciscape.io/p/68f8a4f48bc9333da8186b3d52103a5be4fcadddde278f4b1a2a906017c9c456) (`sci:sha256:68f8a4f48bc9333da8186b3d52103a5be4fcadddde278f4b1a2a906017c9c456`). Finding: Exact bouton-degree null gave 4.06 (3.70-4.45) vs observed 4.43 partners, closing 75-79% of the oversharing gap; a residual remained in the larger region (3.92 vs 3.52, 0/200 null) and concentrated in pairs with somata <10 um apart. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Reproduces published value and uses MCMC with convergence checks; but one animal, one lobule and edge effects.
- [In the male fly connectome, cells of most visual types with two or more cells that each cover half of one eye or more share the field and partner types; cells that cover less mostly divide the eye](https://sciscape.io/p/2dd9d5f8e0e99e494dbf8e669565566361775cb42ce43f761fa18a28bfb49909) (`sci:sha256:2dd9d5f8e0e99e494dbf8e669565566361775cb42ce43f761fa18a28bfb49909`). Finding: Of 100 wide-field groups (52 types, 294 cells), 76 shared the field, 19 partly split, 5 split; same-type partner differences median 4% vs 96% for different types; cells of coverage 0.2-0.5 mostly divide the eye (120/160); 53/100 groups redundant. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Preregistered rule, two connectomes, left-right replication; but types are partly defined by connectivity and prior prediction was wrong.
- [In two fly connectomes, wide-field visual neurons send local outputs to their own input cell types at stable type-specific rates, which nearby synapse sites explain only in part](https://sciscape.io/p/52250f762461379e9a07059a8c42a750e0d8174454d215338f9b2aef492af2ec) (`sci:sha256:52250f762461379e9a07059a8c42a750e0d8174454d215338f9b2aef492af2ec`). Finding: Median feedback preference W=1.89 (1.63-2.28) vs same-layer sites, 0.78 vs other wide-field inputs; persistent at 2 um in FlyWire (type preferences retained, Spearman 0.96 with 10 um); left-right Spearman 0.98, male vs FlyWire 0.74. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Two connectomes and robust reference variants; but the preregistered measure C showed no preference and effect weakens at local scale.

### Bounds and trade-offs

- [In one human (H01) and one mouse (MICrONS) cortex volume, the layer 2/3 share of synapses from inhibitory axons onto interneurons was 1.3–4.6 times higher in human, not 8.6–10 times](https://sciscape.io/p/d493bbff507f0d8a89269dc4765ea11f3783ce7d3cd6860d93678138697bf925) (`sci:sha256:d493bbff507f0d8a89269dc4765ea11f3783ce7d3cd6860d93678138697bf925`). Finding: Layer 2/3 ratio was 1.3-1.5 with observed arbors (a lower bound), and 3.5-4.6 within 50 um of soma; at most 5.4 after detection correction. Most of the increase came from more interneurons (34.9% vs 9.5%), not inhibitory selectivity; per-synapse E/I labels can inflate preferences (odds ratio 17). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Directly re-tests a published number in the two largest volumes with explicit bounds; but H01 interneuron dendrites are truncated and one volume per species.
- [At five contacts per channel, a model without internal axon-to-dendrite transfer kept 99.74% of two-edge reachability in one larval fly graph](https://sciscape.io/p/b0742ac548aa60b6ef2f19e403a55e21f4141ff2f35f101b5d30a1fb1bf8788e) (`sci:sha256:b0742ac548aa60b6ef2f19e403a55e21f4141ff2f35f101b5d30a1fb1bf8788e`). Finding: At five contacts per connection, blocking axon-to-dendrite transfer kept 99.74% of two-edge reachable pairs (172,654/173,101); 1,637 of 241,618 two-edge sequences needed it, 55.1% of them via Kenyon cells. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Exact counts on one graph but a descriptive graph benchmark that does not measure transmission.

### Negative and null results

- [Beyond hemilineage and neuropil, neurons with the same annotated birth time shared only 1.4–4.5% more synapses in two fly nerve cords (MANC, BANC)](https://sciscape.io/p/4fb484ff27df614ad0a886e3a93f20bd4c405654d816519c69539cdbabad6a1c) (`sci:sha256:4fb484ff27df614ad0a886e3a93f20bd4c405654d816519c69539cdbabad6a1c`). Finding: Same-birth-time excess is small: 1.4% (MANC) and 2.0% (BANC) with soma segment fixed (4.5%/3.1% otherwise); hemilineage carries 130-170x more partner information; early-born neurons are hubs but labels correlate with size. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Two animals agree, but birth-time labels are partly inferred from soma/neurite size, confounded with degree.
- [Cell lineage predicted neurite contact but gave no clear synapse prediction gain after contact area in nine C. elegans nerve-ring connectomes from birth to adult](https://sciscape.io/p/53d84c3bb448aeb8203a02d1bbe7f402996be30e651f506079b20723319a5428) (`sci:sha256:53d84c3bb448aeb8203a02d1bbe7f402996be30e651f506079b20723319a5428`). Finding: Lineage predicts neurite contact (OR 1.11 per shared division, p=0.002, all 9 animals) but synapse OR falls to 1.04 (0.98-1.10, p=0.18) after contact; class-level R2 gain is -0.09 to +0.20 points, inside the null. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Permutation nulls and planted-effect power check (94-98%), but only 40 crossed L/R pairs give within-class lineage variation.
- [In 472 mouse tracer injections, shared MERFISH neuron types of two sites predicted output similarity as well as CCF anatomy, but added at most 0.03 R² to it, about as much as non-neuronal cells](https://sciscape.io/p/9e84e540ec08b40de06d533adef0e8789e1a8d7782c5f3b28724fcb2492197c1) (`sci:sha256:9e84e540ec08b40de06d533adef0e8789e1a8d7782c5f3b28724fcb2492197c1`). Finding: Shared neuron-type fraction predicts output similarity (R2 +0.061, CI 0.013-0.110), same as CCF anatomy (+0.071) and about as much as non-neuronal cells placebo, with at most 0.03 R2 added beyond CCF. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Spatial jackknife and placebo control, but different mice registered to CCF and wild-type injections hit all types.
- [In the FlyWire brain, different-type neurons from one hemilineage clone connected (≥5 synapses) 0.66 times as often as unrelated neurons with equal 2 µm overlap; Kenyon cells, 3 times as often](https://sciscape.io/p/316e3533d7578352c242e9f68ba0a8c9c17ebad1e73464015b5524a8f93aef5f) (`sci:sha256:316e3533d7578352c242e9f68ba0a8c9c17ebad1e73464015b5524a8f93aef5f`). Finding: The raw 19.6x same-clone excess vanishes with neuropil control (1.16, CI 0.95-1.41) and reverses at 2 um voxels: ratio 0.66 (0.61-0.70) for >=5-synapse connections, 304/332 clones below 1, left/right clone ratios agree (rho 0.89); Kenyon cells are the exception at 2.98x (all 8 clones >1). No effect for contacts (>=1 synapse, 1.02). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): strong: Large n (1e9 pairs), fine spatial control, within-brain replication and bootstrap CIs; but a single brain and a finding that a neuropil-scale control removes the lineage effect is what is expected.
- [Predicted inhibitory input shares of 3,710 unique left-right FlyWire neuron pairs were not closer than random input sets, but were closer in cell types with 2–10 neurons per side](https://sciscape.io/p/c8a0f63cdff4fc807976f8bcb74fb9e6276a128805a8eb462210ec6131136b48) (`sci:sha256:c8a0f63cdff4fc807976f8bcb74fb9e6276a128805a8eb462210ec6131136b48`). Finding: For 3,710 unique left-right pairs, u=0.493 (0.485-0.503), equal to random input sets (about 2% compensation equivalent); in cell types with 2-10 neurons per side u=0.46-0.47 (about 9-13% compensation), which may arise from connectivity-based type definitions. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Well-controlled null with injection calibration and identified false-conservation artefacts; but predicted transmitters, one brain and synapse counts as strength.

### Methods, tools and resources

- [Motif tests that assume independent targets gave false calls when neurons differed in target number; fixed-total tests kept 5 of 6 V1 MAPseq and 26–33 of 39 VTA POINTseq motifs](https://sciscape.io/p/dd5c2468ce2ffcf91b34245063fc04e5b4bc1b9b29c1ebb6f1daade4c63ed4ea) (`sci:sha256:dd5c2468ce2ffcf91b34245063fc04e5b4bc1b9b29c1ebb6f1daade4c63ed4ea`). Finding: Independence-null false-call rate rises from ~10% to 63% when breadth varies; fixed-total (curveball) null stays <=1.3%; 5/6 V1 MAPseq and 26-33/39 VTA POINTseq motifs survive. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): strong: Simulations with false-positive and power estimates plus re-analysis of three public data sets.
