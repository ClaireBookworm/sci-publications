# Campaign: connectomes, from wiring to activity and behaviour

Subcampaign of the campaign `sci:sha256:c6fef5a19cfdc10222ff79abeab6bc3903cb22958f27a5f1b79d3a0b96ff8700` (what do connectomes reveal about how nervous systems are built?).

## Subquestion and scope

Tests of whether wiring predicts measured activity, response properties or behaviour.

## Status and main result

Status: mixed. Wiring matched receptor expression (histamine receptors best); a flyvis model and FlyWire counts correlate (r 0.71); passive cable models match receptive fields of some large visual types; but predicted transmitter signs did not improve ON/OFF polarity prediction, a whole-brain model gave different responses for left and right inputs, and C. elegans perturbation predictions only partly repeat across datasets. Diffusion tractography adds little to the prediction of individual functional connectivity (own structural connectivity explains about 0.15% of reliable individual variance).

## Open questions and proposed next tests (not yet run)

Compare connectome-constrained model predictions with calcium imaging of the same cell types under the same stimuli.

## Publications

Grouped by what each paper reports. A failed improvement test or a model-only bound does not rule out the approach in general; each line states the paper's own scope.

### Positive findings

- [The transmitter mix of FlyWire synaptic input predicted histamine, GABA and dopamine receptor gene expression in 56 fly cell types and in 50 optic lobe types of a second transcriptome](https://sciscape.io/p/d2978ae3f8157bcb633ce4cc0e6a215f34f0781454f433e564f0c73d13de57b7) (`sci:sha256:d2978ae3f8157bcb633ce4cc0e6a215f34f0781454f433e564f0c73d13de57b7`). Finding: Matching index M=0.21 (Davis, 56 types) and 0.15 (Ozel, 50 types), permutation p<=1e-4; histamine receptors match best (0.51/0.43), then dopamine and GABA; ACh, 5-HT, OA families not clear. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Pre-declared gene families and independent replication, but modest correlations, ~50 related types and predicted transmitter labels.
- [50 trained flyvis models stayed stable under larger FlyWire count changes than under random count changes of equal size; few loops, mainly the TmY4 self-connection, set the limit](https://sciscape.io/p/819205d27e2bd8a837f6f40379364711efd96a97fc820ce947717bad028df0fe) (`sci:sha256:819205d27e2bd8a837f6f40379364711efd96a97fc820ce947717bad028df0fe`). Finding: flyvis vs FlyWire counts correlate r=0.71 (56-58% within 2x); the two FlyWire lobes r=0.99; median stability blend alpha*=1.00 (25/50 models stable) vs 0.26 for random changes; few loops, mainly TmY4 self-connection, set the limit. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Clear contrast with random changes, but no retraining and many original models already disagree with data.
- [Passive cables on male-connectome arbors matched receptive fields of four large fly medulla types only with Rm/Ri of 3-14 cm, at or below fly visual-neuron fits and far below olfactory-neuron fits](https://sciscape.io/p/1fb8406863354dbf0ba5ed65c05f2281c68e97f7a943ac3004027cf9a8c10189) (`sci:sha256:1fb8406863354dbf0ba5ed65c05f2281c68e97f7a943ac3004027cf9a8c10189`). Finding: Passive cables match RFs of four large types only with Rm/Ri 3-14 cm (best common 5 cm, bootstrap 2-10), at or below fly visual-neuron fits (9-21 cm) and far below olfactory fits; Dm13 does not match; whole-cell one-compartment fails; extrapolation to 6-16 local units per wide-field cell. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Forward model with noise and bootstrap, but calcium not voltage, different animals/sexes, only 5 types >=25 columns, and first version was corrected for major flaws.
- [In 305 mouse Cre-line tracer experiments for 26 peptide and modulator systems, axon targets had more cognate receptor mRNA than other receptor mRNA, but only a little more than nearby control axons](https://sciscape.io/p/0995d53ffba85ae0c7ecbe881c042b7a4d703ba6284e22799584f09454a3685d) (`sci:sha256:0995d53ffba85ae0c7ecbe881c042b7a4d703ba6284e22799584f09454a3685d`). Finding: Cognate receptors had mean percentile 0.586 vs null 0.504 (p=0.0003; 19/26 systems), replicated with ABC (0.602); only slightly above nearby control axons. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Pre-planned analysis with nulls, but a small effect, mRNA not protein and site controls reduce it.
- [In 50 adults, own tractography connectomes matched own resting fMRI connectivity better than other people's after geometry controls, but explained under 0.2% of its reliable individual variance](https://sciscape.io/p/1153fcb42f71df59261e891e8318d31bd02a4a40d08d0086d9ba86ea70470e89) (`sci:sha256:1153fcb42f71df59261e891e8318d31bd02a4a40d08d0086d9ba86ea70470e89`). Finding: Yes but tiny: specificity delta r=0.029 (CI 0.018-0.038, p=0.0001) at Schaefer 400 with controls; own SC explains ~0.15% of reliable individual FC variance. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate - permutation-tested and robust to controls but effect is minute and SC noise uncorrected.

### Bounds and trade-offs

- [In a Brian2-matched FlyWire v783 whole-brain LIF model, sampled sign errors changed sugar responses more than input noise; other sign tables did not, but they changed olfactory-region responses](https://sciscape.io/p/8f698f912cb2d2e458429c227614a842ef38b12af73766ac0c2b9d1159ea31e1) (`sci:sha256:8f698f912cb2d2e458429c227614a842ef38b12af73766ac0c2b9d1159ea31e1`). Finding: Fast numba copy matches Brian2 (r 0.9994-0.9997); published sign table wrong for 3.7% of 67,341 reported neurons; sampled sign errors change MN9 65 Hz to 54-92 Hz and up to 38% of responding set; 15/323 single reversals shift MN9 >20%. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Calibration against literature labels plus sampling, but labels may overlap classifier training data (true error higher) and errors assumed independent.
- [In additive models of two human datasets, tractography and microstructure added at most 0.06 held-out R² of cortical FC beyond geometry; 7-network labels added 0.30–0.39](https://sciscape.io/p/5193165a57b3318ab090526970fb5dfdd5a271bd5908f1655cb7201961cd2294) (`sci:sha256:5193165a57b3318ab090526970fb5dfdd5a271bd5908f1655cb7201961cd2294`). Finding: Tractography and microstructure added at most 0.06 held-out R2; 7-network labels added 0.30-0.39. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Public data and nested held-out design but linear/additive models.

### Negative and null results

- [In a linear path model of both FlyWire optic lobes, predicted transmitter signs did not improve ON/OFF polarity prediction over an unsigned L1-versus-L2 rule for 51 visual cell types](https://sciscape.io/p/2b71bbf1dcf3e7b5ea43d7140af0c5449ea964f69f1ef587850ee6fdfdd29b27) (`sci:sha256:2b71bbf1dcf3e7b5ea43d7140af0c5449ea964f69f1ef587850ee6fdfdd29b27`). Finding: Unsigned rule correct for 44/51 types per hemisphere; signed rule 42 (left) and 44 (right), McNemar p=0.75 and 1.0; signs carry some information (shuffled signs 33 correct, p<=0.026) but fix and break different types. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Only 51 labels, linear static model and hemispheres not independent, so low power to detect a difference.
- [In a whole-brain fly spiking model on FlyWire v783, left and right copies of 151 sensory inputs gave less similar responses than new seeds, and runaway activity needed antennal lobe local neurons](https://sciscape.io/p/f0c8c8f2564b1efae7bdbf9826987398a8ca60dcc0b8443e1afc8774f821484e) (`sci:sha256:f0c8c8f2564b1efae7bdbf9826987398a8ca60dcc0b8443e1afc8774f821484e`). Finding: Median response similarity was 0.96 for a new seed but 0.17 for left vs right input (89 no-runaway channels; lower in all 72 measurable channels); 62/151 channels ignited runaway activity (about 8,200 neurons), removed by silencing 166 excitatory antennal-lobe local neurons (8,239 to 105 active). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Direct within-brain replicate and clear ignition mechanism test; but one model, one stimulus rate and biological asymmetry cannot be separated from reconstruction differences.
- [C. elegans muscle control predictions were matching bottlenecks; DD04–06 and PDB repeated in Cook 2019 data, but AVA, AVB, AVD and PVC did not, and NMJ position errors removed DD and PDB](https://sciscape.io/p/2099675ae49bd4ed2464eee2630c92424b93664f27e755fdd0536ce7c242bc43) (`sci:sha256:2099675ae49bd4ed2464eee2630c92424b93664f27e755fdd0536ce7c242bc43`). Finding: Predictions are matching bottlenecks (3,214/3,222 exact screens); DD04-06 and PDB repeat in Cook 2019 but AVA, AVB, AVD, PVC do not; NMJ position errors removed DD and PDB. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Exact calculation with mechanism and swap design; but annotations not independent animals and no behaviour test.
