# Mean platelet volume did not establish incremental elevated-CRP prediction across two NHANES releases

## Abstract
A source-frozen predictive audit in adults compared routine clinical/CBC measures with the same model plus mean platelet volume (MPV). Models fitted in 4,100 NHANES 2005–2006 participants were tested without recalibration in 5,189 separately sampled 2007–2008 participants. For CRP >3 mg/L, weighted target log loss increased from 0.51895385 to 0.51935182 (improvement −0.00039798; source/target coefficient-refit rescaled-PSU bootstrap 95% interval −0.00947539 to +0.00132803). Source grouped cross-validation also worsened. Brier score improved slightly, but discrimination did not. These results do not establish incremental utility, equivalence or harm. This is an audit of a known platelet-size/inflammation relationship, not discovery of a biomarker. A documented analyzer transition, complete-case selection and same-survey validation limit physiological interpretation.

## Question and prior evidence
Does MPV contain useful *incremental predictive* information about contemporaneous elevated CRP after accounting for platelet count and routine clinical/CBC measures? The inverse MPV–CRP association is already reported in a pooled NHANES 2005–2010 analysis ([primary study, PMID32313453](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32313453/)). [A population MPV study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30731389/) motivated consideration of platelet size; [published correspondence](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7340450/) highlights interpretive concerns. Our contribution is a reproducible withheld-main-effect, source-frozen transport audit rather than a claim that current analyses universally ignore MPV. MPV is not assumed to measure platelet activation unambiguously.

The literature/data-led question was registered before computation. One SCI duplication check for this MPV question searched “platelet volume” in title, abstract and keywords. It found related interaction studies, not the registered withholding/adding of the MPV main-effect block. Their results were not used or tested here, so no SCI citations are added. Search coverage cannot exclude concurrent duplication. An earlier RDW question was retired without fitting after its own check found close overlap; its registration and evidence remain archived locally.

## Population, data and access
The statistical unit is a participant aged ≥20, excluding known pregnancy, with complete required measures and positive MEC examination weight. Source flow: 10,348 all participants →4,979 adults →4,643 not known pregnant →4,100 complete cases, 1,573 elevated-CRP events, weighted prevalence 35.05%. Target: 10,149→5,935→5,878→5,189, 1,876 events, weighted prevalence 32.03%. Source/target have 30/32 masked PSUs. These differing pregnancy exclusions and missingness may shift the population; weights do not repair complete-case selection.

Public CDC [CBC D](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/2005/DataFiles/CBC_D.htm), [CBC E](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/2007/DataFiles/CBC_E.htm), [CRP D](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/2005/DataFiles/CRP_D.htm) and corresponding CRP E, DEMO, BMX and SMQ files were acquired or hash-verified on the UTC dates in the provenance manifest. Full URLs, SHA-256 hashes, sizes and cache origin are supplied. The [NCHS agreement](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/policy/data-user-agreement.html) allows statistical use and prohibits identification attempts. No special access, recruitment or new measurements occurred. No raw participants or individual predictions are bundled; input terms remain separate from the SCI license.

The two releases are distinct cross-sectional datasets, not splits. Public SEQN sets are disjoint; SEQN is not definitive lifetime person linkage, so exact repeat-person exclusion cannot be proven. Both share survey infrastructure and are not institution-independent cohorts. No claim of independent-platform validation is made; that objective remains unmet.

## Frozen methods
Outcome: LBXCRP >0.3 mg/dL, equivalent to >3 mg/L. A single result is not a chronic inflammatory diagnosis. Baseline: ridge logistic regression, C=1, cubic additive splines with four equally spaced source-derived knots for age, BMI, WBC, hemoglobin, MCV and platelet count, plus sex, current smoking and sex×age. MPV model adds an identically specified spline block for LBXMPSI (fL). Current smoking is defined by ≥100 lifetime cigarettes plus current daily/some-day smoking; never/former smoking is zero, unknown statuses are excluded. Never smokers' structurally missing current-smoking response is handled explicitly.

Spline degree3, include_bias=False, linear extrapolation, StandardScaler, lbfgs max_iter3000, tolerance1e-8. Features and all preprocessing are source-only; weighted fitting normalizes WTMEC2YR to mean1. Primary metric is target examination-weighted mean log-loss improvement (baseline minus MPV; positive favored). No target-driven fitting, hyperparameter selection or threshold optimization. Source five-fold CV groups stratum–PSU identifiers, refitting preprocessing within folds. This pipeline was fixed before CRP outcomes were analysed, although CBC inputs had been cached for previous unrelated projects; this is not globally untouched researcher exposure.

Uncertainty: seed20261118; 2,000 fixed-model target Rao–Wu rescaled-PSU draws and 300 paired source/target draws with both coefficient sets refitted. Within each masked stratum, sample n−1 PSUs with replacement and multiply counts by n/(n−1). All source covariate preprocessing remains fixed in the coefficient-refit bootstrap. Percentile 95% intervals condition on model specification, preprocessing and selected complete cases; they are not exhaustive model-development or nonresponse uncertainty. No confirmatory subgroup p values or multiplicity-adjusted claims.

## Results and challenges
| Metric | Baseline | +MPV |
|---|---:|---:|
| Target weighted log loss |0.51895385|0.51935182|
| Target weighted Brier |0.17142732|0.17137282|
| Target weighted AUROC |0.77534198|0.77368704|
| Target mean predicted probability |0.34847630|0.34057046|
| Source grouped-CV log loss |0.52842741|0.53021896|

The primary improvement was −0.00039798. Fixed-model target interval: [−0.00141543,+0.00063112]; coefficient-refit source/target interval: [−0.00947539,+0.00132803]. The latter is appreciably asymmetric and wide: coefficient instability/extrapolation is part of observed model-development uncertainty, not suppressed. Absence of demonstrated improvement is not evidence that MPV has no physiological information. Slight Brier improvement (0.00005450) and closer mean prediction coexist with worse AUROC/log loss; we do not select the favorable secondary metric.

Prespecified log-loss gains: C=0.1 −0.00022151; C=10 −0.00035053; six knots −0.00078108; refit after excluding CRP >10mg/L −0.00002565 (n=3,645/4,666); nonanemic restriction +0.00026520 (n=3,793/4,746), a small favorable point estimate without subgroup uncertainty or superiority claim. Nonanemic means Hb≥13g/dL male, ≥12 female; it does not establish iron sufficiency. Frozen-model target subgroup gains: male −0.00042029, female −0.00037684, ≥65 −0.00364018, 20–64 +0.00025666. Unweighted target gain −0.00072995. Full unfavorable and favorable results and 39 source-label-permutation fits are supplied; permutations are a descriptive stress test, not a formal design-based null test.

CBC documentation explicitly reports a MAXM→HMX instrument transition. Weighted MPV mean shifted 8.1113→7.7370 fL, SD 0.8366→0.8285. A post-primary exploratory diagnostic translated target MPV by the unlabeled mean difference, leaving all source coefficients fixed; gain remained negative (−0.00087037). This uses target covariates, is not pristine confirmation and does not identify assay causation. It cannot substitute for calibrated independent validation.

## Limits and future test
Cross-sectional associations do not establish temporal prediction, mechanism or clinical utility. CRP is nonspecific; repeated values, medication, iron deficiency, ethnicity, renal conditions and acute illness are incompletely addressed. Complete-case exclusion, top-coded ages, survey design approximation, measurement error and changing prevalence can distort transport. No decision-curve analysis, clinical thresholds or intervention outcomes were tested. These results apply only to this representation, baseline and two samples; they do not exclude better platelet representations or other outcomes.

A future independent clinical cohort could prespecify the frozen model, harmonize MPV instrument/timing, measure CRP repeatedly with iron status and medications, and compare calibration, log loss and decision consequences. That study has not been performed here. The present audit supplies a negative computational test, not a validated clinical tool.
