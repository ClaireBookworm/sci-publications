# Campaign: non-invasive brain measurement, magnetic resonance contrasts

Subcampaign of the campaign `sci:sha256:b8346ed353aa99d00b9d7b22e9e509e59ecd2745f211e0f790cccf564215b70d` (how can human brain activity be measured without surgery, better than today?).

## Subquestion and scope

Nuclear magnetic resonance signals: BOLD and its physics, neural-current MRI (spin-lock), sodium and xenon MRI, susceptibility, diffusion, and electrical-property tomography. Elastography is in the coupled branch.

## Status and main result

Status: several proposed contrasts fail. In cortical models, neural-current fields are 14-1300 times below what published phase-agnostic spin-lock MRI tests at 3 T can detect, while a phase-referenced readout could detect alpha in about 1 h; in rodent cortex models one forepaw pulse changes the sodium MRI signal by about 0.02-0.25% (medians) against a reported 2-3%; an ADC decrease in BOLD-active cortex was not found and circular voxel selection and b-value ordering can produce the published decrease; bSSFP functional-conductivity phase is at most about one fifth explained by BOLD, and in folded-cortex models the functional EPT phase signal comes mostly from blood displacing CSF. Diffusion and exchange designs show vascular confounds that robust protocols reduce.

## Open questions and proposed next tests (not yet run)

Run phase-known spin-lock excitation in phantoms with known currents; test sodium MRI changes with independent voxel selection; repeat ADC-fMRI with timing and b-value ordering randomised.

## Publications

Grouped by what each paper reports. A failed improvement test or a model-only bound does not rule out the approach in general; each line states the paper's own scope.

### Positive findings

- [In human cortex models, blood-oxygen changes alone make diffusion MRI report a false 3-7% rise of membrane water exchange during activation; a vessel-robust protocol reduces it at 3 T](https://sciscape.io/p/42577b3bd0cf641ae4ab51034086833c5121d6e05e4e47e2fe1f92c69bca0d4b) (`sci:sha256:42577b3bd0cf641ae4ab51034086833c5121d6e05e4e47e2fe1f92c69bca0d4b`). Finding: False +3% at 3T and +7% at 7T exchange rise; robust protocol reduces to +0.4% at 3T; true effect predicted +5-22% if pump-coupled; 10% change in 5 cm3 needs ~0.7 h. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Quantified confound with protocol fix, still model-only.

### Bounds and trade-offs

- [Neuronal fields in MRI voxels are too small for published random-phase spin-lock designs at 3 T, and an EEG phase reference decreases the data needed for alpha by more than 1000 times](https://sciscape.io/p/38987a4f936c769e0d58b321455c1a3a57b323e10c09ae14ba57f4e69fa7ccf4) (`sci:sha256:38987a4f936c769e0d58b321455c1a3a57b323e10c09ae14ba57f4e69fa7ccf4`). Finding: Voxel fields are about 2-9 pT (steady-state), 11-105 pT (alpha), at most ~1 nT at physiological maximum; the Bern 120/240 Hz protocol needs >=4.5 nT. An EEG-phase-referenced linear spin-lock needs ~70 h per voxel vs ~400,000 h without phase (4 mm voxel, tSNR 65, PLV 0.6). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Quantitative, calibrated with public data and reviewed, but purely modelled; no phantom or in vivo test.
- [In 16 modelled hemispheres, oscillations give 2-150 pT fields in the strongest 3-mm voxels: phase-referenced MRI could detect alpha in about 1 h; random-phase analyses need 100-1000 times longer](https://sciscape.io/p/6633e7a10717e2e49f6e21e112bb021fead4dfdcc807397b62c931581c8b800a) (`sci:sha256:6633e7a10717e2e49f6e21e112bb021fead4dfdcc807397b62c931581c8b800a`). Finding: 95th-percentile voxel field along B0: alpha 28 pT (13-58), SSVEP 4 pT, max physiological 0.29 nT; random-phase detection needs >=1000 h per voxel at 3 T, whereas phase-referenced readout needs 100-1000x less (alpha ~1 h scale at 3-7 T). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Multi-subject geometry and Monte Carlo checks, but modelled; overlaps strongly with 38987a4f.
- [Three exact MRI echoes leave initial phase uncertain in tested positive static spin mixtures](https://sciscape.io/p/008d903dd6bb3bacababe592d807aef98f588b6e7115e7e6abdecd8e90ab0bac) (`sci:sha256:008d903dd6bb3bacababe592d807aef98f588b6e7115e7e6abdecd8e90ab0bac`). Finding: No: at TE 15.6, 41.6, 67.6 ms a constructed pair with offsets within +/-10 Hz differs by 2.219 deg initial phase (upper limit 2.22 deg); adding a 2 ms echo cuts the limit to 1.96e-5 rad in exact data, but finite noise greatly widens ambiguity. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Constructive proof with Bloch checks, but conditional on a simple signal model.
- [Repeated Gaussian draws changed water density and MRI contrast around one impermeable vessel](https://sciscape.io/p/f6078d8022e8047de84cd9ccb431167e7b67d48e70b64dededb02febcb7fff87) (`sci:sha256:f6078d8022e8047de84cd9ccb431167e7b67d48e70b64dededb02febcb7fff87`). Finding: Redrawing a Gaussian step at walls gave relative contrast errors -4.21% to -0.19%; holding position gave +0.046% to +11.70% (individual signal errors <0.38%). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Verified against diffusion equation and analytic solutions, but narrow geometry.
- [Equal magnetic maps and total power allowed different local absorption in passive slabs with one field direction](https://sciscape.io/p/6213863249d05cd35e227d8b0b4b19ade93680cec4c333a1a3611426f4abbc01) (`sci:sha256:6213863249d05cd35e227d8b0b4b19ade93680cec4c333a1a3611426f4abbc01`). Finding: No: in a 50 mm slab at 128 MHz two smooth materials share the complex magnetic field and total power yet differ in local power density by up to 25.986% of peak (19.2% after 10 mm averaging). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Special-case existence example with limited physical relevance.
- [One axial wavelength gives zero mean MRI field change in an infinite circular cylinder with weak scalar susceptibility](https://sciscape.io/p/97c326eb63c234bfe3e8a00d374c4a4bd9de84408cfbd9f6f0ce00ad5d413269) (`sci:sha256:97c326eb63c234bfe3e8a00d374c4a4bd9de84408cfbd9f6f0ce00ad5d413269`). Finding: Yes: zero at wavelength 6.0166 radii; local uniform-cylinder formulas need >=54.89 radii for <5% gain drop. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Exact result but little brain relevance shown.
- [Passive magnetic resonance models have a sharp rate sensitivity limit at fixed geometry and controls](https://sciscape.io/p/06245ced46ad1ceef1d44e23fd12574080be5452d26aa40543f48a5256c7baae) (`sci:sha256:06245ced46ad1ceef1d44e23fd12574080be5452d26aa40543f48a5256c7baae`). Finding: Normalized signal changes at most half the fractional rate change; hyperbolic-secant pulse reaches 0.499999887. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate - sharp theorem.
- [Concave pulse design bounds quadratic magnetic resonance readouts for zero-mean fields under reversible Markov diffusion](https://sciscape.io/p/d9a1ec07383bd30a9c1654acbf09a97e81906db34c0bcc20c269e3ea0dc764eb) (`sci:sha256:d9a1ec07383bd30a9c1654acbf09a97e81906db34c0bcc20c269e3ea0dc764eb`). Finding: Concave pulses reach within 0.060% of computed upper values and retain 50.3-63.6% of the maximum; a fixed 45-degree axis retains exactly half; no motion gives 2/pi. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Analytic/numeric bounds for nine assigned models; no experiment.
- [In a literature-based mass and flux budget, normal transmitter release changes the MRS glutamate signal by amounts about 10 to 160 times smaller than event-related fMRS studies report](https://sciscape.io/p/a0c00001aac3d223c77cdc29b50003c8c5d8072e751a85c562efa44b7676beef) (`sci:sha256:a0c00001aac3d223c77cdc29b50003c8c5d8072e751a85c562efa44b7676beef`). Finding: Release changes MRS glutamate by 0.10% within 3 s (95% range 0.018-0.51%); hard ceiling 1.9%; reported changes are 10-160x larger. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Monte Carlo over published parameters with a favourable-mechanism assumption.
- [Ideal gradients maximize the linear MR signal from a sinusoidal field in uniform free diffusion](https://sciscape.io/p/3c82f5f88fa238c29d869a8dfebefcb644afec688a472f6b498ea303d9a0e19c) (`sci:sha256:3c82f5f88fa238c29d869a8dfebefcb644afec688a472f6b498ea303d9a0e19c`). Finding: Proved an attained global bound; 20 um period under 80 mT/m and 150 T/m/s keeps 99.9978%, while a separate proof limits 5 um to a small fraction. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Analytic proofs, but an idealised physics setting.

### Negative and null results

- [In rodent cortex models with literature ion and energy budgets, one forepaw pulse changes the 23Na MRI signal by 0.02-0.25 percent (median), below the reported 2-3 percent NARS response](https://sciscape.io/p/1a6617e558a8a105c92c9ac87a209c0f98bd886ea67c88eee617575e163b7d74) (`sci:sha256:1a6617e558a8a105c92c9ac87a209c0f98bd886ea67c88eee617575e163b7d74`). Finding: Median signal change is -0.02% (cytoplasmic entry) to -0.25% (all moved ions invisible), versus the reported 2-3%; explaining 2.5% needs ~10x more sodium moved or a 23-28% rise in quadrupolar coupling. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): strong: transparent budget with uncertainty, directly challenges a published claim.
- [Frequency and relaxation changes copy prescribed functional conductivity MRI signals in a single-pool steady-state model](https://sciscape.io/p/b9cdc63760c649f4bcf4597450b75c90f5d02c376ccc20ddf7016d1c9bda13e3) (`sci:sha256:b9cdc63760c649f4bcf4597450b75c90f5d02c376ccc20ddf7016d1c9bda13e3`). Finding: No: frequency (0.9631 Hz) and T2 (0.6723 ms) shifts copied a 0.4505% magnitude and 0.3 mrad phase change with no RF phase change; apparent conductivity change -0.00126 S/m. Several phase settings separate the causes. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: exact, validated by Bloch, ideal single pool.
- [Vessel fields changed apparent diffusion despite equal signals from opposite gradients in tested reflecting cylinder models](https://sciscape.io/p/0a148ebff8a4d2794e81e26560986aa3c149945fc21e45aa48cd763e48b00dc2) (`sci:sha256:0a148ebff8a4d2794e81e26560986aa3c149945fc21e45aa48cd763e48b00dc2`). Finding: No: vessel fields changed apparent diffusion by up to 4.52% (two-lobe) and 1.13% (four-lobe) with equal opposite-polarity signals. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: confirms known mechanism with open reference set.
- [Exact magnetic maps and full power calibration leave wide local absorption intervals in a uniform head sphere](https://sciscape.io/p/6fc4577547cccca715f9e6639321e25915413d726987cd4c15e9f4ed011363ae) (`sci:sha256:6fc4577547cccca715f9e6639321e25915413d726987cd4c15e9f4ed011363ae`). Finding: No: a 10 g ball at 298 MHz ranges 0.01545-1.08238 W/kg with identical maps and power matrix. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: sharp counterexample.
- [In folded-cortex models with literature tissue values, the 3 T fEPT phase signal of brain activation comes mostly from blood pushing out CSF, not from tissue conductivity, which changes under 1%](https://sciscape.io/p/62c6f7cc1494d61e9f97c98c2bb84e2338a53377740e3665eef45d9389542d7f) (`sci:sha256:62c6f7cc1494d61e9f97c98c2bb84e2338a53377740e3665eef45d9389542d7f`). Finding: Tissue conductivity changes <~1% at 128 MHz; most of the fEPT phase signal (about 90% boundary share) comes from blood displacing CSF at the pial boundary; modelled phase change 1-4e-4 rad; reported conductivity values are not physical tissue changes. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Plausible mechanism with Monte Carlo budget and 2D full-wave check, but quantitative boundary share unverified in anatomical heads.
- [In a model of human cortex, neural magnetic fields are 14 to 1300 times below what published spin-lock MRI tests can detect; only excitation with a known neural phase comes near](https://sciscape.io/p/6a977403237c79c3f425a15c5cb25b7cecf2c1865a107a91f9b9762ba8fc5842) (`sci:sha256:6a977403237c79c3f425a15c5cb25b7cecf2c1865a107a91f9b9762ba8fc5842`). Finding: Fields are 14 to 1300 times below detectability at 3 T, 3 mm voxels (0.29-0.38 nT voxel-mean per nAm/mm2); only phase-known excitation comes near. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): strong: Directly addresses published claims with realistic cortex geometry and favourable noise assumptions.
- [A physical model shows that published hyperpolarized xenon-129 MRI task responses exceed what local blood flow can cause, and that current xenon methods need about 1 cm voxels](https://sciscape.io/p/250e388d8af6de3605308d2061503c0b8c25c806691ed93d28343ec555826afe) (`sci:sha256:250e388d8af6de3605308d2061503c0b8c25c806691ed93d28343ec555826afe`). Finding: Reported responses exceed blood flow; needs about 1 cm voxels. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Literature.
- [In 83 adults at 3 T, BOLD field changes account for at most a fifth of reported bSSFP functional-conductivity phase but bias gradient-echo phase extrapolation as much as the expected signal](https://sciscape.io/p/0ce34128d9f2d836d1e479e6e353acdcd73d6372f7bc3cd153f5daf64c9b4449) (`sci:sha256:0ce34128d9f2d836d1e479e6e353acdcd73d6372f7bc3cd153f5daf64c9b4449`). Finding: In 83 adults at 3 T (ds006926), BOLD leaks 0.014-0.018 mrad per 1% BOLD into bSSFP phase, 5-7% of the reported 0.30 mrad (about 20% in phase-selected voxels); but in gradient-echo extrapolation to TE=0 it gives 0.6-0.8 mrad per 1%, as large as the expected conductivity signal. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: real data, N=83, with replication run on 30 subjects, but bSSFP is inferred not measured.
- [In 3 T models, per-subject spin-lock MRI findings in flicker and epilepsy studies need brain fields above maximal cortical synchrony; a phase-locked flicker test could check the method](https://sciscape.io/p/8ca426bcd7f35395e35f041cddd776d66d007ff521280457691410e1dd001ec8) (`sci:sha256:8ca426bcd7f35395e35f041cddd776d66d007ff521280457691410e1dd001ec8`). Finding: Needed field 11-12 nT per voxel per subject (2.3-3.0 nT pooled) vs 1.9-2.9 nT at maximal synchrony and ~0.024 nT for median alpha; findings need fields 3-7x maximal synchrony, ~270x expected. A phase-locked flicker test would take 0.3-4 h at 3 T. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: broad cross-checking with generous assumptions, no real scans.
- [Large reported sodium MRI responses to brain activity need all known mechanisms near their joint upper limits in calibrated models; a 7 T design needs about a day per cubic centimetre](https://sciscape.io/p/b3e0f97d43d9a2223abfe667e15c8222bb51ecf34175d69fb1e43fb743bdc943) (`sci:sha256:b3e0f97d43d9a2223abfe667e15c8222bb51ecf34175d69fb1e43fb743bdc943`). Finding: Rodent 2-3% NARS dip is 1.0-1.6x the model limit (1.92%) and ~15x the 99th percentile; human 7 T M1/SMA decreases of -27%/-37% lie beyond all 44,700 calibrated draws; a 7 T design needs about a day per cm3. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: calibrated Monte Carlo plus optimization limits, no new data.
- [Signed spin patterns add at most five percent pooled signal in six smoothed cortical models with fixed water positions](https://sciscape.io/p/e4eb24d69b4193013056d9d61b207983e591affec32aabf9b34565d3a77235e1) (`sci:sha256:e4eb24d69b4193013056d9d61b207983e591affec32aabf9b34565d3a77235e1`). Finding: Signed patterns add at most 4.83% pooled signal (1 mm cells gain 0.25-2.56%) in six smoothed cortical models; in an assigned current-loop field a smooth spin reversal keeps 90.47% of ideal signal. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: small gain in one anatomy with smoothing built in.
- [An independent two-head model reproduces earlier voxel-field estimates per unit cortical dipole density within 1.6-fold and confirms that clinical rotary-echo spin-lock MRI cannot detect brain fields](https://sciscape.io/p/1b5e590166e6da36581c09447c5ea3b11403b72aff5908ebbf0c5587642ee0d3) (`sci:sha256:1b5e590166e6da36581c09447c5ea3b11403b72aff5908ebbf0c5587642ee0d3`). Finding: Voxel field per unit dipole density agrees with earlier two SCI models within 1.6-fold in folded cortex of two subjects (467 patches); clinical rotary-echo spin-lock cannot detect brain fields. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: independent replication, agreement on geometry.
- [In public 3 T ADC-fMRI data, BOLD-active visual cortex shows no ADC decrease; circular voxel selection inflates the published amplitude and b-value order biases its timing](https://sciscape.io/p/0284064bc4d24942c428556e845444d164cebe7a9b6ea8eace90b7977b733cf3) (`sci:sha256:0284064bc4d24942c428556e845444d164cebe7a9b6ea8eace90b7977b733cf3`). Finding: In re-analysis of public 3T data (n=12) ADC in independent BOLD-active voxels changed +0.13% (-0.06 to +0.31), not -1.2%; circular selection gives -4.5% even on random timing and b-value ordering biases timing; a decrease elsewhere is neither shown nor excluded. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): strong - uses raw public data, injection tests and null controls to expose selection and timing artefacts; power limits stated.

### Methods, tools and resources

- [A tensor bound limits static MRI signal error for all field directions in fixed voxel models](https://sciscape.io/p/0d9eb1fdd576c07cf2fd4bad4b234f3395f6fae26e9fcfb3cc86b7b7eeed8235) (`sci:sha256:0d9eb1fdd576c07cf2fd4bad4b234f3395f6fae26e9fcfb3cc86b7b7eeed8235`). Finding: Largest signal error was 0.9804% over 420 tests; the bound guarantees 1% up to phase scales 2.82-4.11 rad; exact and FFT agree to 1e-15. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: proof and reference solver.
- [Field variation bounds MRI signal error from phase averaging in stationary reversible motion](https://sciscape.io/p/15b6ef9b791f6f1448a60fefb6866606387a1b9682aeb824130df036f9da5832) (`sci:sha256:15b6ef9b791f6f1448a60fefb6866606387a1b9682aeb824130df036f9da5832`). Finding: Error at most T h^2 E/12; largest conditional-mean error 0.1216% at 0.2 ms. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: sharp proof.
- [A conditional side choice gives exact end positions in a passive one dimensional diffusion model with one membrane](https://sciscape.io/p/66b9fa602f5edb9dd1bce63502c091102758779879d19f5afc0425aed3426ee0) (`sci:sha256:66b9fa602f5edb9dd1bce63502c091102758779879d19f5afc0425aed3426ee0`). Finding: 216 checks within 3.569 standard errors; flux solver agreed within 0.277 ppm; Bessel crossing rule in existing MRI software changed fitted permeability by up to 17.05% (echo difference up to 0.9958%). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): moderate: Verified sampler with independent checks; tangential to brain imaging goal.
- [Six weighted poses give the least mean squared tensor error in an ideal MRI field model with limited head tilt](https://sciscape.io/p/06f2769771bcb19ae31ceb852ab506ec478183cae7c54140cbf8a62ebf659be5) (`sci:sha256:06f2769771bcb19ae31ceb852ab506ec478183cae7c54140cbf8a62ebf659be5`). Finding: Six weighted poses are optimal (25 degree tilt: one neutral 23.68% of time plus five tilted 15.26% each); uniform sampling is 2.22x worse. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Clean optimality proof but idealised.
- [Path tensors reduced direction sampling cost in two MRI lattice models with weak scalar susceptibility](https://sciscape.io/p/c15d2c91d8168596938fef4c3b4f9063ae0830abff722edabe234de69a776324) (`sci:sha256:c15d2c91d8168596938fef4c3b4f9063ae0830abff722edabe234de69a776324`). Finding: Gave 1.65-2.13x better precision per time in 16 conditions of two periodic vessel models; angle integration error 1e-10. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Efficiency gain in toy models.
- [Field gradients bound phase averaging error in free diffusion MRI with a constant diffusion tensor](https://sciscape.io/p/70f504f7b19e49a4bf84caea785cbcf07a8c7c18b41d6b37455ce2fff24029b3) (`sci:sha256:70f504f7b19e49a4bf84caea785cbcf07a8c7c18b41d6b37455ce2fff24029b3`). Finding: Error <= sum dt^3 g^2/12; 360 tests max 0.0605% at 0.2 ms vs trapezoid 0.0571%. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: Sharp but minor, comparable to trapezoid.
- [Averaging grid directions removes reflection error from one periodic MRI field formula with scalar susceptibility](https://sciscape.io/p/9d3bc6e6c3514b96cc89d7825ae7f91705ce073671ec42ea4a8c1e2051cd67ef) (`sci:sha256:9d3bc6e6c3514b96cc89d7825ae7f91705ce073671ec42ea4a8c1e2051cd67ef`). Finding: Exact alternating pattern coefficient is -1/6 versus -2/3 and +1/3 in two descriptions; on two public brain maps coordinate changes were 7.91% and 11.26% of susceptibility RMS (29.4%, 42.1% of field RMS). Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: narrow numerical fix.
- [Ideal MR encodings minimize the worst static diffusion range in passive spectral models with bounded slow modes](https://sciscape.io/p/c95cd049ebfcdb6cd772610d101f94eabe4c3da2deefb4436ce2aa917012b56f) (`sci:sha256:c95cd049ebfcdb6cd772610d101f94eabe4c3da2deefb4436ce2aa917012b56f`). Finding: A cosine design for known minimum rate cuts worst zero-error range from 9.9995% to 6.4587% of the high-frequency coefficient (35.41% reduction) at duration x rate = 10; twelve measurements without priors differ by less than 0.030%. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak: abstract theory only.
- [Local reduced models gave fast MRI signals with error bounds in three fixed vessel grids](https://sciscape.io/p/e7154100d44d5d9d2edea3fc534eeb9d9d70553cb313bdb959c4424ce6c798b2) (`sci:sha256:e7154100d44d5d9d2edea3fc534eeb9d9d70553cb313bdb959c4424ce6c798b2`). Finding: All 864 held-out queries met 0.001 absolute error (max 0.000537); needed about 1,000 queries to pay back construction. Evidence strength (reader's assessment): weak - tool verified on small grids.
- [Phase moments bound MRI signals across scalar susceptibility changes in fixed vessel models](https://sciscape.io/p/eeaf30e543e3b1c73e224803dc54a66af4987e0615799a7a1d6a7e28ccc97021) (`sci:sha256:eeaf30e543e3b1c73e224803dc54a66af4987e0615799a7a1d6a7e28ccc97021`).
