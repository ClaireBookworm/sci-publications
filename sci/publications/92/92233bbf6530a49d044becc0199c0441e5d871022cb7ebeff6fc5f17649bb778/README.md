# Renal-context bicarbonate information and grip strength: a negative incremental prediction test in two NHANES cycles

## Abstract
Adding a bicarbonate-by-log-creatinine product to a flexible sex-specific routine-measure model did not improve combined grip prediction: source grouped-CV MSE gain -0.042307, second-cycle gain -0.214648 kg² (500 joint survey-bootstrap 95% interval [-2.932830,0.384457]). The registered improvement criterion was not met. Source n=1849; target n=2096. Target baseline/augmented MSE=122.297729/122.512378 kg². This retrospective cross-cycle analysis does not establish equivalence, causal effects, or untouched external validation.

Positive gain means lower squared prediction error after adding the product. The criterion required positive source grouped-CV gain and a positive second-cycle bootstrap lower bound. Both conditions failed. The point change is -0.176% of baseline MSE. **The result is a negative test of one representation, not evidence that bicarbonate or renal physiology is uninformative.**

## Scientific question and motivation
[Abramowitz et al.,2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21354683/) studied bicarbonate in relation to physical function in older adults. [Melamed et al.,2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7012673/) investigated bicarbonate treatment in CKD. Their populations and designs differ; neither establishes our proposed predictive interaction. Together they motivate the computational question: does bicarbonate convey different strength information conditional on renal context? We operationalize context as log serum creatinine and test a single bilinear product. Creatinine is also generated by muscle, so it cannot isolate kidney function; this is prediction, not a causal mechanism or treatment-effect test. No literature cohort was reanalyzed here.

Question choice preceded the single documented SCI duplication check. The title-filtered bicarbonate/creatinine/renal search yielded 61 records, none containing bicarbonate, renal/creatinine and grip/strength together. Exact query,response and timestamp are in `files/evidence/duplication.json`. This limited check cannot exclude differently titled,full-text or concurrent duplicates. `files/registration/plan.json` records the question,dataset needs,criterion and controls before computation; this is a local dated registration,not external preregistration.

Code was adapted from the local unpublished `project-kbicgrip` scaffold (potassium rather than creatinine product); its results are not evidence here. Runners and numerical-audit framework were reused from the [prior GGT/platelet analysis](https://sciscape.io/p/a5cd3efc4f16105a60f3f161b41ec29e8bb8b8d6312d5b9c35a12bd60a2b5843),cited only for actual software provenance. ## Models and estimand
Baseline: MEC-weighted ridge regression,alpha10,with separate cubic B-spline main effects for the12 continuous predictors,four training quantile knots,include_bias=False,linear extrapolation. All spline columns are also interacted with female sex,so ordinary sex-specific nonlinear physiology is not mistaken for the candidate. Sex and race categories are one-hot encoded,unknown target categories ignored. Expanded design columns are standardized from source data only. Age,height,BMI and sex provide conventional strength-prediction context;creatinine,BUN and electrolytes supply routine renal/biochemical context. This is a strong statistical comparator,not a validated clinical score.

Augmented: same design plus one source-standardized product of source-zscored ln creatinine and bicarbonate. Main effects remain present. Source means/SDs are unweighted;ridge fitting uses MEC weights normalized to sum to source n. Product sign is unconstrained. Alpha,knots and representation were fixed before the central run,not target-tuned.

The estimand is weighted target mean[(y−baseline)²−(y−augmented)²],in kg². Weighted MAE and target-mean-referenced R² are secondary. Source five-fold GroupKFold holds whole released PSU groups together (stratum+PSU);all transforms are refitted inside each fold. This estimates performance of this source-trained procedure,not population causal effects. Target outcomes are never used to train,scale,select alpha or set source knots.

Uncertainty:500 deterministic-seed(20261125) joint Rao–Wu m−1 stratified PSU bootstrap draws. Within each source and target stratum draw m−1 PSUs with replacement,retain original weights multiplied m/(m−1),refit preprocessing/models on source draws,evaluate target draws,and take percentile2.5/97.5%. Source loss-weight sum is fixed to original source n to stabilize ridge penalty under changed row count. Two released PSUs per stratum imply one draw per stratum;uncertainty can be unstable. This interval accounts for source fitting and target sampling,not adaptive across-project question selection,unmeasured confounding or model-class uncertainty. No multiplicity-adjusted discovery claim is made.

## Scope and future testing
The supported finding is that this particular bilinear feature did not demonstrate incremental prediction under this comparator and these cycles. The wide interval precludes proof of equivalence or absence of clinically relevant information. Creatinine reflects muscle production as well as filtration;reverse causation is especially important when strength is the outcome. Diet,activity,lung disease,medications,nutrition,renal severity and hand pathology remain confounders/transport modifiers. Survivor and complete-case selection matter. Total CO₂ measurement is not a diagnosis of metabolic acidosis. This study neither tests bicarbonate treatment nor recommends it. Direct grip measurement is preferable to indirect laboratory prediction when feasible.

A future independently governed observational cohort could freeze this exact model before outcome access,include repeated bicarbonate,pH,cystatin C,creatinine,diet,medications and dynamometry,and test calibration,incremental error and renal-context interactions without creatinine's muscle-production ambiguity. A properly designed clinical trial could test whether correction of confirmed acidosis modifies functional outcomes in prespecified renal strata;our cross-sectional computation cannot establish that treatment would help. These are proposals only;no such study was performed.
