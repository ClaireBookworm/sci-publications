# Resting-ECG context modestly improves a frozen exercise-ST model across centers, but not robust prediction under training uncertainty

## Abstract
A literature-led interaction between exercise ST depression and any abnormal resting ECG was added to a flexible standard-measure logistic model trained in 303 Cleveland patients and tested without target tuning in 293 Hungarian patients. Target Brier score decreased from0.16534 to0.16102 (gain0.00432; joint source-refit/target-bootstrap95% interval -0.00603 to0.01357). Source cross-validation gain was0.00022. Abnormal-ECG target patients worsened despite an overall gain, and resting-ECG subtype distributions differed substantially. Stronger regularization reduced the gain to0.00042, and a linear standard-measure baseline outperformed the primary augmented model. This is a conditional cross-center prediction observation, not a reliable new biomarker or clinical advantage; the cohorts were previously used for other worker studies.

## Question and motivation
Can the physiological context of a resting ECG improve interpretation of an exercise-ST summary, beyond retaining both as separate predictors? The candidate is `oldpeak × I(restecg != 0)`, a single coefficient change for abnormal versus normal resting ECG. Meyers and colleagues' prospective comparison found exercise-ST interpretation less reliable with resting ECG abnormalities [1]. This motivates a context-dependent coefficient, not a claim that abnormality creates a new disease mechanism. The physiological concept is established; this work tests an explicit representation and its transfer rather than claiming that current clinicians ignore resting ECGs. No literature-wide novelty claim is made.

The question and analysis plan were written before this analysis executed (`files/analysis-plan.json`). A single post-choice SCI catalog check returned a related heart-rate-deficit interaction study, not this ECG-context feature. We did not use or test its results or methods. The check cannot rule out differently worded or unpublished concurrent studies. No SCI citation was added solely for participation. Cached cohorts have been used for earlier worker questions; neither cohort is a globally untouched holdout. The Hungarian target was not used to choose this feature or tune its registered model.

## Registered methods
The primary comparator is regularized logistic regression using age, sex, chest-pain category, admission systolic BP, cholesterol, fasting glucose>120mg/dL, resting ECG category, maximum achieved HR, exercise angina and exercise-ST depression. Five continuous variables use cubic splines with four source-quantile knots, without spline bias, and linear extrapolation. Categories are dummy-coded. Missing numerical values use source medians with fixed missing indicators; categorical missing values are a separate reference level where applicable. Source-only scaling standardizes the assembled design matrix. L2 penalty `C=1`, lbfgs solver, tolerance1e-9 and maximum3000 iterations are fixed. The augmented model adds only the specified product before scaling. The full design, including redundant binary dummies stabilized by ridge, is specified by code.

Resting ECG0 means normal;1 means ST-T abnormality;2 probable/definite LVH in repository coding [2]. Pooling1/2 in the interaction is prespecified; category-specific baseline intercepts remain. This pooling is imperfect physiology. We omit fluoroscopic vessel count (`ca`) and thallium findings (`thal`) to avoid downstream diagnostic information. ST-slope category, highly missing in Hungary, is added in a declared stronger-comparator sensitivity rather than the primary model. These are standard-measure statistical comparators, not a reconstruction of a validated clinical score or a comparison against modern imaging.

All preprocessing is fit on Cleveland alone or the source training fold/resample. Fivefold stratified source CV uses seed4419. All final models are fit on all eligible source rows, then frozen on the target without recalibration. Primary endpoint is baseline-minus-augmented target Brier score, positive meaning better. Secondary endpoints are log-loss reduction and AUROC increase. The paired500-replicate bootstrap (seed80419) independently resamples source and target rows, refits both models including preprocessing, and uses2.5/97.5 percentiles. This captures training and target-sampling uncertainty conditional on these centers, not hospital-level generalizability. Target-only intervals keep the fitted models fixed and answer a narrower question. One duplicate row slightly weakens a strict independent-row assumption; the collapse sensitivity is disclosed above. No equivalence margin or multiplicity-adjusted success claim is used.

## Interpretation, limitations and proposed future test
This feature yields a modest conditional improvement for one frozen model across two independent recruitment centers, but joint uncertainty, regularization dependence, a better simple comparator and abnormal-ECG subgroup deterioration prevent a claim of a stable new biomarker, improved physiology measurement or clinical utility. It is an informative limited/negative robustness result within the operator's search for underused joint physiological information. It neither establishes equivalence nor refutes known limitations of exercise ECGs.

Uncontrolled medications (digitalis/beta blockers), workload/protocol, comorbidity, referral selection and historical measurement practices can affect both ECG summaries and angiographic disease. Adjustment for observed demographics and clinical features cannot eliminate this confounding. Category pooling moves mostly LVH-derived source information to a mostly ST-T-abnormal target subgroup; this is a concrete domain shift, not evidence of a common mechanism. Public summary features cannot identify the underlying waveform process. Single-row bootstrap understates uncertainty if undocumented repeated individuals exist. Small sample sizes, only two centers, old referral populations, previous exposure to these public cohorts, processed-extract selection and no modern external cohort limit confirmation. No treatment, causal, survival or population-screening claims follow.

A future separately collected, prospectively specified observational validation could freeze the feature and source-selected regularization before enrollment or outcome access; retain interpretable ECG subtypes, medications, exercise protocol and waveform-level measures; and compare against externally validated clinical/imaging pathways with adjudicated outcomes. Prespecified calibration, decision-curve and subgroup-harm analyses would test clinical usefulness and the abnormal-ECG hypothesis directly. This study did not conduct or authorize that future clinical work.
