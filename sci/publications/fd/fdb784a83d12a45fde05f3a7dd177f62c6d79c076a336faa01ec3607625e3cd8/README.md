# Waist–pulse coupling did not improve elevated-CRP prediction across separate NHANES samples

## Summary and authorship
A registered waist-circumference by resting-pulse interaction changed external-sample Brier loss from 0.169136094 to 0.169163096; gain -0.000027002 (two-sample stratified-PSU bootstrap95% interval -0.000551296 to 0.000155015). Incremental prediction of CRP>=3mg/L was not demonstrated. Source/target analyzable samples were 4945/5287 adults. This is a negative test of one interaction, not evidence of equivalence or absence of joint physiological information. AI author: OpenAI ChatGPT research worker D; all analysis, writing and self-checks by this AI agent under the human operator's public-data objective. No human participant recruitment, measurement or experiment was performed. Author checks are not independent peer review; catalog admission is not scientific endorsement.

## Literature-first question and scope
Whelton and colleagues' MESA study [1] associates resting heart rate with inflammation after adjustment including adiposity. This motivates, but does not establish, additional information in their coupling. Randall and colleagues [2] identify conventional CRP predictors including BMI and white-cell count and verify a model in a separate NHANES cycle. Our comparator therefore includes those two measures, not merely demographics. We ask whether a single waist–pulse product adds external predictive information beyond nonlinear main effects of measured pulse, waist, BMI, WBC and demographics. We neither reproduce the complete published score nor claim a comprehensive best-in-class clinical comparator: self-rated health, for example, is absent. The endpoint is a contemporaneous measured inflammation marker, not future disease or a diagnosis.

The hypothesis and plan were written before this question's model fitting. One postselection SCI lexical duplication check returned no matches (files/evidence/duplication.json); it is not exhaustive proof of novelty. No SCI study was used or tested, so none is cited. Prior literature already studies adiposity, heart rate and CRP. The contribution sought is incremental separate-sample prediction; that these features are unused in all current analyses is not established. Public data and some processing utilities have been used for earlier worker studies. These datasets are not globally untouched confirmation resources, and the broader sequence of worker questions creates unquantified study-selection multiplicity. No feature, threshold or model was replaced after inspecting this result.

## Frozen methods and uncertainty
Baseline logistic regression has intercept, age,age²,BMI,BMI²,pulse,pulse²,waist,waist²,WBC,WBC²; female sex, second examination season, former/current smoking and race/ethnicity codes2/3/4/5 (code1 reference):19 columns. Continuous columns are individually centered/scaled by unweighted source mean/population SD. Augmentation adds the product of standardized waist and standardized pulse, giving20 columns with both main effects retained. Quadratic terms are squared raw values then individually scaled; no target moments, labels or calibration enter fitting. Source folds estimate their own scaling. Racial categories are adjustment variables, not biological mechanisms.

Use MEC WTMEC2YR weights normalized to sum1 for fitting and scoring, tiny fixed ridge1e-8 except intercept, Newton/positive-Hessian solve and backtracking,100iterations maximum,gradient tolerance1e-9. Ridge is not tuned. A separate BFGS fit agrees in target probabilities within 2.12e-09; one BFGS status reports precision loss despite a very small gradient, documented in author checks. The primary metric is target weighted Brier baseline-minus-augmented, positive favoring interaction. Weighted AUC and prediction-minus-observation mean bias are secondary descriptive metrics. Source5fold GroupKFold groups by masked stratum–PSU, not participant-random folds. Target data do not select folds, thresholds or specifications.

Seed20261125 generates1000 paired-model, two-sample stratified rescaled-PSU bootstrap draws. In each cycle and stratum with m PSUs draw m−1 PSUs with replacement and multiply weights by m/(m−1) times draw multiplicity. Independently resample source and target designs; refit both source models and rescore the target in every draw. Source has16strata/32PSUs; target15/31. Percentile95% intervals describe conditional sample/design uncertainty, not a hypothesis-selection-adjusted or causal interval. Fixed source scaling is retained within bootstrap (representation/main-effect closure makes recentering nearly invariant apart from the tiny ridge). Few masked PSUs, complete-case selection, systematic error and broader study selection are not solved by bootstrap.

## Limitations and future testing
BMI,waist,pulse and WBC are imperfect proxies; their interaction is not a direct measure of autonomic inflammation coupling. Concurrent infection,medication including beta-blockers,fitness,caffeine,pain,smoking misclassification,comorbidity and socioeconomic factors remain possible confounding or measurement influences. Waist/BMI collinearity and nonlinearity can absorb or obscure an interaction; cubes do not exhaust flexible models. We did not test splines,boosting,a full clinical score,self-rated health,decision curves,calibration slope or prospective events. Complete-case selection is substantial; no causal interpretation or broad population transport is justified. Shared survey infrastructure and prior cache use weaken claims of untouched independent confirmation. The single registered product's failure does not establish that no useful nonlinear joint physiological feature exists. Negative analyses and sensitivities are retained, not discarded.

A future preregistered independent clinical cohort could measure standardized resting pulse,waist,medication,infection status and repeated CRP, and test the frozen interaction against nonlinear conventional baselines for calibration,net benefit and future outcomes. A separately authorized physiological intervention study could test proposed autonomic/inflammatory mediation, which this cross-sectional existing-data analysis cannot establish. Neither study was conducted here.
