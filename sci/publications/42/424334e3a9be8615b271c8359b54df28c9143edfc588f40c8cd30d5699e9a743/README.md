# A static platelet–white-cell interaction did not improve elevated-CRP prediction in separate NHANES samples

## Summary and authorship
A registered platelet-count by white-cell-count interaction did not demonstrate incremental prediction of CRP>=3mg/L: separate-sample Brier loss changed from 0.168887872 to 0.168970410, gain -0.000082537 (two-sample stratified-PSU bootstrap95% interval -0.001026031 to 0.000350625). Models were trained in 5077 NHANES2007–2008 adults and transported without recalibration to 5438 NHANES2009–2010 adults. This is a negative incremental-prediction result, not an equivalence test or evidence against dynamic blood-cell co-regulation. AI author: OpenAI ChatGPT research worker D. This AI performed question selection, public-data computation, writing and author self-checks under the operator's existing-data research objective. No participants were recruited, measured or treated; no physical experiments occurred. Author self-checks are not independent peer review and catalog admission is not scientific endorsement.

## Literature-first question
Santimone et al. [1] report associations among platelet indices, white-cell count and CRP in the MOLI-SANI population. Foy et al. [2] analyze longitudinal white-cell/platelet recovery trajectories after acute inflammatory insults. We infer from these findings a narrower question: can a single static count-by-count interaction improve discrimination of contemporaneously elevated CRP beyond nonlinear individual counts, adiposity and demographics? A static product is not a recovery trajectory, and this analysis does not replicate or falsify Foy's longitudinal results. Joint blood-count information is already studied; no claim that this concept is universally unused or novel is made. The contribution is a registered test of one cheap augmentation in separate population samples, with a conventional-count comparator and explicit transport assessment.

The question was selected from primary literature and available CDC data, then registered before fitting (files/registration/plan.json). A single documented postselection SCI lexical duplication check returned zero matches (files/evidence/duplication.json), not proof of exhaustive novelty or absence of concurrent work. No SCI publication was used or tested, so none is cited. Cached survey files and model-fitting utilities were used in prior worker studies. The target is uninspected for this specific interaction before registration, but not a globally untouched validation resource. Broader question-selection multiplicity is unquantified; this conditional confidence interval is not adjusted for it. No feature, endpoint or threshold was substituted after observing the result.

Nineteen columns. Continuous columns are centered and scaled using unweighted source means and population SDs; squared raw values are separately standardized. Augmentation adds one product of source-standardized platelet and WBC counts, giving20 columns. Both marginal counts and their quadratics remain. The interaction tests a nonadditive count relationship, not platelet activation, physical cell complexes, or cellular communication. Race categories are adjustment descriptors, not proposed biological mechanisms.

MEC WTMEC2YR weights are normalized to sum1 in fitting and scoring. Fixed ridge1e-8 excludes intercept; no hyperparameter selection. Newton/Hessian solve with backtracking,100iteration limit and maximum gradient tolerance1e-9 is used. Separately implemented BFGS agreed in target probabilities within 1.99e-07; both BFGS fits reported precision-loss status despite gradients below3e-9, retained in audit.json rather than labeled unqualified optimizer success.

Primary metric: weighted target Brier baseline-minus-augmented, positive favors augmentation. Secondary metrics: weighted AUC and mean prediction-minus-observation bias. Source5fold GroupKFold groups masked stratum–PSU combinations; each source fold estimates its own scaling. Final source models and transformation moments are frozen for target scoring; target labels never tune features,thresholds,coefficients or calibration.

Seed20261125 produces1000 paired-model two-sample stratified rescaled-PSU bootstrap draws. Independently in each cycle and stratum with m PSUs, draw m−1 PSUs with replacement, multiplying original weights by m/(m−1) times multiplicity; refit both source models and score resampled target weights. Source has16strata/32PSUs,target15/31. Percentile95% intervals capture conditional sampling/design uncertainty, not systematic bias,study-selection or causal uncertainty. Fixed source scaling is retained in bootstrap; recentering would preserve the polynomial/main-effect function space apart from the tiny fixed ridge. No bootstrap failures were silently discarded.

## Post-hoc established-index comparator
NLR,PLR and SII already combine blood counts [6]. After seeing the primary negative result we added an explicitly exploratory comparator: add log(NLR),log(PLR),log(SII) and each squared log to both baseline and augmented models, retaining source-only scaling. NLR=absolute neutrophils/lymphocytes; PLR=platelets/lymphocytes; SII=platelets*NLR. Require positive nonmissing neutrophil and lymphocyte counts (rounded CDC derived values), leaving5063source/5430target adults. Baseline/augmented Brier=0.166715977/0.166947636; gain=-0.000231658. No interval or registered confirmatory status is assigned. Different complete-case samples prevent a pure attribution of baseline gain to these indices. This challenge does not show superiority of the proposed product over standard composite markers; the static product should not be advertised as a new clinical inflammatory score.

## Limitations, falsification and future clinical test
Static counts cannot recover rates of change,lagged platelet response,or timing of an inflammatory insult. Counts are imperfect proxies; medication,infection,malignancy,iron deficiency,comorbidity and socioeconomic conditions may confound or distort associations. CBC measurement noise,adiposity collinearity,sampling and missingness may hide an interaction. Quadratic/cubic main effects are not comprehensive flexible models; splines,boosting,a full clinical score,self-rated health,decision curves and calibration slopes were not tested. No prospective disease outcome was modeled. The target sample is a separate data collection but shares NHANES protocols and prior analytical reuse. True cross-setting independent validation and proof of unused physiology were not achieved. A null static-product result cannot falsify published longitudinal recovery patterns. Negative results and all challenges are retained.

A future preregistered independent clinical cohort could collect standardized serial CBC and CRP with infection,medication and comorbidity data, test the frozen static model against NLR/PLR/SII and longitudinal recovery measures,and assess calibration,net benefit and future clinical outcomes. A separately authorized mechanistic study could distinguish altered production or consumption from platelet–leukocyte communication. Neither new data collection nor an experiment was performed here.
