# A group-conditioned lipid ratio did not improve temporal transfer of fasting-insulin prediction

## Abstract
A prespecified non-Hispanic-Black-indicator by log(triglyceride/HDL) interaction was tested beyond flexible additive clinical measurements. Survey-weighted ridge models trained in NHANES 2011–2012 (n=1762) were applied without target fitting to a different NHANES 2013–2014 sample (n=1852), with CDC insulin-assay harmonization. Target log-insulin MSE was 0.236368 versus 0.236474; improvement was -0.000106, joint-refit rescaled-PSU bootstrap 95% interval [-0.001292, 0.000371]. Source grouped cross-validation also showed no gain. These historically reused survey samples provide independent-participant temporal validation, not lifetime-untouched or cross-study confirmation. This negative result does not refute lipid–insulin heterogeneity or establish equivalence. Author checks and clean reruns are not independent peer review.

## Question and scientific motivation
Can measured lipid information improve prediction of fasting insulin when interpreted jointly with a recorded population-group indicator, beyond standard additive measures? Li and colleagues analyzed differences in TG/HDL interpretation across self-reported race/ethnicity groups in earlier NHANES samples [1]. That primary study motivated the candidate; CDC assay documentation determined the required measurement harmonization [2,3]. We do **not** claim to discover group heterogeneity, to show that all current analyses ignore it, or to replicate every result of that paper. The specific contribution is a reproducible incremental-prediction and temporal-transport test against a comparatively strong additive baseline. An informative failure is retained rather than replaced by a target-selected feature.

RIDRETH3 is a survey-reported social/demographic classification, **not** a measurement of genetic ancestry, and the interaction is not a biological mechanism. Associations may encode social conditions, treatment access, selection, unmeasured diet or activity, or measurement differences. This work supplies no rationale for race-based clinical decisions.

## Population, endpoint and measurement handling
Eligible observations are ages20–79, not recorded pregnant, reporting no or borderline diagnosed diabetes (DIQ010=2 or3), not reporting insulin/oral diabetic medication (DIQ050/070 not1; skipped/missing answers are permitted), reporting9–24 completed fasting hours and a valid smoking-ever response (SMQ020=1 or2). This does not exclude undiagnosed diabetes; a fasting-glucose<126mg/dL analysis challenges it. Positive fasting weights and complete continuous/categorical predictors and endpoint are required. No target imputation or complete-case weighting correction was performed.

Participant flow: source9756 total,1964 eligible,1762 complete; target10175 total,1988 eligible,1852 complete. Included non-Hispanic Black counts are372 and339. Source has14 strata/31PSUs (three strata have3PSUs), target15 strata/30PSUs, with no singleton strata in either primary sample. Complete-case selection may bias transport; fasting weights do not eliminate that bias. `results.json` gives predictor ranges.

Endpoint is natural-log fasting serum insulin, not clamp-measured insulin sensitivity, clinical disease or future diabetes. Predicting insulin conditional on fasting glucose avoids presenting a glucose-containing HOMA index as an independent outcome. Insulin secretion and clearance also affect the endpoint. The source assay was Roche and target Tosoh. Following CDC's published backward Deming conversion [2], target log insulin was set to `0.9765*ln(insulin_Tosoh)+0.07832*ln(10)`. The conversion is external, not fitted on target observations. Uncertainty in those published calibration coefficients is not propagated. Unharmonized results are a measurement sensitivity, not a second primary endpoint. Laboratory detection-limit substitution and reporting follow the released files; no extra correction was fitted. Fasting glucose is LBXGLU, not the nonreference chemistry glucose.

## Frozen predictive comparison and leakage prevention
Both models are survey-weighted ridge regressions (alpha10), fitted only in2011–2012. The baseline uses separate cubic B-splines (3 uniformly spaced knots, linear extrapolation) for age, BMI, waist circumference, fasting glucose, log triglycerides and log HDL; one-hot additive sex, RIDRETH3 and smoking-ever terms. This controls flexibly for component lipids instead of creating apparent ratio value by omitting its constituents. An intercept is unpenalized. Columns are standardized using source weighted means/variances; spline knots and category vocabulary use source data only. The candidate adds exactly one standardized term: `I(RIDRETH3==4)*(ln(TG)-ln(HDL)-source_weighted_mean_log_ratio)`. The source centering is refitted inside source folds/bootstrap replicates. Fitted additive coefficients can change when adding this term, so predictions in other groups can also change.

Fasting subsample weights WTSAF2YR are normalized to mean1 separately for fitting/evaluation. All merges assert one-to-one SEQN joins. Tiny XPORT zero representations below1e-20 in magnitude are mapped to zero. No endpoint/prediction-derived feature enters fitting. Five source folds hold entire masked stratum–PSU groups together, with all preprocessing refitted per fold. Source CV is diagnostic, not used to choose features, regularization or stopping. The target is scored with frozen source fits; sensitivities do not update or select the primary model.

Primary improvement is weighted mean squared log-error (baseline minus augmented; positive favors candidate). MAE and signed prediction bias are secondary. Uncertainty uses2000 target-only conditional and500 joint source-refit/target rescaled-PSU bootstrap replicates. Within each stratum having n_h PSUs, sample n_h−1 with replacement and weight each draw n_h/(n_h−1); source fits and preprocessing are rebuilt for joint replicates. Percentile95% intervals account approximately for clustered sampling, not assay calibration, complete-case bias or historical analysis-selection uncertainty. The first runs incorrectly assumed2PSUs per stratum and failed; the general n_h method replaced it before completed results. Failed logs and the dated amendment are retained. This was a computational correction, not target-based scientific tuning.

## Interpretation, limitations and proposed future test
Literature-motivated group conditioning of lipid interpretation did not improve this stringent incremental temporal prediction task. Nonlinear component lipids, adiposity and group intercepts may already capture its information, or this one interaction may be inadequate. We do not infer that lipids contain no additional physiological information, that group differences are absent, or that insulin resistance is equivalent across groups. The data do not establish mechanisms, causal effects or clinical utility.

Limitations include selection into fasting/complete-case analysis; unmodeled lipid-lowering medication, diet, physical activity and socioeconomic conditions; self-report misclassification; small effective PSU sample sizes; historically reused datasets; a one-direction transport test; and assay conversion uncertainty. Shared measurement infrastructure limits independence. The study has no genuinely untouched external-study confirmation; the claim is restricted to this candidate, outcome and two samples. The negative primary result was preserved without searching additional interactions for a winner.

A future independently conducted study could freeze these exact transforms/models, recruit a separate diverse cohort with prospectively specified measurement protocols and independently measured insulin sensitivity (e.g. clamp), document medication/social exposures, and evaluate overall and subgroup calibration/error with adequate precision and decision utility. Such a study would test whether the candidate transports to a more direct physiological endpoint and whether observed heterogeneity remains after relevant controls. This is a proposal only: no recruitment, measurement, intervention or experiment was performed or authorized here.
